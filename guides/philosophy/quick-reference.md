## 🆘 **I. QUICK ACCESS: Khi nào dùng gì?**

### **😰 Bạn đang stress/overwhelmed?**
→ **[Stoicism](./practical-applications/stoicism-for-modern-life.md)**
- **Tool:** Dichotomy of Control (Phân biệt điều kiểm soát được vs không)
- **Action:** Focus vào effort, không vào outcome
- **Quote:** "You have power over your mind - not outside events." - Marcus Aurelius

---

### **🤔 Phải đưa ra quyết định khó?**
→ **[Decision Making Frameworks](./practical-applications/decision-making-frameworks.md)**
- **Tool:** Second-Order Thinking ("And then what?")
- **Action:** Write down 3 levels of consequences
- **Framework:** Kant's Universal Law Test + Regret Minimization

---

### **⚖️ Gặp vấn đề đạo đức (ethical dilemma)?**
→ **[Ethics in Tech](./practical-applications/ethics-in-tech.md)**
- **Tool:** 3-Test Framework
  1. **Utilitarian Test:** Does it maximize happiness?
  2. **Kant Test:** Would I want this to be universal law?
  3. **Grandma Test:** Can I explain this to grandma without shame?
- **Action:** If fails 2/3 tests → Don't do it

---

### **😞 Cảm thấy cuộc sống vô nghĩa?**
→ **[Meaning & Purpose](./practical-applications/meaning-and-purpose.md)**
- **Tool:** Ikigai framework + Logotherapy
- **Action:** Answer 4 questions:
  1. What do I love?
  2. What am I good at?
  3. What does world need?
  4. What can I be paid for?
- **Quote:** "He who has a Why can bear any How." - Nietzsche

---

### **🎯 Muốn hiểu bản chất vấn đề?**
→ **[Critical Thinking](./fundamentals/critical-thinking-basics.md)**
- **Tool:** Socratic Method (5 Whys) + First Principles
- **Action:** Keep asking "Why?" until you hit fundamental truth
- **Watch out:** 10 common logical fallacies (Ad Hominem, Straw Man, etc.)

---

### **💭 Tranh luận với ai đó?**
→ **[Critical Thinking](./fundamentals/critical-thinking-basics.md)**
- **Tool:** Argument Analysis Framework
  1. Identify premises & conclusion
  2. Check logic validity
  3. Check premises truth
  4. Evaluate evidence
- **Avoid:** Attacking person (Ad Hominem), Misrepresenting argument (Straw Man)

---

### **🚀 Build startup/product mới?**
→ **[Pragmatism](./schools-and-thinkers/contemporary-philosophy.md#-2-pragmatism-truth--what-works)** + **[Nietzsche](./schools-and-thinkers/modern-philosophy.md#-3-friedrich-nietzsche-1844-1900-triết-gia-của-sức-mạnh)**
- **Tool:** MVP approach (Pragmatism) + Will to Power (Nietzsche)
- **Action:** Test idea fast, don't wait for perfection
- **Mindset:** Create your own values (Übermensch), don't follow herd

---

### **😵 Startup/Project thất bại?**
→ **[Absurdism (Camus)](./schools-and-thinkers/contemporary-philosophy.md#b-albert-camus-1913-1960-the-absurd-hero)** + **[Stoicism](./practical-applications/stoicism-for-modern-life.md)**
- **Tool:** Amor Fati (Love your fate) + Negative Visualization
- **Action:** Ask "What can I learn?" not "Why me?"
- **Quote:** "One must imagine Sisyphus happy." - Camus

---

### **🤝 Xung đột với team/boss?**
→ **[Stoicism](./practical-applications/stoicism-for-modern-life.md)** + **[Ethics](./practical-applications/ethics-in-tech.md)**
- **Tool:** Dichotomy of Control + Kant's Humanity Principle
- **Action:** 
  1. Can I control this? (No → Let go)
  2. Does this violate core values? (Yes → Stand firm with respect)
- **Remember:** Control your response, not others' actions

## 📚 **II. ONE-PAGE SUMMARIES**

### **🏛️ Ancient Philosophy**

**Socrates (470-399 BC)**
- **Core:** "Know thyself" + Question everything
- **Method:** Socratic questioning (5 Whys)
- **Use when:** Need to understand root cause

**Plato (428-348 BC)**
- **Core:** Theory of Forms (Perfect ideas exist beyond reality)
- **Use when:** Designing ideal system/product

**Aristotle (384-322 BC)**
- **Core:** Virtue Ethics (Be a good person → Do good things)
- **Golden Mean:** Courage = between cowardice & recklessness
- **Use when:** Character building, habit formation

**Stoicism (Epictetus, Marcus Aurelius)**
- **Core:** Control what you can, accept what you can't
- **4 Virtues:** Wisdom, Courage, Justice, Temperance
- **Use when:** Stress, adversity, uncertainty

**Buddhism**
- **Core:** Suffering exists, Attachment causes suffering, Detachment ends suffering
- **8-Fold Path:** Right view, intention, speech, action, livelihood, effort, mindfulness, concentration
- **Use when:** Dealing with desire, FOMO, anxiety

**Taoism**
- **Core:** Flow with nature (Wu Wei - effortless action)
- **Use when:** Over-planning, forcing things

**Confucianism**
- **Core:** Harmony through hierarchy, rituals, education
- **Use when:** Building team culture, social dynamics

---

### **💡 Modern Philosophy**

**Descartes (1596-1650)**
- **Core:** "I think, therefore I am" + Radical doubt
- **Method:** Question all assumptions until you find certainty
- **Use when:** Debugging, First Principles thinking

**Kant (1724-1804)**
- **Core:** Categorical Imperative - Act only on maxims you'd want universal
- **Formula:** "Would I want EVERYONE to do this?"
- **Use when:** Ethical decisions, rule-setting

**Nietzsche (1844-1900)**
- **Core:** Will to Power + Create your own values (Übermensch)
- **God is dead:** Traditional morality no longer works
- **Use when:** Breaking from herd, creating new paths

**Mill (1806-1873)**
- **Core:** Utilitarianism 2.0 - Quality over quantity of pleasure
- **Higher pleasures:** Intellectual, moral, artistic
- **Use when:** Life choices (meaningful work vs high salary)

---

### **🚀 Contemporary Philosophy**

**Existentialism (Sartre, Camus)**
- **Core:** Existence precedes essence - You create your meaning
- **Bad Faith:** Pretending you have no choice
- **Use when:** Career pivots, finding purpose, taking responsibility

**Absurdism (Camus)**
- **Core:** Life is absurd, embrace it anyway
- **Use when:** Dealing with meaningless tasks, accepting chaos

**Pragmatism (James, Dewey)**
- **Core:** Truth = What works in practice
- **Use when:** Agile development, experimentation

**Analytic Philosophy (Wittgenstein, Popper)**
- **Core:** Clarity through language, falsifiability
- **Use when:** API design, documentation, hypothesis testing

## 🧭 **III. DECISION TREES**

### **A. Ethical Decision Tree**

```
Is this ethical?
│
├─ Utilitarian Test: Does it maximize overall happiness?
│  ├─ Yes → Continue
│  └─ No → 🚫 Stop
│
├─ Kant Test: Would I want this to be universal law?
│  ├─ Yes → Continue
│  └─ No → 🚫 Stop
│
├─ Virtue Test: Would a virtuous person do this?
│  ├─ Yes → ✅ Proceed
│  └─ No → 🚫 Stop
│
└─ If failed 2+ tests → 🚫 Don't do it
```

---

### **B. Decision-Making Tree**

```
Big decision to make?
│
├─ 1. Clarify Values
│   └─ What truly matters to me? (Top 3)
│
├─ 2. List Options
│   └─ Brainstorm all possibilities
│
├─ 3. Second-Order Thinking
│   └─ For each option: "And then what?" (3 levels)
│
├─ 4. Regret Minimization
│   └─ At 80 years old, which choice would I regret NOT making?
│
├─ 5. Pre-Mortem
│   └─ Imagine failure. Why did it fail? How to prevent?
│
└─ 6. Decide & Commit
    └─ Choose, then go all-in (No half-ass)
```

---

### **C. Stress Management Tree**

```
Feeling stressed?
│
├─ Is this in my control?
│  │
│  ├─ YES → Can I do something NOW?
│  │  ├─ Yes → Do it
│  │  └─ No → Schedule it, then let go
│  │
│  └─ NO → Practice Stoic acceptance
│     └─ "This is not mine to control"
│
└─ Negative Visualization
   └─ "What's the worst that could happen?"
      └─ (Usually not that bad)
```

## ⚠️ **V. COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID**

### **1. Misunderstanding Stoicism**
❌ **Wrong:** "Stoicism = No emotions, be a robot"  
✅ **Right:** Control your response to emotions, not suppress them

### **2. Misapplying Nietzsche**
❌ **Wrong:** "Übermensch = Superman, dominate others"  
✅ **Right:** Übermensch = Overcome your own limitations, create your values

### **3. Utilitarian Trap**
❌ **Wrong:** "Ends justify means (Kill 1 to save 5)"  
✅ **Right:** Use as ONE framework among many, not absolute rule

### **5. Stoicism (Khắc kỷ)**

> "Chúng ta khổ đau vì trí tưởng tượng hơn là vì thực tế." — Seneca

*   **Dichotomy of Control:** Chỉ lo cái mình kiểm soát được (Hành động, suy nghĩ). Kệ cái không kiểm soát được (Kết quả, người khác).
*   **Amor Fati:** Yêu lấy số phận, kể cả nghịch cảnh.
*   **Memento Mori:** Nhớ rằng mình sẽ chết -> Sống trọn vẹn hôm nay.
*   **Negative Visualization:** Tưởng tượng điều xấu nhất để chuẩn bị tâm lý và trân trọng hiện tại.

### **6. Existentialism (Hiện sinh)**

> "Sự tồn tại đi trước bản chất." — Sartre

*   **Freedom & Responsibility:** Bạn tự do, nên bạn phải chịu trách nhiệm 100% cho cuộc đời mình. Đừng đổ lỗi hoàn cảnh.
*   **The Absurd:** Cuộc đời vô nghĩa (Camus). Nhiệm vụ của bạn là tự tạo ra ý nghĩa.

### **7. Eastern Wisdom (Phương Đông)**

*   **Wu Wei (Vô vi):** Hành động nương theo dòng chảy tự nhiên, không gượng ép.
*   **Impermanence (Vô thường):** Mọi thứ đều thay đổi. Đừng bám víu vào nỗi đau hay niềm vui.

---

### **8. Political Philosophy (Chính trị)**

*   **Social Contract:** Chúng ta tuân theo luật pháp để đổi lấy sự an toàn (Hobbes, Locke).
*   **Justice as Fairness:** Thiết kế xã hội như thể bạn không biết mình sinh ra là ai (Veil of Ignorance - Rawls).

### **9. Philosophy of AI (AI & Tâm trí)**

*   **Turing Test:** Nếu không phân biệt được người và máy -> Máy thông minh.
*   **Chinese Room:** Máy chỉ mô phỏng (Syntax) chứ không hiểu (Semantics).
*   **Alignment:** Đảm bảo AI phục vụ lợi ích con người.

### **10. Postmodernism (Hậu hiện đại)**

*   **Grand Narratives:** Chúng ta không còn tin vào những câu chuyện lớn (Tôn giáo, Khoa học) nữa.
*   **Deconstruction (Derrida):** Mọi ý nghĩa đều bất định. Phá vỡ các cặp đối lập (Nam/Nữ, Thiện/Ác).
*   **Hyperreality (Baudrillard):** Cái Ảo (Mạng xã hội, Disneyland) còn thật hơn cái Thật.

### **3. Utilitarian Trap**
❌ **Wrong:** "Ends justify means (Kill 1 to save 5)"

### **4. Existential Nihilism**
❌ **Wrong:** "Life is meaningless, so nothing matters"  
✅ **Right:** No inherent meaning → You CREATE meaning (Liberating!)

### **5. Analysis Paralysis**
❌ **Wrong:** Study philosophy forever without action  
✅ **Right:** Learn → Apply → Reflect → Adjust

### **6. Cherry-picking Philosophy**
❌ **Wrong:** Use Kant when convenient, ignore when not  
✅ **Right:** Build coherent system (see Personal Philosophy Template)

### **7. Treating Philosophy as Religion**
❌ **Wrong:** "Stoicism is THE answer to everything"  
✅ **Right:** Different tools for different situations

### **8. Ignoring Context**
❌ **Wrong:** Apply Eastern philosophy literally in Western workplace  
✅ **Right:** Adapt principles to your context (Vietnam, Tech, etc.)

## 🛠️ **VI. QUICK TOOLS & TEMPLATES**

### **A. Daily Philosophy Practice (5 min)**

**Morning (2 min):**
```
1. What challenges might I face today? (Negative Visualization)
2. What is within my control today? (Dichotomy of Control)
3. What virtue will I practice? (Pick 1: Wisdom, Courage, Justice, Temperance)
```

**Evening (3 min):**
```
1. What went well? Why?
2. What went poorly? What was in my control?
3. What did I learn?
```

### **B. Decision Journal Template**

```markdown
Date: [DATE]
Decision: [WHAT]

Options:
1. [Option A]
2. [Option B]
3. [Option C]

Analysis:
- Utilitarian: Which maximizes happiness?
- Kant: Which would I want as universal law?
- Regret: Which would 80-year-old me choose?
- Second-order: "And then what?" for each

Decision: [CHOSEN OPTION]
Reasoning: [WHY]

--- Follow-up (3 months later) ---

Lessons: [WHAT I LEARNED]
```

### **C. Ethical Dilemma Framework**

```markdown
Situation: [DESCRIBE]

Stakeholders affected:
- [Who 1]
- [Who 2]
- [Who 3]

Tests:
1. ☐ Utilitarian: Does it maximize overall good?
2. ☐ Kant: Would I want this as universal law?
3. ☐ Virtue: Would a person of character do this?
4. ☐ Grandma: Can I explain this to grandma without shame?

Red flags:
- ☐ Information asymmetry (I know more than others)
- ☐ Power imbalance (Exploiting vulnerability)
- ☐ Irreversible harm potential

Decision: [YES/NO]
Reasoning: [WHY]
```
