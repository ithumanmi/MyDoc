## Part 1: Introduction & Interview

**Q: Do you enjoy meeting new people?**
*   **Band 8.0:** Generally, yes. I'm quite an extrovert, so I find it stimulating to interact with people from different walks of life. It broadens my horizons and helps me learn about new cultures and perspectives. However, if I'm feeling drained, I might prefer some alone time.

**Q: What kind of people do you like to be friends with?**
*   **Band 8.0:** I gravitate towards people who are genuine, supportive, and have a good sense of humor. Life is too short for drama, so I value friends who are easy-going and positive. Also, having shared interests is a big plus as it gives us something to bond over.

**Q: Do you think you are a good friend?**
*   **Band 8.0:** I'd like to think so. I try to be a reliable and loyal friend. I'm always there to lend a listening ear or offer a helping hand when my friends are going through a rough patch. I also make an effort to stay in touch, even when life gets busy.

## Part 2: Cue Card (Long Turn)

**Describe a person you admire.**

You should say:
*   Who the person is
*   How you know this person
*   What this person is like
*   And explain why you admire him/her.

### 💡 Idea & Structure (PPF Method)

*   **Past (Introduction):** I've known my grandfather since I was born, obviously. He's always been a towering figure in my life, both literally and metaphorically.
*   **Present (Description):** He's in his late 70s now but still sharp as a tack. He has a warm, wrinkled face that tells a thousand stories. Despite his age, he's incredibly active—he gardens every day and reads voraciously. He's the kind of person who commands respect without demanding it.
*   **Future (Conclusion):** I admire him because he built his life from scratch. He overcame poverty and war to raise a successful family. His resilience and integrity are qualities I strive to emulate in my own life. I hope one day I can be half the man he is.

### 🗣️ Sample Answer (Band 8.0+)
"I'm going to talk about **my grandfather**, who is hands down the most **inspiring figure** in my life. He's been my role model for as long as I can remember.

He is a **self-made man** who started with nothing. Born into a poor farming family, he had to work tirelessly to support his siblings. Despite the hardships, he managed to educate himself and eventually built a successful carpentry business.

What strikes me most about him is his **unwavering integrity** and **work ethic**. He never cuts corners and always treats people with fairness and respect. Even now, in his retirement, he remains active in the community, often volunteering to help the less fortunate.

I admire him not just for his achievements, but for his **humility**. He never boasts about his success. He taught me the value of hard work, perseverance, and kindness. Whenever I face a challenge, I think of him and it gives me the strength to push through. He is the **epitome of resilience**."

## Part 3: Discussion

**Q: What qualities make a good leader?**
*   **Band 8.0:** A good leader needs a combination of **vision, empathy, and decisiveness**. They must be able to inspire others towards a common goal while also caring for their team's well-being. Furthermore, **integrity** is non-negotiable; people won't follow someone they don't trust. Effective communication skills are also paramount to ensure everyone is on the same page.

**Q: Do you think famous people have a responsibility to be role models?**
*   **Band 8.0:** To a large extent, yes. Whether they like it or not, celebrities are constantly in the public eye and influence millions of fans, especially impressionable young people. Therefore, they should be mindful of their behavior and use their platform to promote positive values. However, they are also human and prone to mistakes, so we shouldn't put them on an impossible pedestal.

**Q: How do older generations influence younger generations?**
*   **Band 8.0:** They play a vital role in passing down **traditions, values, and wisdom**. Through storytelling and guidance, they help younger people understand their heritage and learn from past mistakes. Conversely, the younger generation can also influence their elders by introducing them to new technologies and progressive ideas, creating a mutual exchange of knowledge.

## 📚 Essential Vocabulary for Describing People

### **Personality Traits - Positive:**
- **Reliable, dependable, trustworthy** - đáng tin cậy
- **Genuine, authentic, down-to-earth** - chân thật, giản dị
- **Empathetic, compassionate, caring** - đồng cảm, quan tâm
- **Outgoing, extroverted, sociable** - hướng ngoại, thích giao tiếp
- **Resilient, perseverant, determined** - kiên cường, quyết tâm
- **Humble, modest** - khiêm tốn
- **Charismatic, inspiring** - có sức hút, truyền cảm hứng
- **Witty, humorous** - dí dỏm, hài hước

### **Personality Traits - Negative:**
- **Arrogant, conceited** - kiêu ngạo, tự phụ
- **Self-centered, selfish** - ích kỷ
- **Stubborn, inflexible** - cứng đầu, cứng nhắc
- **Reserved, introverted** - kín đáo, hướng nội
- **Impulsive, reckless** - bốc đồng, liều lĩnh
- **Pessimistic, cynical** - bi quan, hoài nghi

### **Physical Appearance:**
- **Tall, short, average height** - cao, thấp, trung bình
- **Slim, slender, well-built, stocky** - mảnh khảnh, chắc nịch
- **Wrinkled face, smooth skin** - mặt nhăn nheo, da mịn
- **Sharp features, soft features** - nét mặt sắc sảo, mềm mại
- **Well-groomed, disheveled** - chỉnh tề, luộm thuộm

### **Describing Relationships:**
- **Close-knit family** - gia đình gắn bó
- **Mentor, role model** - người thầy, hình mẫu
- **Acquaintance vs. close friend** - người quen vs. bạn thân
- **Bond over something** - gắn bó qua điều gì
- **Hit it off** - hợp nhau ngay lập tức
- **Drift apart** - xa cách dần
- **Have someone's back** - hỗ trợ ai đó

### **Character & Behavior:**
- **Self-made man/woman** - người tự thân
- **Go out of one's way** - cố gắng hết sức
- **Lead by example** - gương mẫu bằng hành động
- **Cut corners** - làm qua loa
- **Command respect** - được tôn trọng
- **Lend a listening ear** - lắng nghe
- **Go through a rough patch** - trải qua giai đoạn khó khăn

### **Collocations:**
- **Towering figure, inspiring figure** - nhân vật cao lớn, truyền cảm hứng
- **Unwavering integrity** - chính trực vững vàng
- **Work ethic** - đạo đức lao động
- **Shared interests** - sở thích chung
- **Sense of humor** - khiếu hài hước
- **From different walks of life** - từ nhiều tầng lớp
- **Epitome of resilience** - hình mẫu của sự kiên cường

### **Idioms & Expressions:**
- **Sharp as a tack** - rất thông minh (dù già)
- **Heart of gold** - trái tim nhân hậu
- **Through thick and thin** - qua sóng gió

### **Achievement & Success:**

## 🎯 Part 3 Advanced Structures

**Question:** What makes someone a good friend?

**Band 6.0:** "A good friend is nice. They help you when you need help. They are loyal. They don't lie to you. Good friends are important."

**Band 8.0:** "**From my perspective** [Discourse marker], a good friend **embodies** [Formal verb] several key qualities. **First and foremost** [Discourse marker], they should be **trustworthy**—someone you can **confide in** [Phrasal verb] without fear of judgment. **Additionally** [Linking word], they should be **supportive**, **celebrating your successes** [Gerund phrase] and **offering a shoulder to cry on** [Idiom] during tough times. **What sets apart** [Formal phrase] a great friend from a good one is their ability to **be authentic** [Infinitive phrase]—they tell you what you **need** to hear, not just what you **want** to hear. This kind of **honest feedback** [Collocation], though sometimes uncomfortable, is **invaluable for personal growth** [Formal phrase]."

- "Unwavering integrity", "work ethic", "epitome of resilience"

**✅ Strong Collocations:**

- "Hands down", "go out of one's way", "command respect"
- "Through thick and thin", "heart of gold", "put on a pedestal"

**✅ Descriptive Language:**
- Physical + Character description balanced
- Concrete examples (carpentry business, volunteering)
- Personal connection explained

**✅ Complex Structures:**
- Relative clauses: "who is hands down the most inspiring figure"
- Passive voice: "He is admired by everyone"

- Leadership qualities analyzed
- Role model responsibility discussed

> **Pro Tip:** When describing people, use the **PPF structure**: **P**hysical appearance → **P**ersonality traits → **F**eelings/Why you admire them. Balance objective description with personal emotional connection. Show, don't just tell—use specific examples instead of generic statements!
