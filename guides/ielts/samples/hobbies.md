# 🎮 Topic: Hobbies & Leisure

## Part 1: Introduction & Interview

**Q: Do you have a hobby?**
*   **Band 8.0:** Yes, absolutely. I’m quite passionate about photography. I love capturing candid moments and beautiful landscapes. It’s a great way to document memories and express my creativity.

**Q: What hobbies are popular in your country?**
*   **Band 8.0:** In Vietnam, I think football is the most popular sport by a mile. You can see people playing it everywhere, from dusty fields to professional stadiums. Additionally, online gaming has exploded in popularity, especially among the younger generation.

**Q: Is it important to have a hobby?**
*   **Band 8.0:** Definitely. Having a hobby provides a **creative outlet** and a break from the monotony of daily life. It helps reduce stress and prevents burnout. Moreover, it can be a great way to meet like-minded people and expand your social circle.

**Q: What free-time activities would you like to try in the future?**
*   **Band 8.0:** I’ve always wanted to try **scuba diving**. exploring the underwater world and seeing marine life up close sounds absolutely fascinating. I plan to take a certification course during my next beach vacation.

## Part 2: Cue Card (Long Turn)

**Describe a hobby you enjoy.**

You should say:
*   What it is
*   How often you do it
*   Who you do it with
*   And explain why you enjoy it.

### 💡 Idea & Structure (PPF Method)

*   **Past (Introduction):** I’d like to talk about **cooking**, which is something I’ve been passionate about since I was a teenager. My mom taught me the basics, and I fell in love with experimenting in the kitchen.
*   **Present (Description):** I usually cook on weekends when I have more free time. Sometimes, I invite friends over for a dinner party, and we prepare a feast together. I enjoy trying out new recipes from different cuisines, like Italian pasta or Thai curry. The process of chopping vegetables and simmering sauces is incredibly therapeutic for me.
*   **Future (Conclusion):** In the future, I hope to refine my skills further by taking a professional cooking class. Maybe one day, I’ll even start a food blog to share my recipes with others. It brings me immense joy to see people enjoying the food I’ve made.

### 🗣️ Sample Answer (Band 8.0+)
"I’m going to talk about **cooking**, which is my absolute favorite pastime. It started as a necessity when I moved out for university, but it quickly turned into a **full-blown passion**.

I try to cook at least three to four times a week. On weekdays, I stick to simple, quick meals, but on weekends, I love to **go all out** and prepare elaborate dishes. Often, I invite my friends over, and we turn it into a social event. We chop, stir, and taste-test together while catching up on life.

The reason I enjoy cooking so much is that it’s incredibly **creative and rewarding**. Unlike my desk job where results are often intangible, cooking gives me immediate gratification. seeing a pile of raw ingredients transform into a delicious meal is magical. Plus, the aromas wafting through the house are so comforting.

It also allows me to travel the world from my kitchen. One night I can be making sushi, and the next, I’m baking a French baguette. It’s a never-ending journey of discovery. And of course, there’s nothing better than sharing a **hearty meal** with the people you love."

## Part 3: Discussion

**Q: Why do people have hobbies?**
*   **Band 8.0:** I believe hobbies fulfill our need for **self-actualization**. They allow us to pursue our interests purely for the joy of it, without external pressure. They provide a sense of purpose and achievement outside of our professional lives. Additionally, hobbies can be a great stress reliever and a way to disconnect from technology.

**Q: Can hobbies become a career?**
*   **Band 8.0:** Absolutely. Many successful businesses started as hobbies. If you’re passionate and skilled enough, you can turn your pastime into a profitable venture. For example, a hobby photographer can become a professional wedding photographer. However, turning a hobby into a job can sometimes suck the joy out of it, as it becomes an obligation rather than a leisure activity.

**Q: Do you think men and women tend to have different hobbies?**
*   **Band 8.0:** Historically, yes, due to societal expectations. Men were often encouraged towards sports and mechanics, while women were steered towards arts and domestic activities. However, these gender lines are increasingly blurring. Nowadays, you see women excelling in extreme sports and men taking up knitting or cooking. I think it’s wonderful that people feel free to pursue whatever interests them regardless of gender stereotypes.
