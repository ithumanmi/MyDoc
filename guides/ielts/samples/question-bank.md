Kho câu hỏi luyện tập khổng lồ, bao phủ hầu hết các chủ đề từ dễ đến khó.

## 🟢 Part 1: Personal & Familiar Topics

### **1. Basic Personal Info**
*   **Work/Study:** Do you work or are you a student? What do you do? Do you enjoy it? Will you continue doing it in the future?
*   **Hometown:** Where is your hometown? What is it famous for? Is it a good place for young people? How has it changed recently?
*   **Accommodation:** Do you live in a house or a flat? Who do you live with? Which is your favorite room? Do you plan to move soon?

### **2. Habits & Lifestyle**
*   **Daily Routine:** What is your morning routine? Is your routine different on weekends? What would you like to change about your daily routine?
*   **Sleep:** How many hours do you sleep? Do you think sleep is important? Do you take naps?
*   **Weekends:** What do you usually do on weekends? Do you prefer relaxing or going out?

### **3. Preferences**
*   **Music:** What kind of music do you like? Do you play any instruments? Have you ever been to a concert?
*   **Movies:** What was the last movie you saw? Do you prefer watching movies at home or in the cinema?
*   **Colors:** What is your favorite color? Did you like different colors when you were a child? What color would you never use in your room?

### **4. "Weird" Topics (De thuc te gan day)**
*   **Mirrors:** Do you like looking in the mirror? Do you buy mirrors? Do you think mirrors are a necessary decoration?
*   **Robots:** Are you interested in robots? Would you like a robot to help you at home? Do you think robots will replace humans?
*   **Sky:** Do you like looking at the sky? Is there a good place to see stars in your city? Do you prefer the sky in the morning or at night?

## 🟡 Part 2: Cue Cards Categories

### **1. People (Người)**
*   Describe a famous person you admire.
*   Describe a family member you get along well with.
*   Describe a teacher who influenced you.
*   Describe a person you met recently and want to know more about.

### **2. Places (Nơi chốn)**
*   Describe a city you would like to visit.
*   Describe a quiet place you like to go to.
*   Describe a historical building in your country.
*   Describe a cafe you often visit.

### **3. Objects (Đồ vật)**
*   Describe a piece of technology you use often (phone/laptop).
*   Describe a gift you received that you liked.
*   Describe an old object your family has kept for a long time.
*   Describe something you made by hand.

### **4. Experiences/Events (Sự kiện)**
*   Describe a time you were late.
*   Describe a happy memory from your childhood.
*   Describe a time you helped someone.
*   Describe a difficult decision you had to make.

## 🔴 Part 3: Abstract Discussion

### **1. Technology & Society**
*   How has technology changed the way people communicate?
*   Do you think people are losing their social skills because of technology?
*   Should children be allowed to use smartphones at school?

### **2. Environment & Global Issues**
*   What are the biggest environmental problems in your country?
*   Do you think individuals can make a difference in protecting the environment?
*   Should governments punish companies that pollute?

### **3. Education & Work**
*   Is university education necessary for a successful career?
*   What skills will be most important in the future job market?
*   Do you think robots will replace teachers?

### **4. Media & Fame**
*   Why do some people want to be famous?
*   Do celebrities have a responsibility to be good role models?
*   How does the media influence public opinion?
