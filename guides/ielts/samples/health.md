## Part 1: Introduction & Interview

**Q: Do you have a healthy lifestyle?**
*   **Band 8.0:** I’d like to think so. I try to maintain a balanced diet and get enough sleep, which I believe are the cornerstones of good health. However, I’m sometimes guilty of skipping workouts when I’m swamped with work.

**Q: What do you do to stay healthy?**
*   **Band 8.0:** Apart from eating well, I engage in regular physical activity. I hit the gym at least three times a week for strength training and do some yoga on weekends to improve my flexibility. I also make a conscious effort to stay hydrated throughout the day.

**Q: Do you think people in your country eat healthily?**
*   **Band 8.0:** Generally speaking, yes. Traditional Vietnamese cuisine is actually quite healthy, with lots of fresh herbs and vegetables. However, with the influx of fast-food chains, I’ve noticed a worrying trend towards processed foods, especially among the younger generation.

**Q: How often do you go for a medical check-up?**
*   **Band 8.0:** I aim for an annual check-up just to be on the safe side. Prevention is always better than cure, so I think it’s important to monitor my health regularly.

## Part 2: Cue Card (Long Turn)

**Describe a healthy habit you have.**

You should say:
*   What it is
*   When you started it
*   How often you do it
*   And explain why you think it is healthy.

### 💡 Idea & Structure (PPF Method)

*   **Past (Introduction):** A few years ago, I was feeling constantly fatigued and sluggish. My doctor suggested I try **Meditation**. At first, I was skeptical, but I decided to give it a shot.
*   **Present (Description):** Now, it’s a non-negotiable part of my morning routine. Every day, I wake up 20 minutes earlier than usual, sit in a quiet corner of my room, and focus on my breathing. It’s not just about sitting still; it’s about observing my thoughts without judgment.
*   **Future (Conclusion):** Since I started, I’ve noticed a significant improvement in my concentration and stress levels. It feels like a mental detox. I plan to continue this habit indefinitely because it keeps me grounded and calm amidst the chaos of daily life.

### 🗣️ Sample Answer (Band 8.0+)
"I’d like to talk about a habit that has truly transformed my life, which is **daily meditation**. It’s a practice of mindfulness where you focus your attention on the present moment.

I started this journey about two years ago when I was going through a particularly stressful period at work. I felt overwhelmed and anxious all the time. A friend recommended a meditation app called Headspace, and I decided to give it a try.

Every morning, before checking my phone or emails, I dedicate 15 to 20 minutes to meditate. I sit comfortably, close my eyes, and concentrate on my breath. Whenever my mind wanders, which happens often, I gently bring my focus back.

The reason I consider this habit so healthy is that it benefits not just my mental state but my physical well-being too. It has significantly **reduced my anxiety levels** and improved my sleep quality. It’s like a reset button for my brain. Since incorporating this into my routine, I’ve become much calmer and more resilient to stress. It’s truly a **game-changer** for me."

## Part 3: Discussion

**Q: Why do some people find it difficult to stay healthy?**
*   **Band 8.0:** One major obstacle is **lack of time**. With demanding jobs and family responsibilities, many people struggle to fit exercise and meal prep into their schedules. Another factor is the **cost of healthy food**. Fresh produce and organic ingredients are often more expensive than processed alternatives. Additionally, sedentary lifestyles due to desk jobs contribute to health issues.

**Q: What can governments do to improve public health?**
*   **Band 8.0:** Governments can play a crucial role by implementing policies like **sugar taxes** on unhealthy beverages to discourage consumption. They can also invest in **public sports facilities** and parks to make exercise accessible to everyone. Moreover, promoting health education in schools is vital to instill good habits from a young age.

**Q: Do you think stress is a major health problem in modern society?**
*   **Band 8.0:** Absolutely. Stress is often called the "silent killer" because it’s linked to numerous chronic diseases like heart disease and depression. The pressure to succeed in a competitive world, coupled with financial worries and social isolation, has led to a mental health crisis globally. Therefore, managing stress is just as important as physical fitness.
