## Part 1: Introduction & Interview

**Q: Is there any pollution in your hometown?**
*   **Band 8.0:** Sadly, yes. Since it’s a developing industrial city, air pollution is a major concern due to emissions from factories and vehicle exhaust. The river running through the city is also quite polluted because of untreated waste being dumped into it.

**Q: Do you think we should recycle more?**
*   **Band 8.0:** Absolutely. Recycling is crucial to reduce the amount of waste sent to landfills, which conserves natural resources and saves energy. It’s a simple yet effective way for everyone to contribute to a greener planet.

**Q: What do you do to help the environment?**
*   **Band 8.0:** I try to do my bit by reducing plastic usage, like carrying a reusable water bottle and shopping bags. I also make an effort to conserve electricity by turning off lights when not in use and using public transport whenever possible.

## Part 2: Cue Card (Long Turn)

**Describe an environmental problem in your country.**

You should say:
*   What it is
*   Where it is happening
*   What problems it causes
*   And explain what can be done to solve it.

### 💡 Idea & Structure (PPF Method)

*   **Past (Introduction):** Air pollution has become a pressing issue in major cities like Hanoi and Ho Chi Minh City over the past decade. I remember looking at old photos where the sky was blue, but now it's often gray with smog.
*   **Present (Description):** The primary culprits are vehicle emissions, construction dust, and industrial activities. During peak hours, the air quality index (AQI) often reaches unhealthy levels, causing respiratory problems for residents, especially children and the elderly.
*   **Future (Conclusion):** To tackle this, the government needs to invest in green public transport, like electric buses and metro lines, to reduce the number of private vehicles on the road. Stricter regulations on industrial emissions and promoting renewable energy sources are also imperative for a sustainable future.

### 🗣️ Sample Answer (Band 8.0+)
"I’d like to talk about **air pollution**, which is arguably the most alarming environmental issue in Vietnam right now, particularly in big metropolises like Hanoi.

The situation has deteriorated significantly in recent years. On many days, a thick layer of **smog** blankets the city, reducing visibility and making it hazardous to breathe. The main sources are the millions of motorbikes on the roads and the **unchecked emissions** from construction sites and nearby industrial zones.

This pollution has severe consequences for **public health**. There has been a spike in respiratory diseases such as asthma and lung cancer. It also affects the **quality of life**, as people are reluctant to go outside for exercise or leisure activities.

To address this **grave concern**, a multi-faceted approach is needed. The government must accelerate the transition to **green energy** and improve public transportation infrastructure to discourage private vehicle use. Additionally, implementing **stricter penalties** for violators of environmental laws is essential. On an individual level, we should all strive to reduce our carbon footprint by using public transport or cycling more often."

## Part 3: Discussion

**Q: Who should be responsible for protecting the environment?**
*   **Band 8.0:** In my opinion, it should be a collective responsibility involving both the government and individuals. Governments have the power to enforce laws and regulations, while individuals can make small changes in their daily habits. For example, the government can ban single-use plastics, while citizens can choose to use reusable bags and bottles. Some people argue that big corporations are the main culprits and should bear the most cost, which is also a valid point since they produce the most waste.

**Q: What can individuals do to protect the environment?**
*   **Band 8.0:** There are countless small actions that can make a big difference. We can **reduce, reuse, and recycle** waste. Simple habits like conserving water, using energy-efficient appliances, and supporting eco-friendly businesses all contribute to a healthier planet. Furthermore, raising awareness among friends and family is also a powerful tool.

**Q: Do you think international cooperation is necessary to solve environmental problems?**
*   **Band 8.0:** Absolutely. Environmental issues like **climate change** and **ocean pollution** know no borders. Greenhouse gases emitted in one country affect the global climate. Therefore, nations must work together through agreements like the Paris Agreement to set global targets for emission reduction and share sustainable technologies. Without a **concerted global effort**, it will be impossible to avert a climate catastrophe.
