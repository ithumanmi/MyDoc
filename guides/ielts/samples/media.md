## Part 1: Introduction & Interview

**Q: Do you use social media?**
*   **Band 8.0:** Yes, indispensable. I’m an active user of several platforms like Facebook, Instagram, and LinkedIn. It’s the primary way I stay connected with friends and keep up with what’s happening in the world. However, I try to limit my screen time to avoid getting addicted to the endless scrolling.

**Q: How do you usually get your news?**
*   **Band 8.0:** I rarely read physical newspapers anymore. Instead, I rely on online news portals and social media feeds for real-time updates. It’s much more convenient to get bite-sized news on my phone while I’m on the go. That said, I’m always cautious about verifying sources to avoid falling for **fake news**.

**Q: Do you think TV is still relevant today?**
*   **Band 8.0:** To some extent, yes, but its popularity is definitely waning. Traditional cable TV is being rapidly replaced by **streaming services** like Netflix and Disney+. People prefer the flexibility of watching what they want, when they want, rather than being tied to a broadcast schedule.

## Part 2: Cue Card (Long Turn)

**Describe a website or app you use often.**

You should say:
*   What it is
*   How often you use it
*   What you use it for
*   And explain why you like it.

### 💡 Idea & Structure (PPF Method)

*   **Past (Introduction):** I’d like to talk about **YouTube**, which is arguably the most popular video-sharing platform in the world. I’ve been using it for over a decade, ever since I got my first smartphone.
*   **Present (Description):** It’s my go-to app for almost everything. I use it daily, usually in the evenings to unwind. The variety of content is staggering—from educational tutorials and documentaries to music videos and comedy skits. I also subscribe to several channels related to my hobbies, like cooking and tech reviews.
*   **Future (Conclusion):** I think I’ll continue using it for the foreseeable future because it’s such a powerful learning tool. Just the other day, I learned how to fix a leaky faucet just by watching a 5-minute video! It saves me so much time and money.

### 🗣️ Sample Answer (Band 8.0+)
"I’m going to describe **YouTube**, which is a website I visit practically every single day. It’s a massive **video-sharing platform** owned by Google.

I use it for a multitude of purposes. primarily, it’s my main source of **entertainment**. I subscribe to various channels that produce content on travel, technology, and cooking. Whenever I have some downtime, I just open the app and catch up on the latest videos from my favorite **content creators**.

But beyond entertainment, it’s an incredible **educational resource**. Whenever I need to learn a new skill—whether it’s coding, playing the guitar, or even fixing a household appliance—YouTube is the first place I look. The **visual demonstrations** make it much easier to understand than reading a manual.

I love this website because of its **recommendation algorithm**. It’s frighteningly good at predicting what I want to watch next, which keeps me engaged for hours. Plus, the **user interface** is intuitive and seamless across all my devices. It has truly democratized information and entertainment."

## Part 3: Discussion

**Q: What are the negative impacts of social media on society?**
*   **Band 8.0:** There are several downsides. The most alarming one is the spread of **misinformation and fake news**, which can polarize public opinion. Additionally, social media often curates a "highlight reel" of people's lives, leading to **unrealistic comparisons** and feelings of inadequacy among users. Cyberbullying is another pervasive issue that affects mental health, especially among teenagers.

**Q: Should the government regulate the internet?**
*   **Band 8.0:** This is a contentious issue. On one hand, some regulation is necessary to curb illegal activities like cybercrime, hate speech, and child exploitation. However, excessive control can lead to **censorship** and infringe on freedom of speech. I believe a balanced approach is needed, where tech giants are held accountable for the content on their platforms without stifling open dialogue.

**Q: How has the media landscape changed in the last 20 years?**
*   **Band 8.0:** The transformation has been seismic. We’ve moved from a **broadcast model**, where a few TV stations dictated what we watched, to an **on-demand model** where the user is in control. The rise of **citizen journalism** means that anyone with a smartphone can report news, challenging the monopoly of traditional media houses. However, this has also led to information overload and a decline in journalistic standards.
