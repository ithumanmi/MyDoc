## Part 1: Introduction & Interview

**Q: Do you use any gadgets on a daily basis?**
*   **Band 8.0:** Yes, definitely. I’m practically glued to my smartphone. I use it for everything from checking emails and social media to navigation and online banking. It’s become an indispensable part of my life.

**Q: How often do you use the Internet?**
*   **Band 8.0:** Pretty much constantly. Whether it's for work, study, or entertainment, I'm online most of the day. It’s hard to imagine life without it now.

**Q: Do you prefer to use a computer or a phone for work/study?**
*   **Band 8.0:** For serious tasks like writing reports or coding, I definitely prefer a computer because of the larger screen and physical keyboard. However, for quick checks or communication on the go, a phone is much more convenient.

## Part 2: Cue Card (Long Turn)

**Describe a piece of technology you find difficult to use.**

You should say:
*   What it is
*   When you got it
*   What you use it for
*   And explain why you find it difficult to use.

### 💡 Idea & Structure (PPF Method)

*   **Past (Introduction):** I bought a high-end DSLR camera about a year ago because I wanted to get into photography as a hobby. I thought it would be as simple as point-and-shoot, but I was wrong.
*   **Present (Description):** It’s a Canon EOS model with a myriad of buttons and dials. The manual is thicker than a novel! Trying to understand aperture, shutter speed, and ISO settings is like learning a new language. Most of my photos still turn out blurry or overexposed.
*   **Future (Conclusion):** I’m determined to master it, though. I plan to sign up for an online photography course next month. Hopefully, with some professional guidance, I’ll finally be able to take those stunning landscape shots I’ve always dreamed of.

### 🗣️ Sample Answer (Band 8.0+)
"I’m going to talk about my **DSLR camera**, which has been a real headache for me. I purchased it last year with the intention of taking up photography as a serious hobby.

It’s a professional-grade camera, so it comes with a **steep learning curve**. Unlike my smartphone which does everything automatically, this camera requires manual adjustment for every single shot. The interface is cluttered with confusing icons and menus.

The main difficulty lies in balancing the **exposure triangle**—aperture, shutter speed, and ISO. If I change one setting, I have to adjust the others, and frankly, it’s quite **overwhelming**. I often miss the moment while fumbling with the settings.

Despite the frustration, I’m not ready to **throw in the towel** just yet. I realize that mastering any skill takes time and patience. I’ve started watching tutorials on YouTube and practicing on weekends. My goal is to eventually become proficient enough to capture professional-quality portraits of my family."

## Part 3: Discussion

**Q: Do you think people rely too much on technology nowadays?**
*   **Band 8.0:** Yes, I would argue that society has become overly dependent on technology. We use devices for almost everything, from navigation to communication. Without them, many people feel lost or unable to function efficiently. For instance, many young people cannot even do simple mental arithmetic because they always rely on the calculator app on their phones. However, I don't think this is entirely negative. Technology has also made our lives significantly easier and more productive. It’s a double-edged sword.

**Q: How will technology change education in the future?**
*   **Band 8.0:** I believe technology will revolutionize the way we learn, making it more personalized and accessible. AI and VR can create immersive learning experiences that traditional textbooks cannot match. Students can learn at their own pace. We are already seeing the rise of platforms like Coursera or Khan Academy, which allow anyone with an internet connection to access world-class education. That said, I don't think robots will ever replace teachers completely. The human connection and emotional support that a teacher provides are irreplaceable.

**Q: What are the disadvantages of using technology in the classroom?**
*   **Band 8.0:** One major drawback is distraction. Students might be tempted to play games or chat with friends instead of listening to the lecture. Another concern is the digital divide; not all students have equal access to devices and high-speed internet, which can exacerbate educational inequality. Furthermore, excessive screen time can lead to eye strain and a sedentary lifestyle.
