## Part 1: Introduction & Interview

**Q: Where are you from?**
*   **Band 8.0:** I was born and raised in Hanoi, which is the capital city of Vietnam. It’s a bustling metropolis known for its rich history and delicious street food. Although it can be quite chaotic during rush hour, I still love the vibrant atmosphere there.

**Q: Do you like your hometown?**
*   **Band 8.0:** Yes, absolutely. What I love most about it is the sense of community. Everyone is so friendly and helpful. Plus, it’s a culinary paradise; you can find amazing dishes on almost every corner.

**Q: Can you describe the town where you grew up?**
*   **Band 8.0:** I hail from a quaint coastal town located in the southern part of the country. It’s a picturesque place with pristine beaches and breathtaking scenery. Unlike the hustle and bustle of big cities, the pace of life there is incredibly slow and laid-back.

**Q: What places of interest are there in your home town?**
*   **Band 8.0:** Well, the most prominent landmark would definitely be the ancient pagoda situated right in the heart of the town. It’s a major tourist attraction that dates back hundreds of years. Besides that, we also have a sprawling botanical garden which is a perfect getaway for nature lovers.

**Q: How has your town changed in the last 10 years?**
*   **Band 8.0:** It has undergone a dramatic transformation. Ten years ago, it was just a sleepy village, but now it has turned into a bustling urban area. We’ve seen the mushrooming of high-rise apartments and commercial centers. However, this urbanization has also led to traffic congestion, which is a bit of a nuisance.

## Part 2: Cue Card (Long Turn)

**Describe a town or city where you would like to live in the future.**

You should say:
*   Where it is
*   How you know about it
*   What it is famous for
*   And explain why you would like to live there.

### 💡 Idea & Structure (PPF Method)

*   **Past (Introduction):** I’ve always been fascinated by Da Nang, a coastal city in Central Vietnam. I first visited it about 5 years ago during a summer vacation with my family, and I was immediately captivated by its beauty.
*   **Present (Description):** It is renowned as one of the most livable cities in Vietnam. It boasts stunning white sandy beaches, particularly My Khe Beach, and the majestic Marble Mountains. Unlike Hanoi or Ho Chi Minh City, the air quality there is much better, and the pace of life is less frantic. The local cuisine is also mouth-watering, with dishes like Mi Quang being a personal favorite.
*   **Future (Conclusion):** I would love to settle down there because it offers the perfect work-life balance. I can imagine myself jogging along the beach every morning and enjoying fresh seafood on the weekends. It seems like an ideal place to raise a family away from the pollution and stress of the capital.

### 🗣️ Sample Answer (Band 8.0+)
"I’m going to talk about **Da Nang**, which is widely considered the most **livable city** in Vietnam. It’s located in the central region, sandwiched between the mountains and the sea.

I first **set foot in** Da Nang about five years ago on a family trip, and to be honest, it was love at first sight. What struck me most was how clean and modern the city looked compared to where I currently live.

Da Nang is **renowned for** its **pristine beaches**, specifically My Khe beach, which is often voted as one of the most beautiful beaches on the planet. Besides that, it’s famous for the Dragon Bridge, which actually breathes fire on weekends—it’s quite a spectacle! The city strikes a perfect balance between **bustling urban life** and **tranquil nature**.

The main reason I **yearn to** live there is the **quality of life**. The cost of living is reasonable, the traffic is manageable, and the locals are incredibly **hospitable**. I’m really into outdoor activities, so the idea of being able to go for a swim or a hike after work is very appealing to me. It feels like a place where I could pursue my career while still enjoying a **laid-back lifestyle**, which is something I find lacking in big metropolises."

## Part 3: Discussion

**Q: Why do many young people prefer to live in cities?**
*   **Band 8.0:** Primarily because of the **abundance of opportunities**. Cities are **economic hubs** where multinational companies are located, offering better career prospects and higher salaries. Additionally, cities offer **top-notch amenities** like hospitals, universities, and entertainment centers, which makes life much more convenient and exciting for young adults.

**Q: What are the main problems associated with living in big cities?**
*   **Band 8.0:** I think the most pressing issue is **traffic congestion**. Getting stuck in gridlock during rush hour is a daily nightmare for many commuters. Another significant problem is the **exorbitant cost of living**, especially housing prices, which makes it difficult for people to save money. And we can’t ignore **pollution**, both air and noise, which takes a toll on people’s health.

**Q: How can governments improve living conditions in cities?**
*   **Band 8.0:** There are several measures they could take. First, they should invest heavily in **public transport systems** like metros or buses to reduce reliance on private vehicles, which would alleviate traffic jams. Second, creating more **green spaces** like parks and vertical gardens would help filter the air and provide residents with places to relax. Finally, implementing stricter regulations on emissions from factories and vehicles is crucial to combat pollution.
