## Part 1: Introduction & Interview

**Q: Do you like traveling?**
*   **Band 8.0:** Absolutely. I’m bitten by the travel bug! Exploring new places and immersing myself in different cultures is my passion. It’s the perfect way to unwind and escape the daily grind.

**Q: How often do you travel?**
*   **Band 8.0:** Not as often as I’d like, unfortunately. I usually try to squeeze in one or two trips a year, typically during the summer holidays or public holidays. However, I do take short weekend getaways to nearby provinces whenever I can.

**Q: Do you prefer traveling alone or with others?**
*   **Band 8.0:** It really depends on my mood. Traveling solo gives me total freedom and flexibility to do whatever I want. On the other hand, traveling with friends or family is great for bonding and creating shared memories. So, I’d say I enjoy a mix of both.

**Q: Which country would you like to visit in the future?**
*   **Band 8.0:** Japan is definitely at the top of my bucket list. I’ve always been fascinated by its unique blend of ancient tradition and futuristic technology. plus, I’m a huge fan of Japanese cuisine, so I’d love to go there and try authentic sushi and ramen.

## Part 2: Cue Card (Long Turn)

**Describe a memorable holiday you had.**

You should say:
*   When and where you went
*   Who you went with
*   What you did there
*   And explain why it was memorable.

### 💡 Idea & Structure (PPF Method)

*   **Past (Introduction):** Two years ago, right after graduating from university, I decided to treat myself to a trip to Ha Long Bay, a UNESCO World Heritage site in Northern Vietnam. I went with a group of my closest friends.
*   **Present (Description):** We booked a 2-day-1-night cruise, which was an incredible experience. We spent the day kayaking through limestone karsts, visiting spectacular caves, and swimming in the emerald waters. In the evening, we had a BBQ party on the deck under the stars. The scenery was simply breathtaking, like something out of a movie.
*   **Future (Conclusion):** It remains etched in my memory because it was one of the last times we were all together before everyone went their separate ways for work. The laughter, the camaraderie, and the stunning landscape made it a trip of a lifetime. I hope we can organize a reunion trip there someday.

### 🗣️ Sample Answer (Band 8.0+)
"I’d like to talk about a **memorable trip** to Ha Long Bay that I took a couple of years ago. It was a graduation trip with my best friends, so it held a lot of **sentimental value**.

We decided to go there because Ha Long Bay is world-renowned for its **breathtaking scenery**. We opted for an overnight cruise to fully experience the beauty of the bay.

The itinerary was packed with exciting activities. We went **kayaking** through the limestone islets, which was a bit tiring but totally worth it for the views. We also visited a massive limestone cave called 'Sung Sot Cave', which was truly **awe-inspiring**.

The highlight of the trip, however, was the evening. We had a **sumptuous seafood feast** on the boat’s deck while watching the sunset over the bay. It was a magical moment.

This holiday is unforgettable to me not just because of the **picturesque landscapes**, but because of the company. We laughed, shared stories, and enjoyed each other’s presence without any distractions. It was the perfect way to mark the end of our student life and the beginning of a new chapter."

## Part 3: Discussion

**Q: What are the benefits of traveling?**
*   **Band 8.0:** Traveling offers a plethora of benefits. Firstly, it **broadens your horizons** by exposing you to new cultures, languages, and perspectives. It fosters tolerance and understanding. Secondly, it’s a great stress reliever; stepping away from your routine allows you to recharge. Finally, it builds confidence and independence, especially if you travel solo and have to navigate foreign environments.

**Q: Do you think tourism can have negative impacts on a country?**
*   **Band 8.0:** Yes, unfortunately, mass tourism can be a double-edged sword. On the downside, it can lead to **environmental degradation**, such as littering and pollution. It can also cause overcrowding, which puts a strain on local infrastructure and resources. Moreover, there’s the risk of **cultural erosion** where local traditions are commercialized to cater to tourists.

**Q: How has traveling changed compared to the past?**
*   **Band 8.0:** It has become significantly more **accessible and affordable**. With the advent of budget airlines and booking platforms like Airbnb, more people can travel than ever before. Technology has also made navigation and communication much easier; we can now use Google Maps or translation apps instantly. However, the charm of discovery might be somewhat diminished since everything is so documented on social media now.
