# ⚠️ Common IELTS Mistakes (Vietnamese Learners)

> [← Back to IELTS Guide](./README.md)

Danh sách 50+ lỗi phổ biến nhất mà người Việt thường mắc phải khi thi IELTS. Đọc kỹ và tránh!

---

## 📝 1. Grammar Mistakes (Ngữ pháp)

### **1.1. Adverb Position (Vị trí trạng từ)**
❌ "I very like coffee."
✅ "I **really** like coffee." hoặc "I like coffee **very much**."

**Rule:** "Very" chỉ đứng trước tính từ/trạng từ, KHÔNG đứng trước động từ.

---

### **1.2. Comparative & Superlative**
❌ "More better", "More easier"
✅ "Much **better**", "**Easier**"

❌ "The most tallest building"
✅ "The **tallest** building"

**Rule:** 
- Short adjectives: tall → taller → tallest
- Long adjectives: beautiful → more beautiful → most beautiful
- NEVER mix both!

---

### **1.3. Countable vs Uncountable Nouns**
❌ "Informations", "Advices", "Homeworks"
✅ "Information", "Advice", "Homework" (Luôn ở dạng số ít)

❌ "I need an advice."
✅ "I need **some** advice." hoặc "I need **a piece of** advice."

**Common uncountable nouns:**
- Information, Advice, Furniture, Equipment, Luggage, Research, Progress

---

### **1.4. Articles (a/an/the)**
❌ "I play the guitar everyday in the morning."
✅ "I play the guitar every day in the morning." (Không có "the" trước "morning" khi nói chung chung)

❌ "Internet has changed our lives."
✅ "**The** Internet has changed our lives."

**Rule:**
- "The" + specific noun hoặc unique thing (the sun, the Internet, the government)
- NO "the" + general plural (Dogs are loyal. / People love traveling.)

---

### **1.5. Prepositions (Giới từ)**
❌ "Depend **of**", "Different **with**", "Interested **on**"
✅ "Depend **on**", "Different **from**", "Interested **in**"

**Common collocations:**
- Worried **about**
- Good **at**
- Famous **for**
- Responsible **for**
- Addicted **to**

---

### **1.6. Subject-Verb Agreement**
❌ "The number of students **are** increasing."
✅ "The number of students **is** increasing."

❌ "A lot of people **is** unhappy."
✅ "A lot of people **are** unhappy."

**Rule:**
- "The number of" → Singular verb
- "A number of" / "A lot of" → Plural verb

---

### **1.7. Passive Voice Confusion**
❌ "The technology is developed by scientists." (Khi nói chung chung về quá trình)
✅ "The technology **was developed** by scientists." (Quá khứ - đã phát triển xong)
hoặc "The technology **is being developed** by scientists." (Hiện tại - đang phát triển)

## 🗣️ 2. Speaking Mistakes

### **2.1. "Yes/No" Answers (Trả lời ngắn cụt)**
❌ Examiner: "Do you like reading?"
You: "Yes."

✅ "Yes, **definitely**. I'm actually a bit of a bookworm. Reading helps me relax after a long day."

**Always expand:** Answer + Reason + Example

---

### **2.2. Memorized Answers (Thuộc lòng câu trả lời)**
❌ Nói như robot, không tự nhiên, giọng monotone.
✅ Tự nhiên, có ngắt nghỉ, intonation đi lên xuống.

**Tip:** Learn IDEAS, not full sentences. Practice paraphrasing.

---

### **2.3. Using "People" Too Much**
❌ "People think that... people do... people say..."
✅ Alternatives:
- **Individuals** tend to...
- **The general public** believes that...
- **Many** argue that...
- **Some** claim that...

---

### **2.4. Filler Words Overuse**
❌ "Like... you know... actually... I mean... like..."
✅ Use strategically:
- "That's an interesting question..."
- "Let me think about that for a moment..."
- "What I mean is..."

**Avoid:** "Uh", "Um", "Er" (Sounds unprepared)

---

### **2.5. Vietnamese Direct Translation**
❌ "I have a lot of **free times**." (Dịch từ "Thời gian rảnh")
✅ "I have a lot of **free time**." (Uncountable)

❌ "I study in university." (Dịch từ "Học ở đại học")
✅ "I study **at** university."

---

## ✍️ 3. Writing Mistakes

### **3.1. Informal Language in Writing**
❌ "A lot of", "Lots of", "Big", "Get"
✅ "A significant number of", "Numerous", "Considerable", "Obtain/Receive"

❌ "Kids should study hard."
✅ "**Children** should study diligently."

---

### **3.2. Starting Sentences with "And/But/So"**
❌ "And this is why... But some people... So we should..."
✅ 
- "**Furthermore**, this is why..."
- "**However**, some people..."
- "**Therefore**, we should..."

---

### **3.3. Repetition of Words**
❌ "The government should do something. The government must take action. The government..."
✅ Use synonyms:
- The government → **Authorities** → **Policymakers** → **They**

---

### **3.4. No Clear Thesis Statement**
❌ Introduction: "This essay will discuss both views."
✅ "While [View A] has some merit, I believe [View B] is more compelling because..."

**Make your opinion CLEAR from the Introduction.**

---

### **3.5. Examples Too General**
❌ "For example, in many countries..."
✅ "For instance, **in Vietnam**, the government recently implemented..."

**Specific > General**

---

### **3.6. Task 1: Describing Every Single Number**
❌ "In 2010, the figure was 10%. In 2011, it was 12%. In 2012, it was 15%..."
✅ "The figure **rose steadily** from 10% in 2010 to 15% in 2012."

**Focus on TRENDS, not individual data points.**

---

## 🎧 4. Listening Mistakes

### **4.1. Not Reading Questions First**
❌ Nghe xong mới đọc câu hỏi → Không kịp.
✅ Đọc câu hỏi **TRƯỚC** khi audio bắt đầu (30s preparation time).

---

### **4.2. Writing While Listening**
❌ Cố ghi từng từ → Bỏ lỡ câu sau.
✅ Nghe toàn bộ → Ghi keywords → Viết lại sau.

---

### **4.3. Spelling Mistakes**
Common errors:
- "Accomodation" → ✅ "Accomm**o**dation" (2 c, 2 m)
- "Enviroment" → ✅ "Envir**on**ment"
- "Goverment" → ✅ "Gov**ern**ment"

---

### **4.4. Plural/Singular Confusion**
Audio: "There are **two libraries**."
❌ You write: "library"
✅ You write: "libraries"

**Listen for "s" sound!**

---

## 📖 5. Reading Mistakes

### **5.1. Not Understanding "NOT GIVEN" vs "FALSE"**
- **FALSE:** The text says the **opposite**.
- **NOT GIVEN:** The text **doesn't mention** it at all.

❌ Guessing when not sure.
✅ Look for **paraphrasing** in the text.

---

### **5.2. Spending Too Long on One Passage**
❌ 30 mins on Passage 1 → Không đủ thời gian cho Passage 3.
✅ 15-20-20 mins (Passage 3 khó nhất, cần nhiều thời gian nhất).

---

### **5.3. Not Checking Word Limit**
Question: "Answer in NO MORE THAN TWO WORDS."
❌ You write: "A very beautiful city" (4 words)
✅ You write: "Beautiful city" (2 words)

**Follow instructions strictly!**

---

## 🔊 6. Pronunciation Mistakes

### **6.1. /th/ Sound**
❌ "**Tank** you" (Thank you)
❌ "I **sink** so" (I think so)
✅ Put tongue between teeth: "**Th**ank you"

---

### **6.2. /l/ vs /n/**
❌ "**Night**" sounds like "Light"
❌ "**Collect**" sounds like "Correct"

**Practice:** "Long night", "Correct collection"

---

### **6.3. Silent Letters**
- **Climb** (b is silent) → "clime"
- **Knight** (k is silent) → "nite"
- **Psychology** (p is silent) → "sai-ko-lo-ji"

---

### **6.4. Word Stress**
❌ "**pho**-TO-gra-phy" 
✅ "pho-**TO**-gra-phy"

❌ "**de**-ve-lop-MENT"
✅ "de-**VE**-lop-ment"

**Tip:** Use dictionary to check stress marks.

---

## 💬 7. Vocabulary Mistakes

### **7.1. Confusing Similar Words**
| Wrong | Correct | Meaning Difference |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **Economic** problem | **Financial** problem | Economic = Về kinh tế học, Financial = Về tài chính cá nhân |
| **Funny** game | **Fun** game | Funny = Hài hước, Fun = Vui vẻ |
| **Affect** (noun) | **Effect** (noun) | Affect = Động từ (ảnh hưởng), Effect = Danh từ (tác động) |
| I'm **boring** | I'm **bored** | Boring = Chán (mô tả người/vật gây chán), Bored = Cảm thấy chán |

---

### **7.2. Literal Translation from Vietnamese**
❌ "Open the TV" (Mở tivi)
✅ "**Turn on** the TV"

❌ "Close the light" (Tắt đèn)
✅ "**Turn off** the light"

❌ "My brother is a **doctor person**." (Anh tôi là bác sĩ)
✅ "My brother is a **doctor**." (Không cần "person")

---

## 🎯 How to Fix These Mistakes?

1. **Keep a Mistake Log:** Write down every mistake you make and review weekly.
2. **Record Yourself Speaking:** Listen back and identify pronunciation/grammar errors.
3. **Get Feedback:** Have a teacher or ChatGPT check your Writing.
4. **Immerse Daily:** Watch English content, think in English.
5. **Practice with Timer:** Simulate real test conditions.

---

> **Remember:** Everyone makes mistakes. The key is to **learn from them** and **not repeat them**.
