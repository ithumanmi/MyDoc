## 💎 Vocabulary & Grammar

### **[Vocab Upgrade (Band 6.0 → 8.0)](./vocab-upgrade.md)**
- **Swap Table:** Replace common words with academic alternatives
- **Collocations:** Learn word combinations, not isolated words
- **Topic Vocabulary:** Education, Environment, Technology, Work
- **Idioms for Speaking:** Use sparingly in Part 1 & 2 only!

**Example:**
- ❌ "Important" → ✅ "Crucial, Vital, Indispensable"
- ❌ "Bad" → ✅ "Detrimental, Adverse"
- ❌ "Good" → ✅ "Beneficial, Advantageous"

## 📅 Study Planning

### **[Daily Routine](./daily-routine.md)**
Sample schedule for working professionals and students:
- **Morning (30 mins):** Listening (Passive - Podcasts while commuting)
- **Lunch (30 mins):** Vocabulary + Grammar review (Anki/Quizlet)
- **Evening (1.5-2 hours):** Writing/Speaking practice + Correction
- **Before Bed (15 mins):** Review mistakes, light listening

**Golden Rules:**
1. Never skip more than 2 days - Consistency beats intensity
2. Quality over Quantity - 1 test deeply analyzed > 10 tests rushed
3. Active Learning - Don't just consume, produce (write/speak)

## 🎯 Understanding Band Scores

👉 **[Complete Guide: Band Descriptors Explained](./band-descriptors.md)** (⭐ **New**)

**Quick Overview:**

### What does 7.5 mean?
- **CEFR Level:** C1 (Advanced)
- **Fluency:** Can express ideas clearly with only occasional errors
- **Vocabulary:** Uses less common vocabulary with some flexibility
- **Grammar:** Uses complex structures with good control
- **Task Response:** Addresses all parts of the task with developed ideas

### 4 Criteria (Writing & Speaking):
1. **TR/FC (Task Response/Fluency & Coherence):** Answer the question fully, organized ideas
2. **CC (Coherence & Cohesion):** Logical flow, proper linking words
3. **LR (Lexical Resource):** Vocabulary range and accuracy
4. **GRA (Grammatical Range & Accuracy):** Variety of structures, minimal errors

## ⚠️ Common Mistakes (Vietnamese Learners)

👉 **[Complete Guide: 50+ Common Mistakes](./common-mistakes.md)** (⭐ **New**)

**Quick Preview:**

### Grammar:
- ❌ "I very like" → ✅ "I really like"
- ❌ "More better" → ✅ "Much better"
- ❌ "Informations" → ✅ "Information" (uncountable)

### Pronunciation:
- /th/ sound: "Think" not "Sink", "That" not "Dat"
- /l/ vs /n/: "Night" vs "Light"
- Silent letters: "Climb" (b is silent), "Know" (k is silent)

### Word Choice:
- ❌ "Economic problem" → ✅ "Financial problem" (Economic = về kinh tế học)
- ❌ "Funny game" → ✅ "Fun game" (Funny = hài hước)

## 💡 Success Tips

1. **Don't memorize answers** - Examiners can detect this (automatic Band 5)
2. **Record yourself speaking** - Listen back and identify weak areas
3. **Write with a timer** - Task 1 (20 mins), Task 2 (40 mins) - strict!
4. **Learn from mistakes** - Keep a "Mistake Log" notebook
5. **Immerse daily** - Think in English, talk to yourself in English
