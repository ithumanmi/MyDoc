# ⚙️ Task 1: Process - Coffee Production

> [← Back to Writing Samples Index](./README.md)

## 📌 Question:

**The diagram below shows the process of producing coffee from coffee beans.**

**Summarize the information by selecting and reporting the main features.**

**Write at least 150 words.**

---

## ⚙️ The Diagram (Description):

```
COFFEE PRODUCTION PROCESS

Step 1: HARVESTING
[Coffee Plants] → Hand-pick ripe red cherries → [Basket]

Step 2: PROCESSING
[Basket] → Spread on drying mats → [Sun-dried beans]
         → Remove outer shells → [Green coffee beans]

Step 3: ROASTING
[Green beans] → Roasted at 200-250°C → [Brown roasted beans]

Step 4: GRINDING
[Roasted beans] → Grinding machine → [Ground coffee]

Step 5: BREWING
[Ground coffee] + Hot water → Filter/Press → [Coffee drink]

Step 6: PACKAGING (Optional)
[Ground coffee/beans] → Vacuum sealed → [Retail packages]
```

---

## ❌ Band 6.0 Sample (Common Problems)

The diagram shows how to make coffee. **[Weak paraphrase]**

First, people pick coffee cherries from plants. **[Active voice - should use passive]** They are red. **[Unnecessary detail]** Then the cherries are dried in the sun. **[Too simple]** After that, they remove the shells. **[Who is "they"?]** Now they have green beans. **[Now - informal time marker]**

Next, the beans are roasted. **[Too simple - missing temperature]** They become brown. **[Stating obvious]** Then they grind the beans to make coffee powder. **[Active voice again]** Finally, people add hot water to make coffee. **[Too simple - no detail about brewing]**

Coffee can be sold in shops. **[Irrelevant - not in diagram]** The process has many steps. **[Stating obvious]** It takes time to make good coffee. **[Opinion - not allowed]**

**(115 words - too short!)**

## 🏗️ Process Task Structure Template

### **Introduction (1 sentence):**
Paraphrase the question completely.
- "The diagram illustrates the [number]-stage process of [verb]ing [product]..."
- "The diagram demonstrates how [product] is [verb]ed/produced..."

### **Overview (2 sentences - IMPORTANT!):**
Summarize the process type and outcome. NO specific steps!
- "Overall, the process comprises [number] stages, commencing with [first general step] and culminating in [final outcome]."
- "Notably, [important feature, e.g., heat treatment/chemical process] plays a crucial role..."

**Example:**
- "Overall, the process comprises manual and mechanical stages, commencing with harvesting and culminating in brewing or packaging."
- "Notably, heat treatment plays a crucial role in developing the characteristic flavor."

### **Body Paragraph 1 (4-5 sentences):**
Describe FIRST HALF of stages with specific details.
- "Initially, [material] is/are [passive verb]..."
- "Subsequently, [next step]..."
- "Once [completed], [result]..."
- Use passive voice throughout!

### **Body Paragraph 2 (4-5 sentences):**
Describe SECOND HALF of stages with specific details.
- "At the subsequent stage, [step]..."
- "For [purpose], [method] is used..."
- "Alternatively/Finally, [final step]..."

## ⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

### **1. Using Active Voice:**
- ❌ "Workers pick the cherries."
- ✅ "The cherries are hand-picked from plants."

### **2. Missing Overview:**
- ❌ Going straight to "Initially, cherries are picked..."
- ✅ "Overall, the process comprises six stages, commencing with harvesting..."

### **3. Basic Sequencing Only:**
- ❌ "First, Then, Next, After that, Finally"
- ✅ "Initially, Subsequently, Following this, At the subsequent stage, Ultimately"

### **4. Too Vague:**
- ❌ "The beans are heated."
- ✅ "The beans undergo roasting at temperatures ranging from 200 to 250°C."

### **5. Missing Technical Details:**
- ❌ "They are dried."
- ✅ "The cherries are spread on drying mats and left to sun-dry."

### **6. Adding Opinions:**
- ❌ "This is an important step because..."
- ✅ Only describe what you see - no explanations of why

### **7. Using "People/They/Workers":**
- ❌ "People roast the beans."
- ✅ "The beans are roasted..." or "The beans undergo roasting..."

### **8. Present Continuous Instead of Present Simple:**
- ❌ "The beans are being roasted..."
- ✅ "The beans are roasted..." or "undergo roasting"

## 🎯 Process-Specific Grammar

### **1. Passive Voice (95% of sentences!):**
- ✅ "Coffee cherries **are hand-picked**..."
- ✅ "The beans **are roasted** at 200-250°C..."
- ✅ "Hot water **is passed through** the grounds..."

### **2. "Undergo" + Noun (Alternative to Passive):**
- ✅ "The beans **undergo roasting**..." (instead of "are roasted")
- ✅ "The material **undergoes processing**..." (instead of "is processed")

### **3. Participle Clauses (Purpose/Result):**
- ✅ "...left to sun-dry, **removing excess moisture**" (purpose)
- ✅ "...shells are stripped away, **revealing green beans**" (result)
- ✅ "...vacuum-sealed in packages, **preserving freshness**" (purpose)

### **4. Relative Clauses (Adding Detail):**
- ✅ "...temperatures **ranging from** 200 to 250°C..."
- ✅ "...a process **which transforms** them into brown beans..."
- ✅ "...a machine **where they are pulverized**..."
