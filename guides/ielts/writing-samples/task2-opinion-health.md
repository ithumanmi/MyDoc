# 📝 Opinion Essay: Government Role in Public Health

> [← Back to Writing Samples Index](./README.md)

## 📌 Question:

**"Some people think that governments should be responsible for improving public health, while others believe it is the responsibility of individuals. Discuss both views and give your own opinion."**

**Essay Type:** Opinion (Discuss Both Views + Opinion)
**Recommended Time:** 40 minutes
**Word Count Target:** 270-290 words

---

## ❌ Band 6.0 Sample (Common Problems)

Health is very important for everyone. **[Weak opening - states obvious]** Some people think government should help people be healthy, but others think people should do it themselves. **[Oversimplified paraphrase]** I will discuss both sides. **[Mechanical announcement]**

First, government can do many things for health. **[Weak topic sentence]** They can build hospitals. **[Too simple]** Also, they can make healthy food cheaper. **[Undeveloped]** This helps poor people. **[Vague]** Government has money to do big things. **[Informal language]**

However, individuals are also responsible for their health. **[Basic topic sentence]** People should eat healthy food and exercise. **[Too simple]** They should not smoke. **[List without development]** Everyone can choose what they eat. **[Stating obvious]** My friend exercises every day and is very healthy. **[Weak personal example]**

In my opinion, both are important. **[Fence-sitting]** Government and people should work together. **[Too vague]** If they do this, everyone will be more healthy. **[Grammar + overly optimistic]**

**(138 words - too short!)**

## 🏗️ Opinion Essay Structure (Both Views + Opinion)

### **Introduction (2 sentences):**
Acknowledge debate, state YOUR position clearly.

**Template:**
"The question of whether [issue] has sparked debate. While both [X and Y] play roles, I maintain that [your nuanced position - e.g., X is primary but Y is essential]."

### **Body Paragraph 1 (First View - PEER):**
Discuss objectively with evidence.
- **P:** "[X] possess unique capacity to..."
- **E:** Explain how/why this works
- **E:** Specific example with data
- **R:** Result/significance

### **Body Paragraph 2 (Second View + Your Opinion - PEER):**
Start with "Nevertheless" to show your stronger view.
- **P:** "Nevertheless, [Y] fundamentally determines..."
- **E:** Explain your reasoning
- **E:** Specific example with data
- **R:** Connect to your opinion (Ultimately, ...)

### **Conclusion:**
Restate your nuanced position.

**Template:**
"In conclusion, while [X] must [do their part], [Y] bears the fundamental obligation to [main role]. Optimal results emerge from this complementary relationship."

## ⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

### **1. Fence-Sitting:**
- ❌ "Both are equally important."
- ✅ "While both play roles, governments bear primary responsibility for infrastructure, while individuals must make daily choices."

### **2. Personal Anecdotes Only:**
- ❌ "My friend exercises daily and is healthy."
- ✅ "WHO reports that 80% of premature heart disease is preventable through lifestyle modifications."

### **3. Vague Statements:**
- ❌ "Government can do many things."
- ✅ "Governments can regulate harmful substances, mandate nutritional labeling, and subsidize preventive healthcare services."

### **4. No Specific Examples:**
- ❌ "Some countries have good health programs."
- ✅ "Finland's North Karelia Project resulted in an 85% reduction in cardiovascular mortality over three decades."

### **5. Weak Vocabulary:**
- ❌ "Government should help people be healthy."
- ✅ "Governments must establish enabling infrastructures conducive to healthy living."
