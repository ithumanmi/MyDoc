# 📊 Task 1: Line Graph - Internet Usage

> [← Back to Writing Samples Index](./README.md)

## 📌 Question:

**The line graph below shows the percentage of households with internet access in three different countries from 2000 to 2020.**

**Summarize the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.**

**Write at least 150 words.**

---

South Korea had the highest internet access. **[Vague - no data]** It was 40% in 2000. All countries increased. **[Too obvious - no insight]** South Korea increased the most. **[Inaccurate - Vietnam had highest growth rate]** USA increased less than South Korea. **[Vague comparison]** Vietnam started very low but increased a lot. **[A lot - informal]**

## ✅ Band 8.0-9.0 Sample (With Annotations)

**[Introduction - Paraphrase - 1 sentence, 25 words]**

The line graph **illustrates** [Paraphrase: "shows" → "illustrates"] the **proportion of** [Academic phrase - "percentage of"] homes with internet **connectivity** [Paraphrase: "access" → "connectivity"] in South Korea, the USA, and Vietnam **over a 20-year period** [Paraphrase: "from 2000 to 2020" → "over a 20-year period"] **spanning from** [Formal phrase] 2000 to 2020.

---

**[Overview - 2 sentences, 35 words - MOST IMPORTANT PARAGRAPH]**

**Overall** [Overview marker], all three countries **experienced upward trends** [Academic phrase - "increased"] in internet penetration **throughout the period** [Time phrase]. **Notably** [Emphasis], South Korea **consistently maintained** [Strong collocation] the highest **adoption rates** [Formal - "usage levels"], while Vietnam, **despite starting from a minimal base** [Concession clause], **exhibited the most dramatic growth** [Academic phrase - "increased the most"].

---

**[Body Paragraph 1 - Early period detail - 65 words]**

**In 2000** [Time marker], South Korea **stood at** [Formal phrase for data] 40%, **significantly outpacing** [Academic phrase - "much higher than"] the USA at 20% and Vietnam at **a mere** [Emphasis phrase] 2%. **Over the following decade** [Time phrase - 2000-2010], all three nations **witnessed substantial increases** [Formal phrase]. South Korea **surged to** [Strong verb for increase] 80% by 2010, while the USA **climbed steadily** [Verb + adverb] to 50%. Vietnam's figures **rose gradually** [Verb + adverb], **reaching** [Participle] 15%, though **remaining considerably lower** [Comparative phrase] than the other two countries.

---

**[Body Paragraph 2 - Later period detail - 60 words]**

**From 2010 onwards** [Time marker], the upward **trajectories continued** [Formal phrase - "trends continued"], **albeit at varying rates** [Formal phrase - "but at different speeds"]. South Korea's growth **began to plateau** [Specific trend vocabulary], **incrementally rising** [Formal phrase - "slowly increasing"] from 80% to 95% by 2020. The USA **followed a similar pattern** [Comparison phrase], **ascending** [Formal verb - "going up"] to 75%. **In contrast** [Comparison linker], Vietnam **demonstrated remarkable progress** [Strong phrase], **more than quadrupling** [Specific comparison - x4] from 15% to 60%, **thereby narrowing the gap** [Result phrase] with **more developed nations** [Formal phrase].

---

**[Optional: Final comparison - if needed for word count - 15 words]**

**By 2020** [Time marker], the **disparity** [Formal - "difference"] between the three countries had **diminished considerably** [Formal phrase - "become much smaller"] **compared to 2000** [Comparison phrase].

## 📚 Vocabulary Analysis

### **Paraphrasing the Question:**
- **Shows** → **Illustrates, depicts, displays**
- **Percentage** → **Proportion, share**
- **Households** → **Homes**
- **Internet access** → **Internet connectivity, internet penetration, online access**
- **From 2000 to 2020** → **Over a 20-year period, spanning from 2000 to 2020, throughout the period**
- **Countries** → **Nations**

### **Overview Vocabulary:**
- **Overall, In general, Generally speaking** - overview markers
- **Experienced upward trends** - increased
- **Throughout the period** - during the whole time
- **Consistently maintained** - kept continuously
- **Adoption rates** - usage levels
- **Despite starting from a minimal base** - although beginning very low
- **Exhibited the most dramatic growth** - showed the biggest increase

### **Describing Data Points:**
- **Stood at** - was at (formal)
- **Significantly outpacing** - much higher than
- **A mere** - only (emphasizes how small)
- **Witnessed substantial increases** - saw big growth
- **Surged to** - increased rapidly to
- **Climbed steadily** - increased gradually
- **Rose gradually** - increased slowly
- **Reaching** - arriving at (participle)
- **Remaining considerably lower** - staying much lower

### **Time Phrases:**
- **In 2000** - at the start
- **Over the following decade** - in the next 10 years (2000-2010)
- **By 2010** - at the point of 2010
- **From 2010 onwards** - starting from 2010
- **By 2020** - at the end point

### **Trend Vocabulary:**

**Increases:**
- **Surged to** - increased rapidly
- **Climbed steadily** - increased gradually
- **Rose gradually** - increased slowly
- **Ascending** - going up (formal)
- **Incrementally rising** - slowly increasing
- **Demonstrated remarkable progress** - showed impressive growth

**Decreases (not used in this graph but useful):**
- **Plummeted, plunged** - decreased rapidly

**No change:**
- **Began to plateau** - started to level off

**Patterns:**
- **Upward trajectories** - upward trends
- **Followed a similar pattern** - showed the same trend
- **Varying rates** - different speeds

### **Comparison Vocabulary:**
- **Significantly outpacing** - much higher than
- **Considerably lower** - much lower
- **More than quadrupling** - multiplying by more than 4
- **Thereby narrowing the gap** - as a result, reducing the difference

## 🏗️ Task 1 Structure Template

### **Introduction (1 sentence):**
Paraphrase the question completely.
- "The [graph type] illustrates/depicts [topic] in [location] over/from [time period]."

### **Overview (2 sentences - CRITICAL!):**
Identify 2-3 most important trends. **NO SPECIFIC DATA.**
- "Overall, [general trend 1]. Notably/Additionally, [general trend 2]."

**Examples:**
- "Overall, all categories increased throughout the period."
- "Notably, X consistently remained the highest."
- "In contrast, Y showed the most dramatic change."

### **Body Paragraph 1 (3-4 sentences):**
Describe first time period or first group of data with specific figures.
- "In [year], X stood at [figure], significantly outpacing Y at [figure]..."
- "Over the following [time period], X surged to [figure]..."

### **Body Paragraph 2 (3-4 sentences):**
Describe second time period or second group of data with specific figures and comparisons.
- "From [year] onwards, the trend continued..."
- "In contrast, Y demonstrated remarkable progress..."
- "By [final year], the gap had narrowed considerably..."

## ⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

### **1. No Overview or Weak Overview:**
- ❌ Missing overview completely (automatic Band 5 max!)
- ❌ "The graph shows internet usage in three countries." (Just restating intro)
- ✅ "Overall, all three countries experienced upward trends, with South Korea consistently maintaining the highest adoption rates."

### **2. Including Specific Data in Overview:**
- ❌ "Overall, South Korea increased from 40% to 95%."
- ✅ "Overall, South Korea consistently maintained the highest adoption rates."

### **3. Describing Every Single Data Point:**
- ❌ "In 2000 it was 40%, in 2001 it was 42%, in 2002 it was 45%..."
- ✅ "Over the following decade, South Korea surged to 80% by 2010."

### **4. Not Making Comparisons:**
- ❌ "South Korea was 40%. USA was 20%. Vietnam was 2%."
- ✅ "South Korea stood at 40%, significantly outpacing the USA at 20% and Vietnam at a mere 2%."

### **5. Weak Paraphrasing:**
- ❌ "The graph shows the percentage of households with internet access..."
- ✅ "The line graph illustrates the proportion of homes with internet connectivity..."

### **6. Too Short:**
- ❌ 130-149 words
- ✅ 170-200 words (ideal range)

### **7. Giving Opinions or Reasons:**
- ❌ "I think South Korea increased because of good technology."
- ✅ Only report what you see in the graph objectively

### **8. Using Informal Language:**
- ❌ "Went up a lot", "got bigger", "was way higher"
- ✅ "Surged dramatically", "increased substantially", "significantly outpaced"

## 🎯 Useful Phrases Bank

### **Starting Introduction:**
- "The line graph illustrates/depicts/displays..."
- "The chart shows/demonstrates/compares..."
- "The diagram presents information about..."

### **Overview Starters:**
- "Overall, it is clear that..."
- "In general, the data reveals that..."
- "Notably, the most significant trend was..."
- "Overall, while X increased, Y decreased..."

### **Describing Starting Points:**
- "In [year], X stood at [figure]..."
- "At the beginning of the period, X was [figure]..."
- "X started at [figure] in [year]..."

### **Showing Trends:**
- "Over the following [time period], X surged/climbed/rose to..."
- "Throughout the period, X remained relatively stable..."
- "From [year] onwards, X began to plateau..."

### **Making Comparisons:**
- "Significantly outpacing..."
- "Considerably lower/higher than..."
- "In contrast, Y demonstrated..."
- "While X increased, Y decreased..."
- "Compared to...", "In comparison with..."

### **Ending Points:**
- "By [final year], X had reached [figure]..."
- "At the end of the period, X stood at [figure]..."
- "Consequently, the gap had narrowed/widened..."
