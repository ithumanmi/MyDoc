# 🗺️ Task 1: Map - Town Development

> [← Back to Writing Samples Index](./README.md)

## 📌 Question:

**The maps below show the changes that have taken place in Meadowside village and Frogsbrough, a neighboring town, between 1962 and the present day.**

**Summarize the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.**

**Write at least 150 words.**

## 🏗️ Map Task Structure Template

### **Introduction (1 sentence):**
Paraphrase the question completely.
- "The maps depict/illustrate the transformation of [place] over [time period]."
- Always mention BOTH time periods shown

### **Overview (2 sentences - CRITICAL!):**
Describe the OVERALL transformation. NO specific locations!
- "Overall, the area underwent [type of change - urbanization/development/expansion]."
- "Most notably, [main feature 1] and [main feature 2] were introduced."

**Example:**
- "Overall, the area evolved from a rural village into a suburban center."
- "Transportation infrastructure and commercial facilities were progressively introduced."

### **Body Paragraph 1 (4-5 sentences):**
Describe FIRST to MIDDLE time period changes.
- Start: "In [year], [place] consisted of..."
- Changes: "By [year], [feature] was converted into..., while [feature] was erected..."
- Use passive voice throughout!

### **Body Paragraph 2 (4-5 sentences):**
Describe MIDDLE to PRESENT changes.
- "From [year] to the present, further development occurred..."
- "[Feature] was enlarged/introduced..."
- "[Feature] remained intact..."

## ⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

### **1. Not Using Passive Voice:**
- ❌ "They built a supermarket." (Who is "they"?)
- ✅ "A supermarket was erected to the north of the original settlement."

### **2. Weak Location Descriptions:**
- ❌ "The hotel is near the supermarket."
- ✅ "A hotel was built to the west of the supermarket, in close proximity to the leisure complex."

### **3. Missing Overview:**
- ❌ Going straight into describing 1962
- ✅ "Overall, the area underwent significant urbanization, evolving from a rural village..."

### **4. Giving Opinions or Reasons:**
- ❌ "They built a railway because more people moved there."
- ✅ "A railway line was constructed, linking Meadowside with Frogsbrough."

### **5. Present Simple Instead of Past Passive:**
- ❌ "In 1985, there is a supermarket."
- ✅ "By 1985, a supermarket was erected..."

### **6. Vague Descriptions:**
- ❌ "Things changed a lot."
- ✅ "The meadow was converted into a housing estate, while commercial facilities were introduced."

### **7. No Directional Information:**
- ❌ "A hotel was built."
- ✅ "A hotel was built to the west, adjacent to the leisure complex."

### **8. Adding Conclusion:**
- ❌ "In conclusion, the village became modern." (Not needed!)
- ✅ End with final changes or optional comparison

## 🎯 Map-Specific Grammar

### **1. Passive Voice (90% of sentences!):**
- ✅ "A railway **was constructed**..."
- ✅ "The meadow **was converted into** a housing estate..."
- ✅ "Commercial facilities **were introduced**..."

### **2. Participle Clauses (showing purpose/function):**
- ✅ "A railway was built, **linking** Meadowside with Frogsbrough."
- ✅ "The housing estate expanded, **extending southward**."
- ✅ "Infrastructure remained, **continuing to serve** the community."

### **3. Location Prepositions:**
- **to the [direction] of:** "to the north of the station"
- **adjacent to:** "adjacent to the railway"
- **in close proximity to:** "in close proximity to the supermarket"

### **4. Time Expressions:**
- **In [year]:** "In 1962, the area consisted of..."
- **By [year]:** "By 1985, a supermarket had been built..."
- **From [year] to [year]:** "From 1985 to the present..."
