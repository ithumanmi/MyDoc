# 📝 Discussion Essay: Government vs Individual Responsibility

> [← Back to Writing Samples Index](./README.md)

## 📌 Question:

**"Some people believe that governments should solve societal problems, while others think individuals should take responsibility. Discuss both views and give your own opinion."**

**Essay Type:** Discussion (Discuss Both Views + Opinion)
**Recommended Time:** 40 minutes
**Word Count Target:** 270-290 words

---

## ❌ Band 6.0 Sample (Common Problems)

There are many problems in society today. **[Weak opening - states obvious]** Some people think government should fix them. **[Too simple]** Others think people should fix problems themselves. **[Too simple]** I will talk about both ideas. **[Mechanical announcement]**

Governments have a lot of power and money. **[Informal language]** They can make laws to solve problems. **[Too vague]** For example, they can help poor people. **[Undeveloped]** Governments can also build schools and hospitals. **[List without development]** This is important because individuals don't have money for these things. **[Grammar + vague]**

However, individuals should also be responsible. **[Weak topic sentence]** People should not wait for government to fix everything. **[Too simple]** They can help in their communities. **[Vague - how?]** If everyone does small things, society will be better. **[Overly optimistic]** My family always helps neighbors. **[Weak personal example]**

I think both government and individuals are important. **[Fence-sitting]** They need to work together. **[Too vague]** This is the best way to solve problems. **[Weak ending]**

**(148 words - too short!)**

## 🏗️ Discussion Essay Structure (Balanced + Integrated Opinion)

### **Introduction (2 sentences):**
Present debate, state balanced position.

**Template:**
"[Topic] remains contentious. While some advocate [View A], others emphasize [View B]. I contend that optimal outcomes require [synergistic approach]."

### **Body Paragraph 1 (Government View - PEER):**
Objective discussion with strong evidence.
- **P:** "Proponents of governmental primacy convincingly argue..."
- **E:** Explain why government action needed
- **E:** Specific example with data (Singapore 80%)
- **R:** What only governments can do

**Key:** Equal depth as paragraph 2 (100-102 words)

### **Body Paragraph 2 (Individual View + Integrated Opinion - PEER):**
Balance with equally strong argument, weave in opinion.
- **P:** "Nevertheless, individual responsibility proves equally crucial..."
- **E:** Explain individual contributions
- **E:** Specific example with data (Harvard 37%)
- **R:** "From my perspective..." - integrate your nuanced view

**Key:** Opinion shows both needed for different reasons!

### **Conclusion:**
Restate complementary relationship.

**Template:**
"While governments possess capabilities for [macro challenges], individual actions remain indispensable for [cultural/community]. True progress emerges from complementary efforts."

## ⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

### **1. Unequal Treatment:**
- ❌ Body 1: 150 words (detailed), Body 2: 50 words (rushed)
- ✅ Body 1: 102 words, Body 2: 100 words (balanced!)

### **2. Vague "Both Are Important":**
- ❌ "Both government and individuals are important and should work together."
- ✅ "Governments must address systemic issues, while individuals foster cultural values and community engagement - complementary roles."

### **3. No Specific Examples:**
- ❌ "Governments can help poor people."
- ✅ "Singapore eradicated extreme poverty through public housing that accommodated 80% of the population."

### **4. Personal Anecdotes Only:**
- ❌ "My family always helps neighbors."
- ✅ "Harvard's Social Capital Project reveals communities with high individual engagement exhibit 37% lower crime rates."

### **5. Weak Integration of Opinion:**
- ❌ "I think both are good." (fence-sitting!)
- ✅ "From my perspective, while governments establish frameworks for large-scale challenges, sustained improvement hinges on individual commitment to daily ethical choices."
