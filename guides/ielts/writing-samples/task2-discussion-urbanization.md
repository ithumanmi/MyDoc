# 📝 Discussion Essay: City vs Countryside Living

> [← Back to Writing Samples Index](./README.md)

## 📌 Question:

**"Some people prefer to live in the city, while others prefer to live in the countryside. Discuss both views and give your own opinion."**

**Essay Type:** Discussion (Discuss Both Views + Opinion)
**Recommended Time:** 40 minutes
**Word Count Target:** 270-290 words

---

## 🏗️ Discussion Essay Structure (Balanced)

### **Introduction (1-2 sentences):**
Introduce the debate, mention both views briefly.

**Template:**
"[Topic] has long been debated, with proponents of each citing distinct advantages. While [View A offers X], [View B provides Y]. I believe [your nuanced opinion]."

### **Body Paragraph 1 (First View - PEER):**
Discuss objectively and thoroughly.
- **P:** "[View A] advocates compellingly argue that..."
- **E:** Explain advantages clearly
- **E:** Specific examples with data
- **R:** Result/significance

**Key:** Be objective, equal depth as paragraph 2!

### **Body Paragraph 2 (Second View + Opinion - PEER):**
Start with "Conversely" for balance.
- **P:** "Conversely, [View B] proponents emphasize..."
- **E:** Explain advantages clearly
- **E:** Specific examples with data
- **R:** Weave in your opinion ("From my perspective...")

**Key:** Equal length (100-105 words each paragraph)

### **Conclusion:**
Acknowledge both, state your balanced view.

**Template:**
"In conclusion, while [View A] excels in [X], [View B] offers [Y]. The ideal choice ultimately hinges on [personal factors]."

## ⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

### **1. Unequal Treatment:**
- ❌ Body 1: 150 words, Body 2: 50 words (unbalanced!)
- ✅ Body 1: 100 words, Body 2: 105 words (balanced)

### **2. Stereotypical Views:**
- ❌ "Young people should live in cities, old people in countryside."
- ✅ "The choice depends on individual life stage and career priorities."

### **3. No Specific Examples:**
- ❌ "Cities have more opportunities."
- ✅ "UN-Habitat Global Cities Report indicates urban residents earn 35% more."

### **4. Vague Advantages:**
- ❌ "Countryside is nice and quiet."
- ✅ "Rural areas boast 30% lower rates of respiratory illnesses and allergies."

### **5. Fence-Sitting Without Nuance:**
- ❌ "Both are good, it depends on the person."
- ✅ "Urban living suits early career stages, while rural environments increasingly appeal to those prioritizing health and work-life balance, particularly given remote work opportunities."
