# 🕵️ Publisher Due Diligence Checklist

> **Goal:** Giảm rủi ro ký nhầm publisher bằng cách audit năng lực tài chính, marketing, liveops, legal trước khi ký.
> **Deliverables:** Q&A doc, risk matrix, reference check log, deal breaker list.

## 1. Financial Stability
- [ ] Hỏi về nguồn vốn (VC, self-funded, public?).
- [ ] MG (minimum guarantee) có escrow không?
- [ ] Payment schedule? Net 30 hay milestone-based?
- [ ] Case khi game underperform: có clawback không?
- [ ] Yêu cầu bank reference hoặc audited report.

## 2. Marketing & UA Capability
- [ ] Budget UA / month? Channel chính (TikTok, Meta, influencer)?
- [ ] UA team size, track record (game nào scale thành công?).
- [ ] Store featuring support? Quan hệ với Apple/Google/Steam?
- [ ] ASO process? Localization languages?
- [ ] KPI sharing cadence (weekly reporting?).

## 3. Production & LiveOps Support
- [ ] Producer/PM assigned? Team size?
- [ ] Tooling: analytics, crash reporting, CMS?
- [ ] LiveOps cadence support (designers, economy team)?
- [ ] QA resources, test device lab?
- [ ] Playtest community / user research support?

## 4. Legal & IP
- [ ] IP ownership: Who owns? Reversion clause?
- [ ] Termination rights: with/without cause?
- [ ] Territory: Global hay region? Sub-licensing allowed?
- [ ] Exclusivity clause? Non-compete?
- [ ] Arbitration/jurisdiction (Singapore, US?).

## 5. Success Metrics & Governance
- [ ] Define success KPIs upfront (DAU, revenue, retention).
- [ ] Decision rights: ai quyết định delay, monetization change?
- [ ] Reporting cadence: weekly war-room, monthly exec review?
- [ ] Escalation process khi dispute?

## 6. Reference Checks
- [ ] Nói chuyện với 2 studios từng ký publisher này.
- [ ] Câu hỏi: “Họ có trả tiền đúng hạn?”, “Support marketing có như cam kết?”, “Có interfere creative quá mức?”
- [ ] Check Glassdoor / cộng đồng xem review tiêu cực.

## 7. Risk Matrix
| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|------------|--------|------------|
| Delay payment | Medium | High | Escrow, late fee |
| Push paywall quá sớm | Medium | Medium | Control monetization clause |
| Lack marketing budget | Low | High | Ask for committed budget |

## 8. Deal Breakers
- Refuse transparency KPI/marketing spend.
- No recourse khi họ cancel project.
- Ownership chuyển hoàn toàn cho publisher.
- Payment terms > Net 90.
- Legal in jurisdiction khó enforce (random offshore).

## 9. Documentation
- Store Q&A in Notion/Drive.
- Track version of contract redlines.
- Use lawyer review (Budget $1k-3k) cho deal >$200k.
