## 1. Tại sao Freelance?

### Lợi ích

**Financial:**
- Thu nhập phụ $300-$1000+/tháng
- Đa dạng hóa nguồn thu (Không phụ thuộc 1 công ty)
- Học cách định giá bản thân

**Career:**
- Đa dạng dự án → Học nhiều hơn công ty
- Xây dựng portfolio đa dạng
- Network với clients quốc tế

**Freedom:**
- Chọn dự án mình thích
- Remote 100%
- Passive income potential (Asset Store, Templates)

---

### Thách thức

**Time:**
- Full-time job + Freelance = 50-60h/tuần
- Risk of burnout

**Uncertainty:**
- Thu nhập không ổn định tháng đầu
- Clients khó tính, scope creep

**Legal:**
- Thuế, hoá đơn (Nếu thu nhập lớn)

**Solution:** Start small (5-10h/tuần), sau đó scale.

## 2. Platforms & Marketplaces

### A. Freelance Platforms (Service-based)

#### **Upwork (Recommended #1)**

**Pros:**
- Lượng jobs nhiều nhất
- Payment protection (Escrow)
- Long-term clients potential

**Cons:**
- Connects fee (Bid tốn tiền)
- Cạnh tranh cao (India, Philippines)

**Niche tốt cho Game Dev:**
- Unity mobile games (Hyper-casual, Educational)
- Game prototypes cho startups
- AR/VR experiences
- Multiplayer integration

**Profile Setup:**
```
Title: "Unity Developer | Mobile Games | 2D/3D"

Overview:
"Hi! I'm [Name], a Unity game developer with [X] years of experience 
building engaging mobile games. I specialize in:

✅ 2D/3D game development (Unity)
✅ Hyper-casual games for iOS/Android
✅ Game optimization (60fps on mid-range devices)
✅ Multiplayer integration (Photon/Mirror)

Recent projects:
🎮 [Game 1] - 100k+ downloads on Google Play
🎮 [Game 2] - Featured on Itch.io

Let's build something amazing together!"
```

**Rate:**
- Beginner: $15-$25/hour
- Experienced: $30-$60/hour
- Expert: $60-$100/hour

---

#### **Fiverr (Recommended #2)**

**Model:** Bạn tạo "Gigs" (Dịch vụ cố định), clients mua.

**Gig Ideas:**

**Basic ($50-$100):**
- "I will create a Unity 2D mobile game template"
- "I will fix bugs in your Unity project"
- "I will integrate ads (AdMob/Unity Ads)"

**Standard ($200-$500):**
- "I will develop a complete hyper-casual game"
- "I will create Unity VR experience"

**Premium ($500-$1500):**
- "I will develop multiplayer mobile game"
- "I will create AAA-quality game trailer"

**Gig Optimization:**
- **Title:** Keyword-rich ("Unity", "Mobile Game", "2D", "3D")
- **Thumbnail:** Eye-catching gameplay GIF
- **Video intro:** 30s introducing yourself + showcasing work
- **FAQ:** Preempt common questions

**Pricing tiers:**
```
Basic ($100): Simple reskin + 3 days delivery
Standard ($250): Custom mechanics + 7 days + Source code
Premium ($500): Full game + 14 days + Monetization + Support
```

---

#### **Freelancer.com**

**Pros:** Nhiều jobs từ châu Á.  
**Cons:** Quality clients thấp hơn Upwork.

## 4. Bidding & Proposal Strategy

### A. Upwork Bidding

**Anatomy of a winning proposal:**

#### **1. Personalized Opening (Show you read the job)**
```
Hi [Client Name],

I read your job post about creating a 2D platformer game for iOS. 
I noticed you mentioned wanting a "retro pixel art style" - I recently 
completed a similar project [Link] that got 50k+ downloads.
```

#### **2. Relevant Experience**
```
I've built 5+ mobile games with Unity, including:
🎮 [Game 1]: 2D endless runner - 100k downloads
🎮 [Game 2]: Puzzle game - Featured on App Store

Here's my portfolio: [Link]
```

#### **3. Specific Plan (Show you understand scope)**
```
For your project, I propose:

Week 1: Core mechanics (Movement, Jump, Enemy AI)
Week 2: Level design (10 levels) + UI/UX
Week 3: Polish (Sounds, Particles, Testing)

Deliverables:
✅ Unity project (Source code)
✅ APK build
✅ Documentation
```

#### **4. Call-to-Action**
```
I'm available to start immediately. Can we schedule a quick call 
to discuss the art style and gameplay details?

Best,
[Name]
```

**Length:** 300-500 words (Đủ chi tiết nhưng không dài dòng).

---

### B. Red Flags (Jobs cần tránh)

❌ **"We need a GTA clone in 2 weeks"** → Unrealistic scope  
❌ **"Budget: $50"** → Lowball  
❌ **"Send sample work first (unpaid)"** → Spec work scam  
❌ **No reviews, payment method not verified** → Risk không thanh toán

✅ **Look for:**
- Client có reviews tốt (4.5+ stars)
- Payment method verified
- Clear scope, realistic timeline

---

### C. Connects Strategy (Upwork)

**Connects = Tiền:** Mỗi bid tốn 2-6 connects ($0.30-$0.90).

**Strategy:**
- Chỉ bid vào jobs **match 80%+ skills**
- Avoid competitions >20 proposals
- Focus on jobs posted <24h ago

## 6. Pricing Strategy

### A. Hourly vs Fixed Price

**Hourly Rate:**
- **Pros:** Bảo vệ khi scope thay đổi
- **Cons:** Client lo sợ bạn kéo dài

**Fixed Price:**
- **Pros:** Client biết trước chi phí
- **Cons:** Scope creep = bạn lỗ

**Best practice:** 
- **Small projects (<$500):** Fixed price
- **Large/Uncertain projects:** Hourly

---

### B. Cách tính giá

**Formula:**
```
Price = (Hours estimated) × (Hourly rate) × (Risk factor)
```

**Example: 2D Platformer Game**
```
Estimated hours: 40h
Your hourly rate: $25
Risk factor: 1.2 (vì client mơ hồ)

Price = 40 × $25 × 1.2 = $1200
```

**Add buffer:** Luôn estimate thêm 20-30% cho bugs/revisions.

---

### C. Pricing Table (Reference)

| Task | Junior ($15-25/h) | Mid ($30-50/h) | Senior ($60-100/h) |
|------|------------------|----------------|-------------------|
| **Hyper-casual game** | $300-$600 | $800-$1500 | $2000-$4000 |
| **2D Platformer** | $600-$1200 | $1500-$3000 | $4000-$8000 |
| **3D FPS (Multiplayer)** | $2000-$4000 | $5000-$10k | $15k-$30k |
| **Bug fixing** | $50-$100 | $100-$300 | $300-$500 |
| **Game reskin** | $100-$300 | $300-$800 | $800-$1500 |
| **Unity consulting (1h)** | $15-$25 | $30-$50 | $60-$100 |

---

### D. Khi nào tăng giá?

**Signals:**
- 100% completion rate + 5-star reviews
- Clients chấp nhận giá hiện tại không chê
- Bạn quá bận (Nhiều offer hơn thời gian)

**Tăng 10-20% mỗi 3-6 tháng.**

## 7. Time Management

### A. Schedule mẫu (Full-time job + Freelance)

**Weekdays:**
```
06:00-07:00: Sáng - Check emails, plan ngày
08:00-17:00: Công ty chính
18:00-19:00: Dinner, rest
19:00-21:00: Freelance work (2h)
21:00-22:00: Portfolio/Content (Build assets, blog)
22:00: Sleep
```

**Total:** 10h freelance/tuần (weekdays).

**Weekend:**
```
Saturday: 4-6h freelance (Focused work)
Sunday: Rest / Learning new skills
```

**Total:** 14-16h freelance/tuần.

---

### B. Productivity Tips

**1. Time Blocking:**
- Dùng Pomodoro (25 phút work, 5 phút rest)
- App: Forest, Toggl Track

**2. Limit WIP (Work In Progress):**
- Max 1-2 projects cùng lúc
- Finish trước khi nhận mới

**3. Communicate Clearly:**
- Set expectations: "I work evenings 7-9pm GMT+7"
- Update clients hàng ngày (15-minute daily standup message)

---

### C. Tránh Burnout

**Red Flags:**
- Ngủ <6h/đêm liên tục
- Stress, mất hứng thú với cả 2 jobs
- Sức khỏe giảm

**Solutions:**
- 1 ngày/tuần OFF hoàn toàn (không code)
- Exercise 30 phút/ngày
- Nếu quá tải → Tăng giá (để giảm số lượng jobs)

## 8. Legal & Tax (Vietnam)

### A. Thuế Thu nhập cá nhân

**Dưới 120 triệu VND/năm ($5000):**
- Không cần đăng ký kinh doanh
- Tự khai thuế (Hoặc ignore nếu quá nhỏ - risky)

**Trên 120 triệu VND/năm:**
- **Option 1:** Đăng ký Hộ kinh doanh (5% thuế)
- **Option 2:** Công ty (25% thuế doanh nghiệp - chỉ khi thu nhập rất cao)

**Consult:** Tìm kế toán để tư vấn (Fee: 500k-1tr VND/tháng).

---

### B. Nhận tiền từ nước ngoài

**PayPal → Ngân hàng VN:**
- Fee: ~3-5%
- Limit: $25k/năm (cá nhân)

**Wise (TransferWise):**
- Fee thấp hơn PayPal (~1-2%)
- Support VND

**Payoneer:**
- Tốt cho Upwork, Fiverr
- Rút về VN qua ACB, VCB

---

### C. Contract & Legal Protection

**Upwork/Fiverr:** Platform bảo vệ bạn (Escrow, dispute resolution).

**Direct clients:** 
- **Viết contract đơn giản:**
  ```
  Services: [Describe work]
  Deliverables: [List clearly]
  Timeline: [Milestones]
  Payment: 50% upfront, 50% on delivery
  Revision: 2 rounds included
  Copyright: Transferred upon final payment
  ```

- Tool: HelloSign, PandaDoc (Free tiers)

## 10. Common Mistakes

### ❌ Mistake 1: Underpricing

**Ví dụ:** 
- Job: "Build Flappy Bird clone"
- You bid: $50 (Nghĩ sẽ mất 2-3h)
- Reality: 10h (bugs, client revisions)
- **Loss:** $50 / 10h = $5/h (dưới lương tối thiểu!)

**Fix:** 
- Luôn estimate × 2
- Minimum bid: $100 (dưới này không nhận)

---

### ❌ Mistake 2: Scope Creep

**Scenario:**
- Contract: "Build 5 levels"
- Client: "Can you add 10 more levels? And multiplayer?"

**Fix:**
- **Contract rõ ràng:** "Additional features: $X per feature"
- **Learn to say NO:** "That's outside the scope. We can do it for $Y extra."

---

### ❌ Mistake 3: Không collect testimonials

**Sau mỗi project:**
```
"Hi [Client], glad you're happy with the game! 
Would you mind leaving a review on Upwork? 
It really helps me grow my freelance business. Thanks!"
```

**Reviews = Social proof = More jobs.**

---

### ❌ Mistake 4: Quá phụ thuộc 1 platform

**Risk:** Upwork suspend account → Mất hết income.

**Fix:** Diversify
- Upwork + Fiverr
- Direct clients
- Passive income (Asset Store)

---

### ❌ Mistake 5: Không reinvest

**Sau khi kiếm được $1000:**
- ❌ Tiêu hết
- ✅ Reinvest:
  - $200: Paid courses (Nâng cấp skill)
  - $100: Tools (Better assets, plugins)
  - $700: Savings

## 🎯 Kết luận

- Tháng đầu: $50-$200 (Build portfolio)
