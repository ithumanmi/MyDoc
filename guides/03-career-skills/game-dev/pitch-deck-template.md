# 🎟️ Game Pitch Deck Template

> **Goal:** Chuẩn hóa deck 12 slide để gửi publisher/investor, thể hiện rõ vision, market, KPI, tài chính và nhu cầu.
> **Deliverables:** Slide deck (Keynote/PPT), data room link, deal tracker.
> **Use with:** [Working with Publishers](./working-with-publishers.md), [Publisher Financial Model](./publisher-financial-model.md), [Game Indie Hacker Guide](./game-indie-hacker-guide.md)

## Slide-by-slide Structure

| # | Slide | Purpose | Tips |
|---|-------|---------|------|
| 1 | Title | Game name, studio, contact | Include logo, short tagline |
| 2 | Elevator Pitch | 1-liner về game + hook | “Co-op roguelite roguelike + base building” |
| 3 | Market & Player Insight | TAM/SAM + persona | Use data (Steam stats, SensorTower) |
| 4 | Problem & Opportunity | Pain của player → gap thị trường | “No Vietnamese co-op survival game” |
| 5 | Gameplay Pillars | 3 pillars + GIF/visual | Use gifs/video link |
| 6 | Progress & Metrics | Prototype, KPIs (retention, wishlist) | Show numbers (D1=35%, Wishlist=25k) |
| 7 | Team | Core members, shipped titles | Highlight relevant experience |
| 8 | Monetization & LiveOps | Revenue model, economy guardrails | Show LTV vs CAC, BP plan |
| 9 | Roadmap | Milestones tới launch | Visual timeline (Q2 Alpha → Q4 Launch) |
| 10 | Funding Ask / Deal Structure | Bạn cần gì? ($, marketing, UA) | Be specific: “Need $500k MG + UA budget” |
| 11 | Traction & Community | Discord, social proof, press | Include testimonials, influencer interest |
| 12 | Call-to-Action | Contact info, data room link | “Schedule milestone review” |

## Data Room Checklist
- [ ] Build/Gameplay video (Loom/YouTube unlisted)
- [ ] Milestone schedule & budget (Excel)
- [ ] Vertical slice hoặc prototype download
- [ ] KPI dashboard (Notion link/Looker Studio)
- [ ] Legal docs (Company info, cap table)
- [ ] Press kit (Art, screenshots, logo)

## Key Metrics to Highlight
- Wishlist count (Steam) / Pre-registrations (Mobile)
- Retention (D1/D7/D30)
- Playtest feedback (NPS, qualitative)
- Community size (Discord members, newsletter)
- Revenue projections (from [Financial Model](./publisher-financial-model.md))

## Common Mistakes
- Slide quá chữ / thiếu visual
- Không nêu rõ budget sử dụng làm gì
- Bỏ quên plan marketing/UA
- Không nói rõ team capability (ai build gì)
- Deck quá dài (>15 slides)

## Tips
- Embed short gameplay clip mỗi slide (GIF, hyperlink)
- Gửi PDF + link interactive (Pitch.com)
- Follow up 48h sau khi gửi (Email + LinkedIn)
- Prepare data room trước khi publisher hỏi
- Practice 5-7 phút pitch + 10 phút Q&A
