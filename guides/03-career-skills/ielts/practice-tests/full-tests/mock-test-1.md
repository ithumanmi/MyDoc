# 🧪 Mock Test 1 (Full 4-Skill)

> Tái hiện đề thi IELTS Academic chuẩn. Tổng thời gian ~2h45'.

## Structure

1. **Listening (30')** – Section 1-4 (40 câu)
   - Audio: Cambridge Mock (link placeholder)
   - Answer sheet PDF (đính kèm)
2. **Reading (60')** – 3 passages, 40 câu
   - Passage 1: Urban Planning
   - Passage 2: Neuroscience of Learning
   - Passage 3: Space Exploration Ethics
3. **Writing (60')**
   - Task 1: Line graph (Energy mix 1990-2020)
   - Task 2: "Some believe art classes are essential in schools, others say they are a waste of time. Discuss both views and give your opinion."
4. **Speaking (11-14')**
   - Part 1: Hometown, Daily Routine, Technology use
   - Part 2: Describe a community event you joined
   - Part 3: Social cohesion, Digital communities

## Usage

- Làm full test mỗi 2 tuần.
- Ghi chép điểm + nhận xét vào `score-tracker-template.md`.
- Sau khi hoàn thành, đối chiếu đáp án tại `./answer-keys/` (đang cập nhật).
