# 📘 Academic Vocabulary in Context

## 1. Identify Context Clues

- **Definition clues:** "X, meaning Y..."
- **Contrast clues:** however, although → suy ra nghĩa trái ngược.
- **Example clues:** such as, for example.

## 2. 4-step Method

1. Highlight câu chứa từ mới.
2. Gạch keywords liên quan.
3. Đoán nghĩa dựa trên logic.
4. Kiểm tra bằng dictionary → ghi note collocation.

## 3. Vocabulary Log Template

| Word | Context sentence | Guess | Actual meaning | Collocations |
|------|------------------|-------|----------------|--------------|
| resilience | "... remarkable resilience to climate change" | ability to recover | ability to recover quickly | show resilience, build resilience |
