# 📊 IELTS Band Descriptors: Understanding Scoring Criteria

> [← Back to IELTS Guide](./README.md)

Hiểu rõ **tiêu chí chấm điểm** là chìa khóa để cải thiện band score. Hướng dẫn này giải thích chi tiết 4 tiêu chí cho Writing & Speaking.

---

## 🎯 Overall Band Score Calculation

### **Listening & Reading:**
- Straightforward - Number of correct answers = Band score
- Listening: 40 questions (30+ correct ≈ Band 7.0)
- Reading: 40 questions (30+ correct ≈ Band 7.0)

### **Writing & Speaking:**
- Assessed by 4 criteria (equal weight)
- **Final score = Average of 4 criteria** (rounded to nearest 0.5)

**Example:**
- TR: 7.0, CC: 7.0, LR: 8.0, GRA: 7.0
- **Average:** (7+7+8+7) ÷ 4 = **7.25** → Rounded to **7.5**

---

## ✍️ WRITING Band Descriptors

### **4 Criteria (25% each):**

#### **1. TR (Task Response) - Task Achievement**
**"Did you answer the question fully?"**

| Band | Description |
|:---:|:---|
| **9** | Fully addresses all parts, well-developed position, relevant ideas |
| **7** | Addresses all parts, clear position, main ideas supported |
| **6** | Addresses the task but may be incomplete, position unclear |
| **5** | Only partially addresses task, limited development |

**Common mistakes:**
- ❌ Not giving your opinion when asked
- ❌ Only discussing one view in "Discuss both views" question
- ❌ Writing < 250 words (automatic penalty)

**Band 7.5 Checklist:**
- ✅ Answer ALL parts of the question
- ✅ Clear thesis statement in introduction
- ✅ Each body paragraph = 1 main idea + development
- ✅ 270-290 words (optimal length)

---

#### **2. CC (Coherence & Cohesion)**
**"Is your essay logically organized and easy to follow?"**

| Band | Description |
|:---:|:---|
| **9** | Seamless flow, skillful paragraphing, flawless cohesion |
| **7** | Clear progression, appropriate use of cohesive devices |
| **6** | Some organization, but cohesive devices may be faulty |
| **5** | Inadequate organization, repetitive linking words |

**What examiners look for:**
- ✅ Clear paragraphing (Intro → Body 1 → Body 2 → Conclusion)
- ✅ Topic sentences at start of each paragraph
- ✅ Linking words used naturally (not forced)
- ✅ Referencing & pronoun use (The government → It → Authorities)

**Linking Words by Function:**
- **Adding:** Furthermore, Moreover, In addition
- **Contrasting:** However, On the other hand, Nevertheless
- **Cause/Effect:** Therefore, Consequently, As a result
- **Example:** For instance, To illustrate, Such as

**Common mistakes:**
- ❌ Overusing "Firstly, Secondly, Thirdly..."
- ❌ Starting every sentence with linking words
- ❌ One-sentence paragraphs

---

#### **3. LR (Lexical Resource)**
**"Is your vocabulary accurate, varied, and appropriate?"**

| Band | Description |
|:---:|:---|
| **9** | Wide range, natural & sophisticated, rare errors |
| **7** | Sufficient range, some less common words, awareness of style |
| **6** | Adequate vocabulary, some errors in word choice |
| **5** | Limited range, noticeable errors, repetition |

**Band 7.5 Requirements:**
- ✅ Use **less common vocabulary** (not just simple words)
- ✅ Use **collocations** correctly (make a decision, take action)
- ✅ Show **awareness of style** (formal language, no contractions)
- ✅ Paraphrase key words from the question

**Vocabulary Showcase:**
| Band 6 | Band 7+ |
|:---|:---|
| Important | Crucial, Vital, Essential |
| Bad | Detrimental, Adverse, Harmful |
| Good | Beneficial, Advantageous |
| Show | Demonstrate, Illustrate |
| Many | Numerous, A plethora of |

**Common mistakes:**
- ❌ Spelling errors (even one can drop you to 6.5)
- ❌ Using informal words ("kids" → "children")
- ❌ Word repetition (use synonyms!)

---

#### **4. GRA (Grammatical Range & Accuracy)**
**"Do you use complex grammar correctly?"**

| Band | Description |
|:---:|:---|
| **9** | Full range of structures, error-free |
| **7** | Variety of complex structures, frequent error-free sentences |
| **6** | Mix of simple & complex, some errors |
| **5** | Limited range, errors may cause difficulty |

**Band 7.5 Must-Have Structures:**
1. **Complex sentences:** Although X, Y... / While X, Y...
2. **Relative clauses:** The government, which is responsible for..., should...
3. **Passive voice:** This problem can be addressed by...
4. **Conditionals:** If governments invested more..., then...
5. **Participle clauses:** Having considered both views, I believe...

**Common mistakes:**
- ❌ Only using simple sentences
- ❌ Subject-verb agreement errors
- ❌ Article mistakes (a/an/the)
- ❌ Run-on sentences

## 🗣️ SPEAKING Band Descriptors

### **4 Criteria (25% each):**

#### **1. FC (Fluency & Coherence)**
**"Can you speak smoothly without long pauses?"**

| Band | Description |
|:---:|:---|
| **9** | Speaks fluently, coherent ideas, no repetition |
| **7** | Speaks at length without effort, coherent, occasional repetition |
| **6** | Willing to speak at length, but coherence may be lost |
| **5** | Speaks with noticeable effort, frequent repetition |

**Band 7.5 Checklist:**
- ✅ Speak without long pauses (2-3 second pauses OK)
- ✅ Self-correct naturally (don't panic when making mistakes)
- ✅ Use discourse markers (Well..., Actually..., To be honest...)
- ✅ Organize ideas logically (Past → Present → Future)

**Fluency Killers:**
- ❌ Long silences (5+ seconds)
- ❌ Memorized answers (sounds robotic)
- ❌ Answering too shortly (Yes/No answers)

---

#### **2. LR (Lexical Resource)**
**"Do you use a wide range of vocabulary flexibly?"**

| Band | Description |
|:---:|:---|
| **9** | Uses vocabulary with full flexibility, rare imprecision |
| **7** | Flexible use of vocabulary, uses less common words |
| **6** | Has enough vocabulary, attempts paraphrase |
| **5** | Limited vocabulary, inappropriate word choice |

**Band 7.5 Requirements:**
- ✅ Use idiomatic language (once in a blue moon, over the moon)
- ✅ Paraphrase when you don't know a word
- ✅ Use topic-specific vocabulary naturally

**Example - Part 2 (Describing a place):**
- Band 6: "The place is very beautiful and big."
- Band 7+: "The scenery is **breathtaking**, and the area is **incredibly spacious**."

**Common mistakes:**
- ❌ Using big words incorrectly (trying to sound smart)
- ❌ Vietnamese direct translation
- ❌ Overusing fillers (you know, like, actually)

---

#### **3. GRA (Grammatical Range & Accuracy)**
**"Do you use different grammar structures correctly?"**

| Band | Description |
|:---:|:---|
| **9** | Uses full range of structures naturally & accurately |
| **7** | Uses range of structures flexibly, frequent error-free |
| **6** | Uses mix of simple & complex, makes errors |
| **5** | Attempts complex but with errors, basic structures OK |

**Band 7.5 Must-Use:**
1. **Mix of tenses:** Past, Present, Present Perfect, Future
2. **Conditionals:** If I had more time, I would...
3. **Passive:** It was built in 2010...
4. **Relative clauses:** The book, which I read last year, was...

**Common mistakes:**
- ❌ Only using simple present tense
- ❌ Subject-verb agreement (He go → He goes)
- ❌ Wrong prepositions

---

#### **4. Pronunciation**
**"Can native speakers understand you easily?"**

| Band | Description |
|:---:|:---|
| **9** | Precise, effortless to understand |
| **7** | Easy to understand, effective use of intonation |
| **6** | Generally clear, occasional mispronunciation |
| **5** | Mispronunciation reduces clarity |

**What matters for Band 7.5:**
- ✅ **Intonation:** Voice goes up/down naturally (not monotone)
- ✅ **Word stress:** pho-**TO**-graphy (not **pho**-TO-gra-phy)
- ✅ **Sentence stress:** Emphasize important words
- ✅ **Connected speech:** Want to → Wanna (natural contractions)

**NOT required:**
- ❌ Perfect British/American accent (Any clear accent is fine!)
- ❌ Zero mistakes (Native speakers make mistakes too)

**Focus areas for Vietnamese learners:**
- /th/ sound (think, that)
- /l/ vs /n/ (night vs light)
- Ending consonants (walked = "walk-t", not "walk")

## 📈 Band Score Comparison (Writing Task 2)

### **Topic:** "Some people think technology makes life easier, others think it makes life more complicated. Discuss both views and give your opinion."

---

### **Band 6.0 Essay (Excerpt):**

> **Introduction:**
> "Nowadays, technology is very important in our life. Some people think it is good, but some people think it is bad. This essay will discuss both opinions.

**Problems:**
- ❌ TR: Vague thesis, no clear opinion
- ❌ CC: "Some people... some people..." repetitive
- ❌ LR: Simple vocabulary (good/bad)
- ❌ GRA: Simple sentences only

---

### **Band 7.5 Essay (Excerpt):**

> **Introduction:**
> "In the digital age, technology has become ubiquitous in our daily lives. While some argue that it simplifies routine tasks, others contend that it introduces unnecessary complexity. Although both perspectives have merit, I believe that the benefits of technological advancement outweigh its drawbacks, provided it is used judiciously."

**Strengths:**
- ✅ TR: Clear position stated ("I believe...")
- ✅ CC: Smooth flow, sophisticated linking ("While... others...")
- ✅ LR: Advanced vocab (ubiquitous, contend, judiciously)
- ✅ GRA: Complex structure (While X, Y, I believe Z)

## 🎯 Quick Checklist: Am I Ready for 7.5?

### **Writing:**
- [ ] I can write 270+ words in 40 minutes
- [ ] I use 3+ complex sentence structures per paragraph
- [ ] I know 50+ topic-specific vocabulary words
- [ ] I never start sentences with "And/But/So"
- [ ] I proofread and find 80%+ of my own mistakes

### **Speaking:**
- [ ] I can speak for 2 minutes without major pauses
- [ ] I use 5+ idioms/collocations naturally
- [ ] I can self-correct without panic
- [ ] Native speakers understand me 95%+ of the time
- [ ] I vary my intonation (not monotone)

---

## 💡 Final Tips

1. **Don't aim for Band 9** - Even native speakers make mistakes. 7.5 is realistic and impressive.
2. **Focus on your weakest criterion** - If GRA is 6.0 but others are 8.0, fix grammar first.
3. **Consistency matters** - One great paragraph + one weak paragraph = Band 6.5 average.
4. **Practice under pressure** - Do timed tests to simulate real exam conditions.

---

> **Remember:** Band scores measure **communication ability**, not perfection. You can make mistakes and still get 7.5+!
