# 📝 Two-Part Question Essay: Tourism Impact

> [← Back to Writing Samples Index](./README.md)

## 📌 Question:

**"International tourism has become increasingly popular in recent decades. What are the advantages and disadvantages of this trend for local communities?"**

**Essay Type:** Two-Part Question (Advantages + Disadvantages)
**Recommended Time:** 40 minutes
**Word Count Target:** 270-290 words

---

## ❌ Band 6.0 Sample (Common Problems Highlighted)

Tourism is very popular now. **[Weak opening - states obvious]** Many people travel to other countries. **[Irrelevant - focus should be on LOCAL COMMUNITIES]** This essay will talk about good and bad things. **[Mechanical - no sophistication]**

There are many advantages of tourism. **[Weak topic sentence - too vague]** First, it brings money. **[Too simple]** Tourists spend money in shops and restaurants. **[Undeveloped]** This helps local people. **[How? Missing explanation]** Also, tourism creates jobs. **[New point without development]** People can work in hotels. **[Example too simple]**

However, tourism also has disadvantages. **[Weak topic sentence]** Sometimes there are too many tourists. **[Vague]** This makes problems. **[What problems?]** Beaches get dirty. **[One simple example]** Local people don't like it. **[Who? Why?]** Prices go up. **[Undeveloped]** Everything becomes expensive. **[Repetitive]**

In conclusion, tourism has good and bad points. **[Repetitive of intro]** Local communities need to manage it carefully. **[Vague - how to manage?]** They should keep the benefits and reduce the problems. **[Obvious statement]**

**(137 words - too short!)**

## ✅ Band 8.0-9.0 Sample (With Annotations)

**[Introduction - 35 words]**

The **exponential growth** [Formal phrase - "rapid increase"] of international tourism has **profoundly transformed** [Strong collocation - "greatly changed"] countless **destinations worldwide** [Formal phrase]. While this **phenomenon** [Formal noun - "trend"] **yields substantial economic benefits** [Formal phrase - "brings significant financial gains"] for **host communities** [Formal - "local areas"], it **simultaneously engenders** [Formal verb - "also creates"] **significant social and environmental challenges** [Academic phrase - "serious problems"].

---

**[Body Paragraph 1 - Advantages - PEER - 105 words]**

**[P]** **Economically** [Topic sentence start - focus area], tourism **serves as a vital revenue stream** [Formal phrase - "provides important income"] for local communities, **generating employment** [Formal - "creating jobs"] and **stimulating infrastructure development** [Formal phrase - "encouraging building projects"]. **[E]** **Tourist expenditure** [Formal - "money tourists spend"] **directly benefits** [Formal phrase] local businesses, from **hospitality establishments** [Formal - "hotels/restaurants"] to **artisan shops** [Specific example - craft stores], while **indirectly supporting** [Formal phrase] **ancillary services** [Formal - "related businesses"] such as transportation and **food supply chains** [Specific term]. **[E]** **For instance** [Formal linker], in **Bali, Indonesia** [Specific location], tourism **accounts for** [Formal phrase - "makes up"] approximately 80% of the **regional economy** [Formal term], **lifting thousands** [Specific impact] from poverty and **funding** [Formal verb] **essential public services** [Formal phrase - "important government services"]. **[R]** **Moreover** [Formal linker], **increased visitor numbers** [Formal phrase - "more tourists"] often **catalyze** [Strong verb - "cause/trigger"] **improvements in** [Formal phrase] **public infrastructure** [Formal - "roads, utilities, etc.], **benefiting** [Participle - showing result] **residents and tourists alike** [Formal phrase - "both groups"].

---

**[Body Paragraph 2 - Disadvantages - PEER - 110 words]**

**[P]** **Conversely** [Formal linker - transition to disadvantages], **unchecked tourism** [Specific phrase - "uncontrolled tourism"] can **inflict considerable damage** [Strong phrase - "cause serious harm"] on local communities through **environmental degradation** [Formal - "damage to nature"] and **cultural erosion** [Formal - "loss of culture"]. **[E]** **Mass tourism** [Specific term] **places immense strain on** [Formal phrase - "puts great pressure on"] **natural resources** [Formal term], **leading to** [Cause-effect phrase] **habitat destruction** [Specific impact], **water scarcity** [Specific problem], and **pollution** [Specific issue], while **over-commercialization** [Formal - "too much business focus"] can **dilute** [Formal verb - "weaken"] **authentic cultural practices** [Formal phrase - "traditional customs"]. **[E]** **Venice, Italy** [Specific example], **exemplifies** [Formal verb - "shows"] this predicament, where **an influx of** [Formal phrase - "large number of"] **30 million annual visitors** [Specific data] has **precipitated** [Formal verb - "caused"] **severe overtourism** [Specific term], **resulting in** [Result phrase] **residential displacement** [Formal - "locals forced to move"], **environmental decay** [Formal - "nature damage"], and **the commodification of** [Formal phrase - "turning into a product"] **local heritage** [Formal - "cultural traditions"]. **[R]** **Additionally** [Formal linker], **seasonal tourism** [Specific type] often **creates** [Verb] **employment instability** [Formal - "unreliable jobs"] and **inflates** [Formal verb - "increases"] **living costs** [Formal - "prices"], **disadvantaging** [Participle - showing negative effect] **permanent residents** [Formal - "people who live there always"].

---

**[Conclusion - 45 words]**

**In conclusion** [Standard linker], while international tourism **undeniably provides** [Strong phrase] **crucial economic advantages** [Formal phrase] to local communities, its **adverse impacts** [Formal - "negative effects"] on **environmental sustainability** [Formal phrase] and **cultural integrity** [Formal phrase] **necessitate** [Formal verb - "require"] **thoughtful regulation** [Formal phrase - "careful control"] and **sustainable tourism practices** [Specific solution] to **ensure long-term community wellbeing** [Formal phrase - "protect local quality of life"].

**(295 words)**

## 🏗️ Two-Part Question Structure

### **Introduction (1-2 sentences):**
State the topic and acknowledge BOTH parts you'll address.

**Template:**
"[Topic] has [grown/changed/become]. While this [yields/brings] [Part 1 - advantages], it [also creates/engenders] [Part 2 - disadvantages]."

### **Body Paragraph 1 (Advantages - PEER):**
- **P:** Topic sentence focusing on positive aspects
- **E:** Explain how/why it benefits local communities
- **E:** Specific example with data/location
- **R:** Result/further benefit

**Key:** Use formal linking for advantages: "Economically", "Moreover", "Furthermore"

### **Body Paragraph 2 (Disadvantages - PEER):**
- **P:** Topic sentence focusing on negative aspects (start with "Conversely")
- **E:** Explain how/why it harms local communities
- **E:** Specific example with data/location
- **R:** Result/further disadvantage

**Key:** Use formal linking for disadvantages: "Conversely", "Additionally", "However"

### **Conclusion:**
- Acknowledge both parts briefly
- Suggest balance/solution if appropriate

**Template:**
"In conclusion, while [topic] provides [advantage], its [disadvantage] necessitate [solution/careful management]."

## ⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

### **1. Ignoring "Local Communities" Focus:**
- ❌ "Tourism lets people see new places." (Focus on tourists, not locals!)
- ✅ "Tourism serves as a vital revenue stream for local communities, generating employment..."

### **2. Listing Without Development:**
- ❌ "Advantages: money, jobs, culture. Disadvantages: pollution, crowds, noise."
- ✅ Each point needs: Topic sentence + Explanation + Example + Result

### **3. Unequal Treatment:**
- ❌ Advantages: 150 words, Disadvantages: 50 words
- ✅ Give EQUAL attention to both parts (100-110 words each)

### **4. No Specific Examples:**
- ❌ "Many places have problems with too many tourists."
- ✅ "Venice, Italy exemplifies this predicament, where an influx of 30 million annual visitors has precipitated severe overtourism..."

### **5. Vague Language:**
- ❌ "Tourism brings money and helps people."
- ✅ "In Bali, Indonesia, tourism accounts for approximately 80% of the regional economy, lifting thousands from poverty."

### **6. Too Short:**
- ❌ 150-200 words
- ✅ 270-290 words (well-developed)

### **7. Missing Conclusion:**
- ❌ Ending after Body Paragraph 2
- ✅ Always include a conclusion that references BOTH parts

### **Two-Part Question Formula:**

**Introduction:**
"[Topic] has [changed]. While this [benefit - Part 1], it [problem - Part 2]."

**Body 1 (Advantages):**
- "Economically/Socially, [topic] [benefit]..."
- Explain + Example (with data) + Result
- Use: Moreover, Furthermore, Additionally

**Body 2 (Disadvantages):**
- "Conversely, [topic] can [problem]..."
- Explain + Example (with data) + Result
- Use: Additionally, However, Furthermore

**Conclusion:**
- "In conclusion, while [Part 1], [Part 2] necessitate [solution/balance]."

## 🎯 Useful Phrases for Two-Part Questions

### **Introduction (Acknowledging Both Parts):**
- "While this yields [advantages], it simultaneously engenders [disadvantages]..."
- "[Topic] brings both [benefits] and [challenges]..."
- "This trend presents [opportunities] alongside [concerns]..."

### **Advantages Paragraph:**
- "Economically/Socially/Culturally, [topic] serves as..."
- "[Topic] provides substantial benefits, including..."
- "The positive impacts include..."
- "Moreover/Furthermore/Additionally..."

### **Disadvantages Paragraph:**
- "Conversely, [topic] can inflict considerable damage..."
- "However, this trend engenders significant challenges..."
- "The adverse impacts include..."
- "Additionally/Nevertheless/Nonetheless..."

### **Specific to Tourism:**
**Benefits:**
- "serves as a vital revenue stream"
- "generates employment"
- "stimulates infrastructure development"
- "catalyzes improvements in public services"
- "accounts for X% of the economy"

**Problems:**
- "places immense strain on natural resources"
- "leads to environmental degradation"
- "results in cultural erosion"
- "precipitates overtourism"
- "inflates living costs"
- "causes residential displacement"

### **Conclusion:**
- "While [topic] undeniably provides [benefit], its [problem] necessitate [solution]..."
- "The advantages must be balanced against the disadvantages..."
- "[Topic] requires thoughtful regulation to ensure..."
