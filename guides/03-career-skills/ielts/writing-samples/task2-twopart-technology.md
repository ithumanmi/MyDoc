# ✍️ IELTS Writing Task 2: Two-Part Question (Technology Impact)

> [← Back to Writing Samples](./README.md) | [Writing Mastery Guide](../writing-mastery.md)

**Essay Type:** Two-Part Question  
**Topic:** Technology & Society  
**Target Band:** 8.0-8.5  
**Word Count:** 294 words  
**Time:** 40 minutes

---

## 📝 The Question

**Technology has transformed the way people communicate and interact with each other. How has this change affected personal relationships? Do you think the advantages of this development outweigh the disadvantages?**

---

## 🎯 Essay Structure Analysis

This is a **Two-Part Question** requiring:
1. **Body 1:** Answer Question 1 - How has technology affected personal relationships?
2. **Body 2:** Answer Question 2 - Do advantages outweigh disadvantages? (Give your opinion)

**Strategy:** 
- Don't just list advantages/disadvantages generically
- Specifically address HOW relationships have changed
- Then evaluate whether these changes are overall positive or negative

---

## ✅ Band 8.0+ Model Answer

### **Introduction**

The digital revolution has fundamentally altered the landscape of human communication, with social media platforms and instant messaging applications becoming ubiquitous in daily life. **[Context]** This essay will explore how these technological advancements have reshaped interpersonal relationships and argue that, despite certain drawbacks, the benefits of enhanced connectivity significantly outweigh the potential disadvantages. **[Thesis - answers both parts]**

### **Body Paragraph 1: How Technology Has Affected Relationships**

Technology has profoundly transformed the nature and dynamics of personal relationships in several key ways. **[Topic sentence - directly answers Q1]** Firstly, geographical barriers have been virtually eliminated, enabling people to maintain meaningful connections across continents through video calls and social networking. **[Change 1 + Explanation]** For instance, expatriates can now participate in family gatherings remotely, witnessing important milestones despite physical distance. **[Example]** Secondly, the frequency and ease of communication have increased exponentially—friends can share daily experiences instantly through photos, messages, and status updates, creating a continuous sense of presence in each other's lives. **[Change 2]** However, this shift has also introduced superficiality into some interactions, with many relationships existing solely through digital interfaces rather than face-to-face contact. **[Balanced view - shows critical thinking]** Additionally, the phenomenon of "phubbing"—ignoring physical companions while absorbed in mobile devices—has emerged as a concerning trend that diminishes the quality of in-person interactions. **[Change 3 - negative aspect]**

### **Body Paragraph 2: Evaluation - Advantages Outweigh Disadvantages**

Despite these legitimate concerns, I firmly believe the advantages of technology-mediated communication outweigh the drawbacks. **[Clear position - answers Q2]** The primary benefit lies in the democratization of connection—technology has empowered individuals to build diverse social networks, access emotional support systems, and forge relationships that transcend traditional limitations of proximity and circumstance. **[Advantage 1 - expanded access]** Moreover, for individuals with social anxiety or physical disabilities, digital platforms provide invaluable opportunities to communicate comfortably and participate actively in social life. **[Advantage 2 - inclusivity]** While concerns about superficiality are valid, responsible usage and conscious effort to maintain depth in important relationships can mitigate these risks. **[Acknowledging counterargument + rebuttal]** The key lies not in rejecting technology but in cultivating digital literacy and intentional communication practices. **[Nuanced conclusion]**

### **Conclusion**

In conclusion, technology has revolutionized personal relationships by enabling unprecedented connectivity and transforming communication patterns, introducing both opportunities and challenges. **[Restate how relationships changed]** On balance, the capacity to maintain and nurture relationships across distances and circumstances represents a net positive for society, provided users remain mindful of preserving authenticity and depth in their interactions. **[Final judgment - advantages outweigh]**

**[Word count: 294]**

## 📊 Band 6.0 vs Band 8.0 Comparison

### **Introduction Comparison:**

| Aspect | Band 6.0 | Band 8.0+ |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **Opening** | "Nowadays, technology has changed how people talk to each other." | "The digital revolution has fundamentally altered the landscape of human communication..." |

| **Thesis** | "This essay will discuss how relationships changed and if it is good or bad." | "This essay will explore how these technological advancements have reshaped interpersonal relationships and argue that... the benefits significantly outweigh the disadvantages." |
| **Why better?** | ❌ Simplistic "good or bad", no clear position | ✅ Clear position stated upfront, sophisticated vocabulary ("explore", "reshaped", "significantly outweigh") |

| Aspect | Band 6.0 | Band 8.0+ |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **Topic sentence** | "Technology has changed relationships in many ways." | "Technology has profoundly transformed the nature and dynamics of personal relationships in several key ways." |
| **Why better?** | ❌ Repetitive "changed", vague "many ways" | ✅ Strong adverb "profoundly", specific terms "nature and dynamics", precise "several key ways" |
| **Example** | "For example, people can video call their family." | "For instance, expatriates can now participate in family gatherings remotely, witnessing important milestones despite physical distance." |
| **Why better?** | ❌ Basic, obvious statement | ✅ Specific subject ("expatriates"), vivid details ("witnessing milestones"), shows depth ("despite physical distance") |
| **Opinion** | "I think the good things are more than the bad things." | "Despite these legitimate concerns, I firmly believe the advantages... outweigh the drawbacks." |
| **Why better?** | ❌ Childish "good/bad things" | ✅ Acknowledges opposition ("legitimate concerns"), strong stance ("firmly believe"), formal vocabulary |
| **Critical thinking** | Lists only positives OR only negatives | "While concerns about superficiality are valid, responsible usage... can mitigate these risks." |

## 🎓 Grammar Structures Used (Band 8.0+)

### **1. Present Perfect (for changes up to now):**
- "...has fundamentally **altered** the landscape..."
- "Technology **has profoundly transformed** the nature..."
- "...the phenomenon of phubbing **has emerged** as..."

### **2. Passive Voice (for formality):**
- "Geographical barriers **have been virtually eliminated**..."
- "...relationships that **are built** across distances..."

### **3. Complex Sentences with Multiple Clauses:**
- "Despite certain drawbacks, the benefits of enhanced connectivity significantly outweigh the potential disadvantages." (concession + main clause)
- "For instance, expatriates can now participate in family gatherings remotely, witnessing important milestones despite physical distance." (main clause + participle + prepositional phrase)

### **4. Participle Clauses (conciseness):**
- "...**witnessing** important milestones despite physical distance."
- "...**creating** a continuous sense of presence in each other's lives."
- "...**enabling** people to maintain meaningful connections..."

### **5. Modal Verbs (for nuance):**
- "...friends **can** share daily experiences instantly..."
- "...responsible usage **can** mitigate these risks."
- "The key lies not in rejecting technology but in **cultivating** digital literacy..."

### **6. Relative Clauses (adding detail):**
- "...with many relationships **existing** solely through digital interfaces..."
- "...technology **has empowered** individuals to build diverse social networks..."
- "...relationships **that transcend** traditional limitations..."

### **7. Gerunds & Infinitives (variety):**
- "The key lies not in **rejecting** technology but in **cultivating** literacy..." (gerunds)
- "...empowered individuals **to build** diverse networks..." (infinitive)
- "**Witnessing** important milestones..." (gerund as participle)

### **8. Conditional Structures:**
- "...provided users remain mindful..." (conditional meaning: if/as long as)

### **9. Nominalization (advanced academic style):**
- "The **democratization** of connection" (verb "democratize" → noun)
- "The **frequency** and **ease** of communication" (adjectives → nouns)

## ✏️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

### **Structure Mistakes (Two-Part Essays):**

| ❌ Wrong Approach | ✅ Correct Approach | Why? |
|:---|:---|:---|
| Answering only one question | Answer BOTH questions fully | Task Response score = 5-6 if incomplete |
| One body paragraph for both | Separate paragraph for each question | Better organization, clarity |
| No clear opinion on Q2 | State opinion clearly ("I believe...", "On balance...") | Examiners need to see your position |
| Generic adv/disadv without addressing Q1 | First explain HOW changed, THEN evaluate | Shows you read the question carefully |

### **Grammar Mistakes:**

| ❌ Wrong | ✅ Correct | Why? |
|:---|:---|:---|
| "Technology make people communicate different" | "Technology has transformed how people communicate" | Present perfect for changes, word order |
| "People can talks to their family" | "People can talk to their families" | Modal + base verb, plural noun |
| "This is affect relationships" | "This affects relationships" | Subject-verb agreement, no "is" with present simple |
| "More easier to communicate" | "Easier to communicate / Much easier" | No "more" with -er comparatives |
| "Despite of the problems" | "Despite the problems" | "Despite" not "despite of" |

### **Vocabulary Mistakes:**

| ❌ Wrong | ✅ Correct | Why? |
|:---|:---|:---|
| "Technology is very very good" | "Technology offers significant advantages" | Avoid "very", use precise adjectives |
| "People are more closer" | "People feel more connected" | "More closer" is redundant |
| "Communicate with social media" | "Communicate through/via social media" | Correct preposition |
| "Make relationships with people" | "Build/Form/Forge relationships" | Natural collocations |
| "Bad effects on relationships" | "Negative/Adverse effects on relationships" | More academic vocabulary |

### **Content Mistakes:**

| ❌ Wrong | ✅ Correct | Why? |
|:---|:---|:---|
| Only listing advantages | Balanced view acknowledging both sides | Shows critical thinking for Band 8+ |
| No examples | Specific examples (expatriates, phubbing) | Supports arguments, shows knowledge |
| Personal anecdotes ("I use WhatsApp...") | General examples or research | Academic tone required |
| Memorized generic examples | Relevant, topic-specific examples | Demonstrates genuine understanding |

## 💭 Brainstorming: Two-Part Strategy

### **Question 1: How has technology affected relationships?**

**Positive changes:**
- Eliminated distance barriers (video calls, messaging)
- Increased frequency of communication
- Easier to maintain long-distance relationships
- Ability to reconnect with old friends (social media)
- Sharing life moments instantly (photos, stories)

**Negative changes:**
- Superficial interactions (likes instead of conversations)
- Phubbing (ignoring present people for phone)
- Reduced face-to-face quality time
- Misunderstandings (lack of tone in text)
- FOMO and social comparison

**Choose 2-3 from each side for balanced analysis**

### **Question 2: Do advantages outweigh disadvantages?**

**Position options:**
- **YES (chosen in model):** Overall positive because connectivity, inclusivity, opportunities
- **NO:** Overall negative because superficiality, addiction, isolation
- **DEPENDS:** On how we use technology (but this is harder to argue clearly)

**Recommendation:** Take a clear stance (YES or NO) rather than sitting on the fence!

## 📝 Alternative Essay Plans

### **Plan A (Used in model answer):**
- **Body 1:** How relationships changed (balanced: 2 positive + 2 negative changes)
- **Body 2:** Advantages outweigh (+ acknowledge counterarguments)

### **Plan B (Equally valid):**
- **Body 1:** Positive effects on relationships (3 ways technology improved connections)
- **Body 2:** Negative effects exist BUT advantages outweigh (evaluation with opinion)

### **Plan C (If you think disadvantages outweigh):**
- **Body 1:** How relationships changed (focus on negative: superficiality, isolation, addiction)
- **Body 2:** Why disadvantages outweigh (loss of authenticity, mental health issues)

**Key:** Choose a plan BEFORE you start writing. Don't change midway!

## ⏱️ Time Management (40 minutes)

- **5 mins:** Analyze question - identify BOTH parts, decide your position on Q2
- **5 mins:** Brainstorm & plan - list changes (Q1), decide if adv/disadv outweigh (Q2)
- **25 mins:** Write 
  - Intro (3 mins) - must mention both questions
  - Body 1 (10 mins) - answer Q1 thoroughly
  - Body 2 (10 mins) - answer Q2 with clear opinion
  - Conclusion (2 mins) - restate both answers
- **5 mins:** Proofread - check you answered BOTH questions, check grammar/spelling

**Warning:** The biggest mistake in Two-Part essays is forgetting to answer one of the questions!

## 📖 Examiner's Perspective

**What examiners LOVE to see:**
- ✅ Both questions answered in equal depth
- ✅ Clear, defensible position on evaluative question
- ✅ Balanced analysis (not one-sided propaganda)
- ✅ Specific, relevant examples
- ✅ Sophisticated vocabulary and grammar
- ✅ Acknowledgment of counterarguments

**What causes score deductions:**
- ❌ Only answering one question (automatic TR = 5-6)
- ❌ No clear opinion on the evaluative part
- ❌ Completely one-sided (no acknowledgment of other view)
- ❌ Generic statements without examples
- ❌ Memorized essays that don't fit the question
- ❌ Wrong essay type (e.g., treating as Opinion essay)

**Insider tip:** Examiners check if you read the question carefully. Using key words from the question (e.g., "interpersonal relationships", "personal relationships") shows you're on track!
