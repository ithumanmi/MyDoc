# 📝 Opinion Essay: Job Satisfaction vs High Salary

> [← Back to Writing Samples Index](./README.md)

## 📌 Question:

**"Some people believe that job satisfaction is more important than job security, while others think people should prioritize a permanent job over an enjoyable one. Discuss both views and give your own opinion."**

**Essay Type:** Opinion (Discuss Both Views + Opinion)
**Recommended Time:** 40 minutes
**Word Count Target:** 270-290 words

---

## ❌ Band 6.0 Sample (Common Problems)

Choosing a job is very important. **[Weak opening - states obvious]** Some people want a job they like, others want a stable job. **[Oversimplified paraphrase]** I will talk about both sides. **[Mechanical announcement]**

Job satisfaction is important because people spend a lot of time working. **[Weak topic sentence]** If they don't like their job, they will be unhappy. **[Too simple]** Happy workers work better. **[Vague]** For example, my cousin loves his job so he works hard. **[Weak personal example]** This is good for companies too. **[Undeveloped]**

However, job security is also important. **[Weak topic sentence]** People need money to live. **[Obvious]** If they lose their job, they have problems. **[Too simple]** Permanent jobs give regular salary. **[Undeveloped]** This helps people plan their future. **[Vague]** Many people have families to support. **[List without development]**

In my opinion, both are necessary. **[Fence-sitting]** People should find a job that is stable and enjoyable. **[Too idealistic]** But this is difficult to find. **[Weak ending]**

**(142 words - too short!)**

## 🏗️ Opinion Essay Structure (Strong Position)

### **Introduction (2 sentences):**
Acknowledge debate, state YOUR clear position.

**Template:**
"[Issue] has generated debate. While some [View A], others [View B]. I contend that [Your Strong Position - clearly favoring one side]."

### **Body Paragraph 1 (Opposing View - Acknowledge but Brief):**
Start with "Proponents of..." to show you understand the other side.
- **P:** "Proponents of [opposing view] rightly emphasize..."
- **E:** Explain why this makes some sense
- **E:** Give specific example/data
- **R:** Acknowledge validity

**Key:** Keep this shorter (90-95 words) - it's NOT your main argument!

### **Body Paragraph 2 (Your View - Develop Fully):**
Start with "Nevertheless" to show your stronger position.
- **P:** "Nevertheless, I maintain that [your view] proves more..."
- **E:** Explain your reasoning (STRONGER than paragraph 1)
- **E:** Give specific example/data (MORE impressive)
- **R:** Strong conclusion (More critically, ...)

**Key:** This should be longer (100-110 words) - your MAIN argument!

### **Conclusion:**
Restate your position forcefully.

**Template:**
"In conclusion, while [other view] addresses [some benefit], [your view] yields superior outcomes through [reasons], rendering it the more prudent choice."

## ⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

### **1. Fence-Sitting:**
- ❌ "Both are equally important and people need both."
- ✅ "While security addresses immediate needs, prioritizing satisfaction yields superior long-term outcomes."

### **2. Equal Weight to Both:**
- ❌ Body 1: 100 words, Body 2: 100 words (looks indecisive!)
- ✅ Body 1: 90 words (concession), Body 2: 105 words (your strong view)

### **3. Personal Examples Only:**
- ❌ "My cousin loves his job and works hard."
- ✅ "A longitudinal study tracked 5,000 professionals over 15 years, finding 43% higher earnings."

### **4. Vague Claims:**
- ❌ "Happy workers work better."
- ✅ "Professionals engaged in fulfilling work demonstrate enhanced productivity, greater innovation, and superior performance."

### **5. Weak Conclusion:**
- ❌ "Both are necessary and people should try to find both."
- ✅ "Prioritizing job satisfaction yields superior long-term outcomes, rendering it the more prudent choice."

### **Career Essay Formula:**

**Introduction:**
"[Issue] has generated debate. While some [View A], others [View B]. I contend that [Your Strong Position]."

**Body 1 (Concession - 90 words):**
"Proponents of [opposing view] rightly emphasize..."
- Acknowledge validity
- Give some evidence
- Keep brief!

**Body 2 (Your Main Argument - 105 words):**
"Nevertheless, I maintain that [your view]..."
- Strong reasoning
- BETTER evidence (15-year study, 43% data)
- More critically, [final strong point]

**Conclusion:**
"While [other view] addresses [X], [your view] yields superior outcomes through [reasons], rendering it the more prudent choice."
