# 📝 Opinion Essay: Environmental Protection Responsibility

> [← Back to Writing Samples Index](./README.md)

## 📌 Question:

**"Some people believe that environmental protection is the responsibility of politicians, not individuals, as individuals can do too little. To what extent do you agree or disagree?"**

**Essay Type:** Opinion (Agree/Disagree - Partially Agree)
**Recommended Time:** 40 minutes
**Word Count Target:** 270-290 words

---

## ❌ Band 6.0 Sample (Common Problems)

Environmental problems are very serious today. **[Weak opening - states obvious]** Some people think only politicians can solve them. **[Oversimplified paraphrase]** I disagree with this idea. **[Too direct - no sophistication]**

Politicians can do many things to protect environment. **[Weak topic sentence + grammar error: "the environment"]** They can make laws. **[Too simple]** For example, they can ban plastic bags. **[Undeveloped]** Also, they can build parks. **[New point without development]** This is important because they have power. **[Vague reasoning]**

However, individuals can also help. **[Weak topic sentence]** People can recycle. **[Too simple]** They can use less water and electricity. **[List without development]** If everyone does these things, it will be good for environment. **[Grammar + vague]** For example, my family recycles every day. **[Personal anecdote - not strong enough]**

In my opinion, both politicians and individuals are responsible. **[Better position but late]** They should work together. **[Too general]** If they do this, we can solve environmental problems. **[Overly optimistic + vague]**

**(145 words - too short!)**

## 🏗️ Opinion Essay Structure (Partial Agreement)

### **Introduction:**
State both sides briefly, then your position clearly.

**Template:**
"While some argue [View A], I fundamentally disagree/partially agree. On the contrary/However, I contend that [Your Position]."

### **Body Paragraph 1 (Concession - Acknowledge Other View):**
Start with "Admittedly" to show you understand the other side.
- **P:** "Admittedly, [other view] has merit because..."
- **E:** Explain why this view makes some sense
- **E:** Give specific example
- **R:** Result/significance

**Key:** Don't spend too much time here - this isn't your main argument!

### **Body Paragraph 2 (Your Main Position - Stronger):**
Start with strong transition: "Nevertheless", "However"
- **P:** "Nevertheless, [your position] because..."
- **E:** Explain your reasoning (should be stronger than paragraph 1)
- **E:** Give specific example with data
- **R:** Result/significance

**Key:** This should be longer and more developed than paragraph 1!

### **Conclusion:**
Restate position clearly, summarize briefly.

**Template:**
"In conclusion, while [acknowledge other view], [restate your position strongly]."

## ⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

### **1. Unclear Position:**
- ❌ "Both views have merit." (Fence-sitting!)
- ✅ "While governments play a role, individual action is equally crucial."

### **2. Equal Weight to Both Views:**
- ❌ Body 1: 100 words, Body 2: 100 words (looks like discussion essay!)
- ✅ Body 1 (concession): 80-90 words, Body 2 (your view): 100-110 words

### **3. Personal Examples Only:**
- ❌ "My family recycles every day."
- ✅ "Research published in Environmental Research Letters indicates that..."

### **4. No Specific Data:**
- ❌ "Many people doing small things helps a lot."
- ✅ "10% of population reducing meat by 50% = 360M tons CO2 reduction annually."

### **5. Weak Conclusion:**
- ❌ "In conclusion, I think both are important."
- ✅ "In conclusion, while governmental frameworks prove essential, characterizing individual action as inconsequential is profoundly mistaken."

## 🎯 Useful Phrases for Opinion Essays

### **Strong Position Markers:**
- "I fundamentally disagree with..."
- "I wholeheartedly agree that..."
- "I partially agree that... however..."
- "While I concede that X, I contend that Y..."

### **Concession (Acknowledging Other View):**
- "Admittedly, [other view] has merit..."
- "It is true that [other view]..."
- "I acknowledge that [other view]..."
- "To some extent, [other view]..."

### **Transitioning to Your View:**
- "Nevertheless, this view overlooks..."
- "However, I would argue that..."
- "On the contrary, evidence suggests..."
- "More critically, [your point]..."

### **Strengthening Your Argument:**
- "Research indicates that..."
- "Studies published in [journal] demonstrate..."
- "Data from [source] reveals..."
- "More critically/importantly..."

### **Conclusion:**
- "In conclusion, while [concession], [your position] proves more compelling..."
- "Ultimately, despite [other view], [your view] remains essential..."
- "While [acknowledge other side], I maintain that [your position]..."

---

> **Pro Tip:** In partial agreement essays, use the "Concession + Counter" strategy. Acknowledge the opposing view has SOME merit (Body 1), but then present a STRONGER counterargument (Body 2). This shows sophisticated thinking and avoids fence-sitting!
