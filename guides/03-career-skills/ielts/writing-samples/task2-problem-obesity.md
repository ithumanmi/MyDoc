# 📝 Problem-Solution Essay: Obesity Crisis

> [← Back to Writing Samples Index](./README.md)

## 📌 Question:

**"In many countries, the number of people suffering from obesity is increasing. What are the main causes of this problem? What measures could be taken to solve it?"**

**Essay Type:** Problem-Solution (Two-Part Question)
**Recommended Time:** 40 minutes
**Word Count Target:** 270-290 words

---

## ❌ Band 6.0 Sample (Common Problems)

Obesity is a big problem today. **[Weak opening - states obvious]** Many people are getting fat. **[Informal language]** This essay will talk about reasons and solutions. **[Mechanical announcement]**

There are many causes of obesity. **[Weak topic sentence]** People eat too much fast food. **[Too simple]** McDonald's and KFC are everywhere. **[Undeveloped]** Also, people don't exercise. **[List without development]** They sit all day watching TV. **[Too simple]** This makes them fat. **[Informal]**

To solve this problem, there are some ways. **[Weak topic sentence]** Government should ban fast food. **[Unrealistic]** Schools should teach children about healthy eating. **[Undeveloped]** People should exercise more. **[Too vague]** If everyone does this, obesity will decrease. **[Overly optimistic]**

In conclusion, obesity has many causes and needs many solutions. **[Repetitive]** Everyone should work together to fix it. **[Vague ending]**

**(130 words - too short!)**

## 🏗️ Problem-Solution Essay Structure

### **Introduction (1-2 sentences):**
State problem + announce structure.

**Template:**
"[Problem] poses a critical challenge, necessitating examination of underlying causes and viable solutions. This essay will analyze principal factors and propose multi-level interventions."

### **Body Paragraph 1 - Causes (PEER):**
Identify 2-3 main causes with evidence.
- **P:** "The [problem] stems primarily from [cause 1] and [cause 2]."
- **E:** Explain first cause with details
- **E:** Evidence/data (WHO reports 46% increase)
- **E:** Explain second cause
- **R:** How causes relate/compound

**Key:** Focus on ROOT causes, not symptoms!

### **Body Paragraph 2 - Solutions (PEER):**
Provide multi-level solutions matching causes.
- **P:** "Addressing this requires interventions at [gov/school/individual] levels."
- **E:** Government solutions with specifics
- **E:** Example: Mexico tax (12% reduction)
- **E:** School + individual solutions
- **R:** "Ultimately" - why all levels needed

**Key:** Solutions should ADDRESS the causes from paragraph 1!

### **Conclusion:**
Summarize causes → solutions needed.

**Template:**
"[Problem] results from [causes], requiring comprehensive solutions spanning [gov policies], [education], and [individual changes] to effectively combat this crisis."

## ⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

### **1. Causes Don't Match Solutions:**
- ❌ Cause: "Fast food everywhere" → Solution: "People should exercise more"
- ✅ Cause: "Ultra-processed foods proliferation" → Solution: "Sugar taxes + subsidize produce"

### **2. Unrealistic Solutions:**
- ❌ "Government should ban all fast food."
- ✅ "Governments should implement sugar taxes and mandate nutritional labeling."

### **3. No Specific Data:**
- ❌ "Obesity is increasing a lot."
- ✅ "WHO reports global sugar consumption increased by 46% since 1980."

### **4. Vague Solutions:**
- ❌ "People should eat better and exercise more."
- ✅ "Individuals must cultivate mindful eating habits, engage in regular exercise averaging 10,000 steps daily, and prioritize preventive health measures."

### **5. Only Individual OR Only Government Solutions:**
- ❌ All solutions about what individuals should do
- ✅ Multi-level: Government (taxes), Schools (education), Individuals (behavior)
