# 🎲 Part 1 Question Generator

## 1. Topic Pools

1. Hometown & Accommodation
2. Work/Study
3. Daily Routine
4. Hobbies & Free time
5. Technology & Media
6. Food & Health
7. Travel & Holidays
8. Weather & Seasons

## 2. Randomization Method

- Bước 1: Chọn số từ 1-8 (dice/app random number).
- Bước 2: Chọn câu hỏi 1-3 trong chủ đề đó.

| Topic | Question 1 | Question 2 | Question 3 |
|-------|------------|------------|------------|
| Hometown | What do you like about your hometown? | Has it changed recently? | Would you like to live there forever? |
| Work/Study | Why did you choose your major/job? | Describe your typical day at work/school. | Do you prefer studying alone or with others? |
| Hobbies | What do you do in your free time? | How long have you had this hobby? | Would you like to try a new hobby in the future? |

*(Extend table to all topics)*

## 3. Practice Flow

1. Timer 4 phút → trả lời 10 câu (15-20s/câu).
2. Record audio → check filler, grammar.
3. Note câu trả lời yếu → paraphrase lại.

## 4. Variation Tips

- Swap tense: "What did you enjoy as a child?" vs "What will you try next year?"
- Use AI to generate follow-up questions based on answers.
