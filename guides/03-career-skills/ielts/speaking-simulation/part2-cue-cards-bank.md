# 🃏 Part 2 Cue Cards Bank

## 1. Practice Protocol

- Draw 1 cue card ngẫu nhiên (1-50).
- 1 phút chuẩn bị: ghi bullet PPF (Past, Present, Future).
- 2 phút nói liên tục, không dừng >2s.
- Ghi âm + chấm self-score (Fluency/Lexical/Grammar/Pronunciation).

## 2. Cue Card Samples

| # | Topic | Prompts |
|---|-------|---------|
| 1 | A book that changed you | When you read it, what it was about, why it impacted you |
| 2 | A memorable trip | Where you went, who you traveled with, what made it special |
| 3 | A useful app | What it does, how often you use it, why you recommend it |
| ... | ... | ... |
| 50 | A goal you achieved | What the goal was, challenges, how you felt |

*(Fill list 1-50 covering People, Places, Events, Objects, Experiences.)*

## 3. Timer Script

- 60s prep, 120s speaking.
- Sử dụng app Pomodoro / timer điện thoại.
