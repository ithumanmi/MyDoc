# 🌿 Environment Vocabulary – 100 Words

| # | Word/Phrase | Definition | Sample Sentence |
|---|-------------|------------|-----------------|
| 1 | biodiversity | variety of plant/animal life | "Protecting biodiversity ensures ecosystem stability." |
| 2 | carbon footprint | total greenhouse gases emitted | "Cities must reduce their carbon footprint via public transport." |
| 3 | renewable energy | energy from sources like wind/solar | "Investing in renewable energy curbs reliance on fossil fuels." |
| 4 | habitat loss | destruction of natural homes for species | "Urban sprawl accelerates habitat loss." |
| 5 | sustainable agriculture | farming that preserves resources | "Governments should subsidise sustainable agriculture." |
| 6 | water scarcity | lack of sufficient clean water | "Water scarcity pushes communities toward desalination." |
| 7 | reforestation | planting trees to restore forests | "Reforestation projects mitigate soil erosion." |
| 8 | waste segregation | separating trash into categories | "Waste segregation boosts recycling rates." |
| 9 | circular economy | reusing resources to minimise waste | "A circular economy keeps materials in use longer." |
| 10 | green infrastructure | eco-friendly urban design | "Green infrastructure reduces city heat islands." |
