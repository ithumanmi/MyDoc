# 🔗 Collocations by Band (6 → 8)

| Band | Collocation | Example Sentence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 6 | "big problem" | "Traffic congestion is a big problem." |
| 7 | "pressing issue" | "Traffic congestion is a pressing issue." |
| 8 | "systemic bottleneck" | "Traffic congestion has become a systemic bottleneck for cities." |

## Usage Tips

- Ghi chú collocation theo topic (environment, technology, education).
- Tạo bảng Band 6 → 8 tương tự cho 20 cụm khác.
- Luyện Speaking: trả lời 1 câu hỏi dùng ít nhất 2 collocation band 8.
