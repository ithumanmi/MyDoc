# 🏆 Grammar for Band 8

> Tăng “grammatical range & accuracy” bằng cấu trúc nâng cao: inversion, cleft, concession.

## 1. Inversion (Đảo ngữ)

| Pattern | Ví dụ | Ghi chú |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Negative Inversion | "Never **have citizens been** so aware of data privacy." | Dùng never/rarely/seldom/little |
| Conditional Inversion | "Had governments acted earlier, ..." | Thay if + Past Perfect |
| Emphatic So/Neither | "So profound **was the impact** that..." | Nhấn mạnh tính từ |

**Practice:** Viết 3 câu mở bài Task 2 dùng inversion để gây ấn tượng.

## 2. Cleft Sentences

| Type | Ví dụ | Ứng dụng |
| --- | --- | --- |
| It-cleft | "It is the lack of funding **that hampers** rural education." | Nhấn mạnh nguyên nhân |
| Wh-cleft | "What communities need most **is sustainable investment**." | Nêu giải pháp chính |
| All/Where/When cleft | "All I ask is that governments **prioritise** healthcare." | Dùng trong speaking để nhấn mạnh quan điểm |

**Checklist:** Cẩn thận tránh lặp cleft quá nhiều → khiến câu rườm rà.

## 3. Concession & Contrast Structures

- **Not only...but also...** → "Not only **does telemedicine** expand access, but it also **reduces costs**."
- **Adverbial concessive:** "Much as I appreciate private enterprise, regulation **remains essential**."
- **Despite/In spite of + V-ing/Noun** kết hợp clause mệnh đề chính.

## 4. Advanced Linking Devices

- **Beyond mere X, ...**
- **With + noun phrase**, clause ("With resources dwindling, policymakers must prioritise...")
- **So long as / Provided that ...**

## 5. Practice Pack

- [ ] **Writing Sprint:** Viết 2 mở bài Task 2 (opinion + discussion) dùng cleft + inversion.
- [ ] **Transformation:** Paraphrase 5 câu band 6 thành band 8 (ít nhất 2 câu dùng concessive).
- [ ] **Speaking Part 3 Lab:** Trả lời 3 câu hỏi sử dụng concession structure + follow-up.
- [ ] **Error Log:** Ghi chú lỗi mới phát sinh khi dùng cấu trúc khó.
- [ ] **Peer Review:** Chấm chéo 1 bài Task 2, đánh dấu nơi có thể chèn cấu trúc nâng cao hơn.
- [ ] **Micro Drill:** Viết 5 câu chủ đề quen thuộc (education, environment...) dùng "beyond mere...", "with + noun phrase" hoặc "so long as".

### 🔍 Sample Answers

- **Writing Sprint (Opinion):** *"It is the erosion of public trust that compels regulators to intervene, and never has the digital economy been under such scrutiny."*
- **Writing Sprint (Discussion):** *"It is collaborative governance that offers the most viable path, for not only do citizens expect transparency, but investors also demand accountability."*
- **Transformation Example:** Band 6 = "People should exercise more." → Band 8 = *"Were citizens to adopt even modest fitness routines, the burden on national healthcare systems would diminish markedly."*
- **Speaking Part 3 Sample:** *"Much as I value personal freedom, I believe stricter environmental policies remain essential, because only with enforceable standards can industries curb emissions."*
