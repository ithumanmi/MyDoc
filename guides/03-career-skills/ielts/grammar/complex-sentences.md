# 🧩 Complex Sentences Toolkit

> Cầu nối từ Band 6 → 8: kiểm soát relative clause, participle phrase và conditional nâng cao.

## 1. Relative Clauses

| Structure | Band 6.0 | Band 8.0 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Defining | "The man who is tall..." | "The man **who spearheads renewable projects**..." |
| Non-defining | Rarely used | "The city, **whose infrastructure has lagged for years**, is finally modernizing." |
| Possessive | "The woman whose car..." | "The museum, **whose collection spans centuries**, attracts scholars worldwide." |

**Tips:**
- Luôn xác định defining vs non-defining (dấu phẩy).
- Dùng mệnh đề rút gọn: "The report, **highlighting systemic issues**, prompted reforms."

## 2. Participle & Reduced Clauses

| Structure | Band 6.0 | Band 8.0 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Present Participle | "I saw a man. He was running." | "I saw a man **running frantically** toward the exit." |
| Past Participle | "The data was collected." | "Collected across three continents, the data **reveals subtle shifts** in consumer trust." |
| Absolute Phrase | "The meeting finished, we left." | "His confidence shattered, the candidate struggled to continue the interview." |

**Checklist:**
- Đảm bảo chủ ngữ câu chính = chủ ngữ rút gọn để tránh lỗi dangling participle.
- Dùng để gộp câu, giảm lặp “which/that”.

## 3. Conditional Sentences

| Type | Band 6.0 | Band 8.0 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Zero/First | "If I have time, I will study." | "If commuters **are given reliable public transport**, they willingly abandon private cars." |
| Second | "If I had time, I would study." | "Were public funds **allocated more transparently**, citizens would regain trust." |
| Inversion / Mixed | Hiếm | "Had policymakers acted sooner, environmental damage **could have been mitigated**." |

**Tips:**
- Kết hợp inversion để nâng band: "Were I to have more time, I would pursue a master’s degree abroad."
- Mixed conditional cho tình huống quá khứ ảnh hưởng hiện tại: "If governments **had invested** decades ago, cities **would be** less congested now."

## 4. Practice Routine

- [ ] Viết lại paragraph Task 2 dùng ít nhất 1 relative clause + 1 participle phrase.
- [ ] Tạo 3 câu điều kiện với inversion (Were/Had/Should).
- [ ] Speaking drill: trả lời cue card sử dụng clause non-defining để thêm chi tiết.
- [ ] Soát lỗi dấu phẩy và chủ ngữ rút gọn.

> 💡 Band 8+: xen kẽ mệnh đề rút gọn, clause non-defining và conditional đặc biệt để tăng “grammatical range and accuracy”.
