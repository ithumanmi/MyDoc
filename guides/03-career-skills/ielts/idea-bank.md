# 💡 IELTS Idea Bank: Never Run Out of Ideas

> [← Back to Roadmap](./roadmap-7.5.md)

Bí ý là nguyên nhân hàng đầu khiến điểm Writing/Speaking thấp. Dưới đây là kho ý tưởng cho 5 chủ đề phổ biến nhất.

---

## 1. Technology (Công nghệ)

### **Pros (Lợi ích)**
1.  **Efficiency:** Machines work faster and accurately than humans. (Vocabulary: *Automation, Productivity*).
2.  **Connectivity:** People can communicate instantly regardless of distance. (Vocabulary: *Global village, Instant messaging*).
3.  **Access to Information:** The internet is a vast library. (Vocabulary: *Wealth of knowledge, Online resources*).

### **Cons (Tác hại)**
1.  **Isolation:** People spend more time on screens than face-to-face. (Vocabulary: *Social alienation, Virtual interaction*).
2.  **Privacy:** Personal data can be stolen. (Vocabulary: *Cybercrime, Identity theft, Data breach*).
3.  **Sedentary Lifestyle:** Lack of physical activity due to screen time. (Vocabulary: *Obesity, Eye strain*).

---

## 2. Environment (Môi trường)

### **Problems**
1.  **Global Warming:** Rising temperatures due to greenhouse gases. (Vocabulary: *Carbon footprint, Emission*).
2.  **Pollution:** Air, water, and plastic pollution. (Vocabulary: *Contamination, Toxic waste*).
3.  **Deforestation:** Cutting down trees for agriculture. (Vocabulary: *Habitat loss, Biodiversity*).

### **Solutions**
1.  **Renewable Energy:** Solar, wind power. (Vocabulary: *Sustainable energy, Green technology*).
2.  **Government Regulations:** Tax carbon emissions, ban single-use plastic. (Vocabulary: *Impose tax, Legislation*).
3.  **Individual Responsibility:** Recycle, use public transport. (Vocabulary: *Eco-friendly habits*).

---

## 3. Education (Giáo dục)

### **Online Learning vs Classroom**
*   **Online Pros:** Flexible schedule, study from anywhere, cost-effective. (Vocabulary: *Self-paced learning, Remote access*).
*   **Online Cons:** Lack of social interaction, requires self-discipline. (Vocabulary: *Distraction, Lack of motivation*).

### **University vs Work Experience**
*   **University:** Theoretical knowledge, networking, higher salary potential. (Vocabulary: *Academic qualification, Soft skills*).
*   **Experience:** Practical skills, earning money immediately. (Vocabulary: *Hands-on experience, Real-world application*).

---

## 4. Health (Sức khỏe)

### **Obesity Causes**
1.  **Diet:** Fast food is cheap and convenient. (Vocabulary: *Processed food, High in sugar/fat*).
2.  **Lifestyle:** Sedentary jobs, lack of exercise. (Vocabulary: *Inactivity, Couch potato*).

### **Solutions**
1.  **Education:** Teach nutrition in schools. (Vocabulary: *Raise awareness, Healthy eating habits*).
2.  **Facilities:** Build more parks and gyms. (Vocabulary: *Recreational facilities*).
3.  **Tax:** Higher tax on sugary drinks. (Vocabulary: *Sugar tax, Deterrent*).

---

## 5. Crime (Tội phạm)

### **Causes**
1.  **Poverty:** Stealing to survive. (Vocabulary: *Financial hardship, Desperation*).
2.  **Lack of Education:** No job opportunities. (Vocabulary: *Unemployment, Social inequality*).

### **Punishment**
1.  **Prison:** Punish offenders, protect society. (Vocabulary: *Imprisonment, Deterrent*).
2.  **Rehabilitation:** Teach skills so they don't re-offend. (Vocabulary: *Reintegrate into society, Vocational training*).
