## Part 1: Introduction & Interview

**Q: What’s the weather like where you live?**
*   **Band 8.0:** It’s pretty humid most of the year with two main seasons—rainy and dry. We get sudden downpours in the afternoon, so I always carry a compact umbrella.

**Q: Which season do you like most?**
*   **Band 8.0:** I love the cool, breezy weeks right after the rainy season. The air feels fresh, and it’s perfect for morning runs.

**Q: Does the weather affect your mood?**
*   **Band 8.0:** Definitely. Gray, rainy days make me sluggish, while sunny mornings lift my spirits and push me to be productive.

## Part 2: Cue Card (Long Turn)

**Describe a memorable weather experience.**

You should say:
*   When and where it happened
*   What the weather was like
*   How you felt
*   And explain why it was memorable

### 💡 Idea & Structure (PPF Method)
*   **Past:** Unexpected snowstorm during a trip; flights delayed.
*   **Present:** Streets turned white; silence; sense of calm; unprepared clothing.
*   **Future:** Learned to check forecasts and pack layers; appreciate quiet beauty of snow.

### 🗣️ Sample Answer (Band 8.0+)
"A weather experience I’ll never forget is getting caught in a **snowstorm** during a trip to Seoul. I was there in early winter and didn’t expect heavy snow, so I packed lightly. Overnight, the whole city turned white. The usual traffic noise was replaced by an almost **eerie silence** because snow absorbs sound.

At first, I was anxious because flights were delayed, but then I felt strangely **calm**. I walked around in borrowed boots, taking photos of empty streets and warm lights shining through windows. It taught me to always **check the forecast** and pack layers. More importantly, it made me appreciate how weather can completely transform a city’s mood."

## Part 3: Discussion

**Q: How does extreme weather affect people’s lives?**
*   **Band 8.0:** It disrupts transport, damages infrastructure, and can endanger lives. Economically, it hits tourism and agriculture. Psychologically, prolonged heatwaves or gloomy days can affect mental health.

**Q: Should schools close during severe weather?**
*   **Band 8.0:** Safety should come first. If roads are unsafe or air quality is hazardous, temporary closure or online classes make sense. Clear communication is essential to avoid chaos.

**Q: How can cities adapt to climate change?**
*   **Band 8.0:** Invest in flood defenses, heat-resilient infrastructure, and more green spaces. Early-warning systems and public education also help communities respond quickly.
