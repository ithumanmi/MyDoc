# 🧭 Topic: Career Change & Future of Work

> [← Back to Samples Index](./README.md)

*(Khác với Work-Study: tập trung chuyển nghề, gig/remote/AI tương lai.)*

## Part 1: Introduction & Interview

**Q: Would you consider changing your career?**
*   **Band 8.0:** Yes. If my field becomes obsolete, I’d retrain in data or product roles. I see career paths as flexible, not fixed.

**Q: Do you prefer working remotely or in an office?**
*   **Band 8.0:** Hybrid. Office time is great for collaboration, but remote days give me focus and save commuting time.

**Q: What skills are important for future jobs?**
*   **Band 8.0:** Digital literacy, communication, and the ability to learn quickly. Soft skills matter because automation can’t replace human judgment.

---

## Part 2: Cue Card (Long Turn)

**Describe a time you considered switching careers.**

You should say:
*   When this happened
*   Why you considered it
*   What you did to prepare
*   And explain the outcome

### 💡 Idea & Structure (PPF Method)
*   **Past:** Thought about moving from operations to product management.
*   **Present:** Took online courses, shadowed a PM, built a small side project.
*   **Future:** Applied internally; even if not immediate, skills remain valuable.

### 🗣️ Sample Answer (Band 8.0+)
"Last year, I considered switching from **operations** to **product management**. I wanted to work closer to users and influence the product roadmap. To prepare, I enrolled in a short PM course, shadowed a senior PM at work, and built a simple app prototype with friends.

The transition isn’t instant, but those steps gave me **transferable skills** like prioritization and user research. I’ve applied for an internal rotation, and even if it takes time, the learning already benefits my current role. It taught me that career change is about **experiments**, not a single leap."

---

## Part 3: Discussion

**Q: How will automation and AI impact jobs?**
*   **Band 8.0:** They’ll replace routine tasks but create new roles in data, ethics, and human-centered design. Reskilling will be essential.

**Q: Should governments support people who need to reskill?**
*   **Band 8.0:** Yes. Subsidized training and career counseling can prevent long-term unemployment and keep the workforce adaptable.

**Q: Is job loyalty still important today?**
*   **Band 8.0:** Loyalty now means contributing value, not staying forever. People should be loyal to learning and impact, while companies should offer growth.

- **internal rotation / lateral move**
- **automation / AI adoption**
- **gig economy / remote-first**
- **product roadmap / prioritization**
- **human-centered design**
- **redundancy / obsolescence**
