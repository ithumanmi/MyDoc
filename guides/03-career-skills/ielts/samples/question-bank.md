## 🟢 Part 1: Personal & Familiar Topics

### **1. Basic Personal Info**

**Work/Study:**
- Q: Do you work or are you a student?
  - **Sample:** "I'm currently a **software developer** at a tech startup. I **graduated** two years ago and have been **working in this field** ever since."

- Q: Do you enjoy your job/studies?
  - **Sample:** "Yes, absolutely. What I love most is the **creative problem-solving** aspect. Every day brings **new challenges**, which keeps things **interesting and dynamic**."

**Hometown:**
- Q: Where is your hometown?
  - **Sample:** "I **hail from** Da Nang, a **coastal city** in central Vietnam. It's **renowned for** its **pristine beaches** and modern infrastructure."

- Q: Is it a good place for young people?
  - **Sample:** "Definitely. It **strikes a good balance** between **career opportunities** and **quality of life**. The **cost of living** is reasonable, and there's a **vibrant social scene**."

**Accommodation:**
- Q: Do you live in a house or a flat?
  - **Sample:** "I live in a **two-bedroom apartment** in the city center. It's quite **compact** but **conveniently located** near my workplace."

- Q: Which is your favorite room?
  - **Sample:** "I'd say my balcony. It **overlooks** a small park, so it's a **perfect spot** to unwind after work. I often have my morning coffee there."

### **2. Habits & Lifestyle**

**Daily Routine:**
- Q: What is your morning routine?
  - **Sample:** "I usually **wake up around 7**, do some **light stretching**, and have a quick breakfast. Then I **commute to work** by motorbike, which takes about 20 minutes."

- Q: Is your routine different on weekends?
  - **Sample:** "Yes, **quite drastically**. On weekends, I **sleep in** until around 9 or 10. I also **dedicate more time** to hobbies like reading or going to the gym."

**Sleep:**
- Q: How many hours do you sleep?
  - **Sample:** "I typically **get around 7 hours** on weekdays, though I'd prefer 8. On weekends, I **try to catch up** on sleep and might **sleep in** for 9 hours."

- Q: Do you think sleep is important?
  - **Sample:** "Absolutely. **Adequate sleep** is **crucial for** both **physical health** and **mental clarity**. I notice I'm much more **productive and focused** when I'm **well-rested**."

**Weekends:**
- Q: What do you usually do on weekends?
  - **Sample:** "It varies. Sometimes I prefer to **stay home and recharge**, watching movies or cooking. Other times, I'll **catch up with friends** over coffee or explore new restaurants."

### **3. Preferences**

**Music:**
- Q: What kind of music do you like?
  - **Sample:** "I'm **quite into** indie rock and electronic music. I find it **energizing** yet **relaxing** at the same time. Artists like Tame Impala are **my go-to**."

- Q: Have you ever been to a concert?
  - **Sample:** "Yes, several times. The most **memorable one** was a **live performance** by Coldplay two years ago. The **atmosphere was electric**, and the **crowd energy** was **incredible**."

**Movies:**
- Q: Do you prefer watching movies at home or in the cinema?
  - **Sample:** "I'd say the cinema. The **big screen** and **surround sound** create an **immersive experience** you can't **replicate at home**. Plus, it's a **nice way** to **disconnect from distractions**."

**Colors:**
- Q: What is your favorite color?
  - **Sample:** "I'm **drawn to** navy blue. It's **sophisticated** yet **calming**. I find it works well for both **formal and casual settings**."

- Q: What color would you never use in your room?
  - **Sample:** "Probably bright orange. It's **too stimulating** for a bedroom. I prefer **muted tones** that **promote relaxation**."

### **4. "Weird" Topics (De thuc te gan day)**
*   **Mirrors:** Do you like looking in the mirror? Do you buy mirrors? Do you think mirrors are a necessary decoration?
*   **Robots:** Are you interested in robots? Would you like a robot to help you at home? Do you think robots will replace humans?
*   **Sky:** Do you like looking at the sky? Is there a good place to see stars in your city? Do you prefer the sky in the morning or at night?

## 🟡 Part 2: Cue Cards Categories

### **1. People (Người)**
*   Describe a famous person you admire.
*   Describe a family member you get along well with.
*   Describe a teacher who influenced you.
*   Describe a person you met recently and want to know more about.

### **2. Places (Nơi chốn)**
*   Describe a city you would like to visit.
*   Describe a quiet place you like to go to.
*   Describe a historical building in your country.
*   Describe a cafe you often visit.

### **3. Objects (Đồ vật)**
*   Describe a piece of technology you use often (phone/laptop).
*   Describe a gift you received that you liked.
*   Describe an old object your family has kept for a long time.
*   Describe something you made by hand.

### **4. Experiences/Events (Sự kiện)**
*   Describe a time you were late.
*   Describe a happy memory from your childhood.
*   Describe a time you helped someone.
*   Describe a difficult decision you had to make.

## 🔴 Part 3: Abstract Discussion

### **1. Technology & Society**

**Q: How has technology changed the way people communicate?**
- **Sample:** "Technology has **revolutionized** communication in **countless ways**. We can now **stay connected** with people across the globe **instantaneously** through video calls and messaging apps. However, there's a **downside**—face-to-face interactions have **declined**, which **impacts** the **depth of relationships**. It's **more convenient** but perhaps **less meaningful**."

**Q: Do you think people are losing their social skills because of technology?**
- **Sample:** "To some extent, yes. Many young people feel **more comfortable** communicating **behind a screen** than **in person**. This can lead to **difficulties** in reading **body language** and **developing empathy**. That said, technology also **enables** introverts to **express themselves** more freely, so it's **a double-edged sword**."

**Q: Should children be allowed to use smartphones at school?**
- **Sample:** "I think it **depends on the context**. For **educational purposes**, smartphones can be **valuable tools** for **research and learning**. However, **unrestricted access** would likely lead to **distractions**. A **balanced approach** would be to allow **supervised use** for **specific tasks**."

### **2. Environment & Global Issues**

**Q: What are the biggest environmental problems in your country?**
- **Sample:** "The most **pressing issues** are **air pollution** and **plastic waste**. In major cities like Hanoi, the **air quality** is often **hazardous** due to **vehicle emissions** and **industrial activities**. Additionally, **single-use plastics** are still **widely used**, **contributing to** significant **environmental degradation**."

**Q: Do you think individuals can make a difference?**
- **Sample:** "Absolutely. While **systemic change** requires **government intervention**, **individual actions** are **equally important**. Simple habits like **reducing plastic use**, **recycling**, and **using public transport** can **collectively** have a **significant impact**. It's about **raising awareness** and **setting an example** for others."

**Q: Should governments punish companies that pollute?**
- **Sample:** "Definitely. **Stricter regulations** and **hefty fines** are **essential** to **hold corporations accountable**. Without **financial consequences**, companies have **little incentive** to **adopt eco-friendly practices**. However, governments should also **provide incentives** for **green initiatives** to **encourage** rather than just **punish**."

### **3. Education & Work**

**Q: Is university education necessary for a successful career?**
- **Sample:** "It **depends on the field**. For professions like medicine or engineering, a degree is **absolutely essential**. However, for **creative industries** or **tech startups**, **practical skills** and **hands-on experience** can be **just as valuable**. **Increasingly**, employers are **prioritizing** skills over credentials."

**Q: What skills will be most important in the future job market?**
- **Sample:** "I believe **adaptability** and **continuous learning** will be **crucial**. With **rapid technological advances**, the ability to **upskill** and **pivot** to new roles is **more valuable** than **specialized knowledge** alone. **Soft skills** like **critical thinking**, **creativity**, and **emotional intelligence** will also be **highly sought after**."

**Q: Do you think robots will replace teachers?**
- **Sample:** "**Partially**, but not **entirely**. AI can **handle** tasks like **grading** and **providing personalized lessons**, making education **more efficient**. However, teachers **play a crucial role** in **emotional support**, **mentorship**, and **inspiring** students—aspects that **technology cannot replicate**. The future likely **involves** a **hybrid approach**."
