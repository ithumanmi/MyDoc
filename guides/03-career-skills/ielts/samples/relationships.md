# 🤝 Topic: Relationships & Friendship

> [← Back to Samples Index](./README.md)

## Part 1: Introduction & Interview

**Q: Do you prefer a small circle of friends or a big one?**
*   **Band 8.0:** I prefer a small circle. It’s easier to maintain genuine conversations and support each other consistently.

**Q: How often do you meet your friends?**
*   **Band 8.0:** Weekly, either for coffee or a short workout. We also keep in touch daily through group chats.

**Q: What qualities do you value in a friend?**
*   **Band 8.0:** Honesty, reliability, and a sense of humor. I appreciate people who listen without judging.

---

## Part 2: Cue Card (Long Turn)

**Describe a close friend you trust.**

You should say:
*   Who this person is
*   How you met
*   Why you trust them
*   And explain how this friendship has influenced you

### 💡 Idea & Structure (PPF Method)
*   **Past:** Met during a group project; solved conflicts together.
*   **Present:** Share goals; hold each other accountable; communicate openly.
*   **Future:** Plan to start a side project; expect friendship to stay strong.

### 🗣️ Sample Answer (Band 8.0+)
"My closest friend is Minh, whom I met in university during a challenging group project. We had disagreements, but he was always willing to **listen** and find **middle ground**. That built my trust.

Nowadays, we keep each other **accountable** for fitness and career goals. He gives straightforward feedback without being harsh, which I value. Because of his support, I’ve become more **assertive** and better at **conflict resolution**. We’re even planning a small side project together, and I’m confident our friendship will remain solid because it’s built on **trust and transparency**."

## Part 3: Discussion

**Q: Is it harder to make friends as an adult?**
*   **Band 8.0:** Yes, because schedules are tight and people are more cautious. However, shared hobbies or professional communities can make it easier.

**Q: What is more important: family or friends?**
*   **Band 8.0:** They serve different roles. Family often provides unconditional support, while friends offer perspective and chosen companionship. A balanced support system needs both.

**Q: How does technology influence friendships?**
*   **Band 8.0:** It helps maintain long-distance friendships through calls and chats, but online-only connections can feel superficial. Depth still requires time and shared experiences.
