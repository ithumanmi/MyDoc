# 🌿 Topic: Nature & Wildlife

> [← Back to Samples Index](./README.md)

## Part 1: Introduction & Interview

**Q: Do you enjoy spending time in nature?**
*   **Band 8.0:** Absolutely. Hiking helps me unplug from screens and clear my mind.

**Q: Are there any parks or natural spots near your home?**
*   **Band 8.0:** There’s a botanical garden nearby. I go there for morning walks; it’s quiet and full of fresh air.

**Q: Have you ever seen wildlife up close?**
*   **Band 8.0:** Yes, I once saw fireflies in a rural area at night. It was magical and reminded me how fragile ecosystems are.

---

## Part 2: Cue Card (Long Turn)

**Describe a memorable experience in nature.**

You should say:
*   Where you were
*   What you saw or did
*   Who you were with
*   And explain why it was memorable

### 💡 Idea & Structure (PPF Method)
*   **Past:** Night hike; saw bioluminescent plankton/fireflies.
*   **Present:** Felt awe; realized need to protect habitats; technology-free moment.
*   **Future:** Plan to volunteer for a conservation program.

### 🗣️ Sample Answer (Band 8.0+)
"I once joined a night hike along a coastal trail and witnessed **bioluminescent plankton** glowing in the waves. We turned off our flashlights, and the shoreline lit up with tiny blue sparks. It felt surreal, almost like watching stars in the water.

The experience was memorable because it was so **unexpected** and fragile. Our guide explained that light pollution and water quality affect these organisms. Since then, I’ve been more conscious about reducing waste and hope to **volunteer** for a local conservation project. It reminded me that nature’s beauty depends on our choices."

---

## Part 3: Discussion

**Q: How important is it to protect wildlife?**
*   **Band 8.0:** It maintains biodiversity and ecosystem balance. Losing species can disrupt food chains and harm human livelihoods.

**Q: Should zoos exist?**
*   **Band 8.0:** Ethical, well-managed zoos can aid conservation and education, but they must prioritize animal welfare and naturalistic habitats.

**Q: How can individuals contribute to environmental protection?**
*   **Band 8.0:** Reduce plastic use, support sustainable products, volunteer for cleanups, and vote for policies that protect ecosystems.
