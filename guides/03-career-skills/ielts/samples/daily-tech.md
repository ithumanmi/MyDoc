## Part 1: Introduction & Interview

**Q: Which apps do you use most often?**
*   **Band 8.0:** Messaging and maps. I chat with colleagues all day and rely on navigation to avoid traffic. I also track my steps to make sure I move enough.

**Q: How has technology changed your daily routine?**
*   **Band 8.0:** It streamlined everything—online banking, grocery delivery, and digital calendars mean I waste less time on errands.

**Q: Do you think you spend too much time on your phone?**
*   **Band 8.0:** Probably. I set screen-time limits and keep my phone outside the bedroom to avoid doomscrolling at night.

## Part 2: Cue Card (Long Turn)

**Describe a useful app or device you use every day.**

You should say:
*   What it is
*   What you use it for
*   Why it is useful
*   And explain how it has changed your routine

### 💡 Idea & Structure (PPF Method)
*   **Past:** Struggled with time management.
*   **Present:** Use a task manager with reminders, recurring tasks, and calendar sync.
*   **Future:** Plan to automate more with voice commands and shortcuts.

### 🗣️ Sample Answer (Band 8.0+)
"The most useful app I rely on daily is a **task manager**. I used to forget deadlines and juggle too many things. Now I create **recurring tasks**, set reminders, and sync them with my calendar. It keeps me **accountable** and reduces mental clutter.

The app also has a **focus timer**, which helps me avoid distractions when I need deep work. Since adopting it, my mornings are more structured. I plan the day over coffee, then just follow the list. It has genuinely changed my routine from chaotic to **intentional**."

## Part 3: Discussion

**Q: How can people manage screen time effectively?**
*   **Band 8.0:** Use app limits, grayscale mode, and no-phone zones like the bedroom. Replace passive scrolling with purposeful tasks, like language practice.

**Q: Are smart homes a good idea?**
*   **Band 8.0:** They’re convenient—voice-controlled lights, smart thermostats—but raise privacy concerns. Users should balance convenience with data security.

**Q: Does technology make us more productive or more distracted?**
*   **Band 8.0:** Both. It accelerates work with automation and cloud tools, but notifications fragment attention. The outcome depends on self-discipline and good settings.
