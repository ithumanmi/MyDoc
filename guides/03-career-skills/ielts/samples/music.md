## Part 1: Introduction & Interview

**Q: Do you like listening to music?**
*   **Band 8.0:** Absolutely, I'm a huge music fan. I literally listen to music all day long, whether I'm commuting, working, or just chilling at home. It's an essential part of my life that helps me unwind and lift my spirits.

**Q: What kind of music do you like?**
*   **Band 8.0:** My taste is quite eclectic. I listen to everything from pop and R&B to classical and jazz. It really depends on my mood. If I need to focus, I'll put on some instrumental lo-fi beats, but if I'm working out, I prefer something upbeat like EDM or rock to get my adrenaline pumping.

**Q: Do you play any musical instruments?**
*   **Band 8.0:** I used to take piano lessons when I was a kid, but sadly, I gave it up when high school got too busy. I can still play a few simple tunes, but I'm definitely a bit rusty now. I've always wanted to pick up the guitar, though—maybe I'll give it a shot in the future.

**Q: Have you ever been to a concert?**
*   **Band 8.0:** Yes, I've been to a few. The most memorable one was seeing my favorite band, Coldplay, perform live a couple of years ago. The atmosphere was absolutely electric! There's something magical about singing along with thousands of other fans that you just can't replicate with headphones.

## Part 2: Cue Card (Long Turn)

**Describe a song that has a special meaning to you.**

You should say:
*   What song it is
*   When you first heard it
*   What the song is about
*   And explain why it is special to you.

### 💡 Idea & Structure (PPF Method)

*   **Past (Introduction):** I want to talk about "Imagine" by John Lennon. I first heard it in a history class back in secondary school when we were learning about the peace movement. It struck a chord with me immediately.
*   **Present (Description):** It's a timeless ballad with a simple melody but incredibly profound lyrics. It paints a picture of a world without borders, religions, or possessions—a utopia where everyone lives in peace. Even though it was released decades ago, its message is still chillingly relevant today given the conflicts we see around the world.
*   **Future (Conclusion):** Whenever I feel overwhelmed by the negativity in the news, I listen to this song to restore my faith in humanity. It reminds me that peace is possible if we all work towards it. It will always be on my playlist.

### 🗣️ Sample Answer (Band 8.0+)
"I'd like to talk about the song **'Imagine'** by the legendary John Lennon. It's arguably one of the most iconic songs of the 20th century.

I first **came across** this track when I was a teenager. My dad was playing his old vinyl records, and this song came on. I was immediately **captivated** by the piano intro—it was so simple yet so melancholic.

Lyrically, the song is a **peace anthem**. It asks the listener to imagine a world **void of** the things that divide us, like borders, religions, and material possessions. It envisions a global brotherhood of man.

The reason this song holds such a **special place in my heart** is its message of hope. In a world that is often **plagued by** conflict and division, 'Imagine' serves as a gentle reminder of our shared humanity. It's not just a song; it's a **manifesto for peace**. Whenever I'm feeling down or cynical about the state of the world, listening to Lennon's soothing voice helps me find a sense of inner calm. It's a **timeless masterpiece** that I think everyone should listen to at least once."

## Part 3: Discussion

**Q: Do you think music education is important for children?**
*   **Band 8.0:** Yes, unequivocally. Learning music has numerous cognitive benefits. It enhances memory, improves mathematical skills, and fosters creativity. Moreover, playing an instrument teaches children discipline and patience, as mastering a skill requires consistent practice. It's also a great outlet for self-expression.

**Q: How has the way we listen to music changed in recent years?**
*   **Band 8.0:** The shift has been drastic. In the past, we had to buy physical albums like CDs or cassettes. Nowadays, **streaming services** like Spotify and Apple Music dominate the industry. We have access to millions of songs at our fingertips instantly. While this convenience is fantastic, I do think we've lost the **sentimental value** of owning physical music collections.

**Q: Do you think traditional music will eventually disappear?**
*   **Band 8.0:** That's a valid concern, but I don't think it will vanish completely. While pop music is ubiquitous, there's a growing movement to **preserve cultural heritage**. Many young artists are now fusing traditional folk elements with modern beats to keep the genre alive and relevant. As long as there are people who value their roots, traditional music will continue to evolve and survive.
