## Part 1: Introduction & Interview

**Q: What is your favorite food?**
*   **Band 8.0:** Without a doubt, it's Pho, which is the national dish of Vietnam. It's a noodle soup consisting of a rich, savory broth, rice noodles, and tender slices of beef or chicken. I could literally eat it every single day without getting tired of it. It's my ultimate **comfort food**.

**Q: Do you know how to cook?**
*   **Band 8.0:** To some extent, yes. I'm not exactly a master chef, but I can **whip up** a decent meal. I usually stick to simple dishes like stir-fries or pasta. However, on weekends, I sometimes experiment with more complicated recipes just to challenge myself.

**Q: Do you prefer eating out or eating at home?**
*   **Band 8.0:** It really depends. Eating at home is definitely healthier and more **budget-friendly**, so that's my go-to for weekdays. But on special occasions, I love **dining out** at fancy restaurants to enjoy the ambiance and try exotic cuisines that I can't cook myself.

**Q: Is there any food you dislike?**
*   **Band 8.0:** I'm not a **picky eater** at all, but I can't stand durian. The smell is just too overpowering for me. I know it's considered a delicacy in Southeast Asia, but I just can't get past the pungent odor.

## Part 2: Cue Card (Long Turn)

**Describe a traditional meal that you enjoy.**

You should say:
*   What it is
*   What it tastes like
*   When you usually eat it
*   And explain why you like it.

### 💡 Idea & Structure (PPF Method)

*   **Past (Introduction):** I've been eating *Banh Chung* (Chung Cake) since I was a toddler. It's a staple during the Lunar New Year (Tet) in Vietnam. My grandmother used to make it from scratch, and the whole family would gather around to help.
*   **Present (Description):** It's a square-shaped cake made from glutinous rice, mung beans, and fatty pork, all wrapped in banana leaves and boiled for hours. The taste is a unique combination of the savory pork, the creamy beans, and the sticky texture of the rice. It's incredibly filling and rich.
*   **Future (Conclusion):** Even though it's high in calories, I can't imagine Tet without it. It symbolizes family reunion and gratitude to our ancestors. In the future, I hope to learn the secret recipe from my mom so I can pass this tradition down to my own children.

### 🗣️ Sample Answer (Band 8.0+)
"I'm going to describe **Banh Chung**, which is arguably the most **quintessential** traditional dish in Vietnam, especially during the Lunar New Year festival.

It's a savory rice cake that is square in shape, representing the Earth according to ancient Vietnamese folklore. The main ingredients are **glutinous rice**, mung bean paste, and seasoned pork belly. All of these are tightly wrapped in green leaves, usually banana or *dong* leaves, which give the cake its distinctive green color.

In terms of taste, it's quite unique. The rice is sticky and chewy, while the filling is soft and rich. The pork fat melts into the beans, creating a **mouth-watering** flavor profile. It's usually served with pickled onions to cut through the richness.

We typically eat this dish during the Tet holiday. In the past, families would gather to wrap and boil the cakes overnight, sharing stories by the fire. It's a very **nostalgic experience** for me.

I love Banh Chung not just because it's delicious, but because it embodies the **spirit of reunion**. It reminds me of my childhood and the warmth of family gatherings. It's more than just food; it's a piece of our **cultural heritage**."

## Part 3: Discussion

**Q: How have eating habits changed in your country?**
*   **Band 8.0:** There has been a significant shift towards **convenience foods**. With people leading increasingly busy lives, fast food and processed meals have become more popular. However, there's also a growing counter-movement of health-conscious individuals who are opting for organic and plant-based diets. So, we are seeing a polarization in dietary habits.

**Q: Do you think cooking is a skill that everyone should learn?**
*   **Band 8.0:** Absolutely. Knowing how to cook is a fundamental **life skill**. It allows you to be independent and take control of your nutrition. Relying solely on takeout is not only expensive but often detrimental to your health due to high levels of salt and sugar. Plus, cooking for others is a wonderful way to show you care.

**Q: What is the impact of importing food from other countries?**
*   **Band 8.0:** On the positive side, it gives consumers more variety. We can enjoy fruits and ingredients that aren't native to our region. However, the downside is the **carbon footprint** associated with transporting food across the globe. It also puts pressure on local farmers who might struggle to compete with cheaper imports. Therefore, I believe in supporting local produce whenever possible to promote sustainability.

## 📚 Essential Vocabulary for Food & Cuisine

### **Types of Food:**
- **Cuisine, dish, delicacy** - ẩm thực, món ăn, đặc sản
- **Comfort food, soul food** - món ăn yêu thích, thức ăn an ủi
- **Street food, fast food, junk food** - đồ ăn vỉa hè, thức ăn nhanh
- **Organic food, processed food** - thực phẩm hữu cơ, chế biến sẵn
- **Staple food, national dish** - món ăn chủ yếu, món quốc hồn

### **Taste & Flavor - Adjectives:**
- **Savory, sweet, sour, bitter, spicy** - mặn, ngọt, chua, đắng, cay
- **Delicious, mouth-watering, appetizing** - ngon, hấp dẫn
- **Bland, tasteless, flavorless** - nhạt nhẽo
- **Rich, creamy, hearty** - đậm đà, béo ngậy
- **Crispy, crunchy, tender, juicy** - giòn, mềm, mọng nước
- **Pungent, overpowering** - hăng, nồng gắt
- **Aromatic, fragrant** - thơm phức

### **Cooking Methods:**
- **Boil, steam, stir-fry, deep-fry** - luộc, hấp, xào, chiên
- **Grill, roast, bake** - nướng (than), nướng (lò)
- **Sauté, simmer, braise** - áp chảo, om, kho
- **Marinate, season** - ướp, nêm nếm

### **Food-Related Verbs:**
- **Whip up a meal** - nấu nhanh một món
- **Eat out, dine out** - ăn ngoài
- **Grab a bite** - ăn vội
- **Have a sweet tooth** - thích đồ ngọt
- **Be a picky eater** - kén ăn
- **Wolf down, devour** - ăn ngấu nghiến
- **Savor, relish** - thưởng thức

### **Collocations:**
- **National dish, signature dish** - món quốc hồn, món đặc trưng
- **Culinary skills, cooking prowess** - kỹ năng nấu ăn
- **Food coma** - buồn ngủ sau khi ăn no
- **Balanced diet, healthy eating** - chế độ ăn cân đối
- **Food poisoning** - ngộ độc thực phẩm
- **Acquired taste** - món ăn phải làm quen mới thích

### **Idioms & Expressions:**
- **A piece of cake** - dễ như ăn bánh (dễ dàng)
- **Bite off more than you can chew** - tham nhiều quá sức
- **Spill the beans** - tiết lộ bí mật
- **In a nutshell** - tóm lại
- **Food for thought** - điều đáng suy ngẫm
- **The icing on the cake** - điểm nhấn hoàn hảo
- **Bring home the bacon** - kiếm tiền nuôi gia đình

### **Food Trends & Issues:**
- **Plant-based diet, vegan, vegetarian** - ăn chay
- **Farm-to-table movement** - phong trào từ nông trại đến bàn ăn
- **Carbon footprint** - dấu chân carbon
- **Food waste, food security** - lãng phí thực phẩm, an ninh lương thực
- **Sustainable agriculture** - nông nghiệp bền vững
- **GMO (Genetically Modified Organism)** - biến đổi gen

### **Dining Experience:**
- **Ambiance, atmosphere** - không khí, bầu không khí
- **Fine dining, casual dining** - nhà hàng cao cấp, bình dân
- **All-you-can-eat buffet** - buffet không giới hạn
- **Reservation, booking** - đặt bàn
- **Exotic cuisine** - ẩm thực lạ
