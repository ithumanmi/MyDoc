## Part 1: Introduction & Interview

**Q: Do you play any sports regularly?**
*   **Band 8.0:** Yes, I play basketball twice a week at a local court. It keeps me fit and also lets me unwind after work. I enjoy the teamwork and the quick decision-making during the game.

**Q: What’s the most popular sport in your country?**
*   **Band 8.0:** Football is king here. You can see kids playing in the streets and adults crowding around cafés to watch international matches. It brings people together, especially during big tournaments.

**Q: Do you prefer watching sports or playing them?**
*   **Band 8.0:** I prefer playing because I like the adrenaline and the physical challenge. But I also watch professional matches to learn tactics and get inspired.

## Part 2: Cue Card (Long Turn)

**Describe a sport you would like to learn in the future.**

You should say:
*   What the sport is
*   Why you want to learn it
*   What equipment or skills are needed
*   And explain how you would start learning it

### 💡 Idea & Structure (PPF Method)

*   **Past (hook):** I’ve always been curious about tennis after watching Grand Slam finals.
*   **Present (description):** It requires good footwork, hand-eye coordination, and stamina. The equipment is simple—just a racket, balls, and proper shoes—but mastering technique is tough.
*   **Future (plan):** I’d start with beginner lessons, practice serves daily, and join a weekend club to play with better players.

### 🗣️ Sample Answer (Band 8.0+)
"I’d love to pick up **tennis**. I got hooked after watching the Wimbledon finals last year—the pace and precision are fascinating. Tennis looks straightforward in terms of gear—a decent racket, some balls, and non-marking shoes—but the technique is anything but simple. You need solid **footwork**, quick **reflexes**, and a strong **serve** to stay competitive.

If I were to start, I’d take a few **introductory lessons** to avoid developing bad habits. Then I’d practice **serves and volleys** for 20–30 minutes every day. On weekends, I’d join a local club to play with people who are slightly better than me. That way I can gradually raise my game. My ultimate goal is to play friendly matches without feeling out of breath or out of depth."

## Part 3: Discussion

**Q: Why is regular exercise important for people?**
*   **Band 8.0:** It improves cardiovascular health, boosts mood, and builds discipline. Regular workouts also reduce stress and prevent lifestyle diseases like obesity or hypertension. Beyond physical benefits, sports teach teamwork and resilience.

**Q: Should children be encouraged to compete in sports?**
*   **Band 8.0:** Yes, but in a healthy way. Competition can build confidence and goal-setting skills, but coaches and parents should emphasize enjoyment and fair play rather than winning at all costs.

**Q: How have modern technologies changed the way we do sports?**
*   **Band 8.0:** Wearable trackers and video analysis help athletes optimize performance and avoid injuries. Online coaching and VR training also make niche sports more accessible. However, over-reliance on data can overshadow intuition and enjoyment.
