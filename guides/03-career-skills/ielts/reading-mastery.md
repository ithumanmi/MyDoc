# 📖 IELTS Reading Mastery: Strategies for Band 8.0+

> [← Back to IELTS Guide](./README.md)

Reading là skill có **ROI cao nhất** - đầu tư thời gian ít nhưng cải thiện điểm nhanh. Không như Speaking cần phản xạ, Reading cho phép bạn suy nghĩ và áp dụng strategies.

**Key Insight:** IELTS Reading KHÔNG test khả năng đọc hiểu mọi từ. Nó test khả năng **scan thông tin nhanh** và **nhận diện paraphrasing**.

---

## 📊 Test Format Overview

**Total time:** 60 minutes (NO extra transfer time!)
**Total questions:** 40 questions across 3 passages
**Word count:** 2,000-2,750 words total

| Passage | Topic | Difficulty | Length | Recommended Time |
|:---:|:---|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| **1** | Social/General interest | ⭐ Easy | 700-900 words | 15-17 mins |
| **2** | Work-related | ⭐⭐ Medium | 750-950 words | 18-20 mins |
| **3** | Academic | ⭐⭐⭐ Hard | 850-1000 words | 20-22 mins |

**⏰ Time Buffer:** Keep 3-5 minutes at end to transfer answers!

**Band 7.5+ target:** 32-35+ correct answers

## 🎯 The 14 Question Types (Master These!)

### **Category A: Matching Information**

#### **Type 1: True/False/Not Given (T/F/NG)**
**Frequency:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ Very High
**Difficulty:** Hard (most people confuse False vs Not Given)

**The Golden Rules:**
- **TRUE:** Statement matches passage information (may use synonyms)
- **FALSE:** Statement contradicts passage information (opposite meaning)
- **NOT GIVEN:** Information is not mentioned at all in passage

**Strategy:**
1. **Underline keywords** in the statement
2. **Scan passage** for those keywords (or synonyms)
3. **Read carefully** that specific sentence/paragraph
4. **Apply logic:**
   - Can you find the information? → TRUE or FALSE
   - Can't find it at all? → NOT GIVEN

**Example 1 - FALSE:**
- **Statement:** "The company was founded in 1995."
- **Passage:** "Established in 1998, the company..."
- **Answer:** FALSE (1995 ≠ 1998, contradiction)

**Example 2 - NOT GIVEN:**
- **Statement:** "The CEO has a PhD in Economics."
- **Passage:** "The CEO graduated from Harvard Business School."
- **Answer:** NOT GIVEN (No mention of PhD or Economics degree)

**Example 3 - TRUE (with paraphrasing):**
- **Statement:** "Online shopping has become more popular."
- **Passage:** "E-commerce has seen a significant increase in recent years."
- **Answer:** TRUE (online shopping = e-commerce, more popular = increase)

**Common Trap:**
❌ Using your own knowledge → Only use passage information!

---

#### **Type 2: Yes/No/Not Given (Y/N/NG)**
**Frequency:** ⭐⭐⭐ Medium
**Same as T/F/NG but tests OPINIONS/CLAIMS, not facts**

**The Difference:**
- **Yes:** Writer's opinion **agrees** with statement
- **No:** Writer's opinion **disagrees** with statement  
- **Not Given:** Writer's opinion is **not stated**

**Look for opinion markers:**
- "I believe...", "In my view...", "It is argued that..."
- "Some experts claim...", "Critics suggest..."

---

#### **Type 3: Matching Headings**
**Frequency:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐ High
**Difficulty:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ HARDEST TYPE!

**Why it's hard:** 
- Headings use heavy paraphrasing
- Multiple headings seem similar
- Easy to overthink

**Strategy:**
1. **Read all headings first** (30 seconds skim)
2. **Identify keywords** in each heading
3. **Read first + last sentence** of each paragraph (topic sentence method)
4. **Focus on main idea**, not details
5. **Cross out used headings** (can't reuse)
6. **Do easy ones first**, leave tricky ones for last

**Example:**
- **Heading:** "The economic impact of tourism"
- **Paragraph:** "...visitors contribute millions to local economy... businesses thrive... job creation..."
- **Match!** (economic impact = contribute to economy, job creation)

**Common mistakes:**
- ❌ Matching based on 1-2 words appearing → Must match MAIN idea
- ❌ Overthinking → First instinct often correct

---

#### **Type 4: Matching Information to Paragraphs**
**Format:** "Which paragraph contains the following information?"

**Strategy:**
1. **Underline unique keywords** in the information statement
2. **Scan paragraphs** for those keywords/synonyms
3. **Can match same paragraph multiple times**

**Time-saver:** Do this question type LAST after reading the passage.

---

#### **Type 5: Matching Features**
**Format:** Match names/dates/places to features/descriptions

**Strategy:**
1. **Highlight all names/dates** in the passage (2 minutes scan)
2. **Read around each name** (1-2 sentences before/after)
3. **Match description** using paraphrasing

**Example:**
- **Question:** "Dr. Smith - A) discovered a new species B) published a book C) won an award"
- **Passage:** "Dr. Smith's recent publication on marine life..."
- **Answer:** B (publication = published a book)

---

#### **Type 6: Matching Sentence Endings**
**Format:** Complete sentences by choosing correct ending from list

**Strategy:**
1. **Focus on first half** of sentence (given part)
2. **Find that information** in passage
3. **Read next 1-2 sentences** to find logical conclusion
4. **Check grammar** - must fit naturally

---

### **Category B: Completion Tasks**

#### **Type 7: Summary/Note Completion**
**Frequency:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ Very High

**Two variations:**
- **A) Choose from word list** (Easier - no spelling errors)
- **B) Use words from passage** (Harder - must spell correctly)

**Strategy:**
1. **Read summary title** - which paragraph does it summarize?
2. **Check word limit** (e.g., "NO MORE THAN TWO WORDS")
3. **Predict word type** (noun? verb? adjective?)
4. **Scan passage** for that section
5. **Copy exact words** (don't change form unless necessary)

**Grammar check:**
- "The research was conducted in a _____ (adjective needed)" → "controlled environment" ✅
- NOT "control environment" ❌

---

#### **Type 8: Sentence Completion**
**Similar to summary but individual sentences**

**Strategy:**
- Questions follow passage order (usually)
- Answer must fit grammatically
- Use exact words from passage

---

#### **Type 9: Table/Flow Chart Completion**
**Frequency:** ⭐⭐⭐ Medium

**Strategy:**
1. **Understand the structure** (columns = categories)
2. **Follow logic flow** (if it's a process, follow steps)
3. **Fill in obvious ones first**
4. **Use surrounding info** to guess remaining

---

#### **Type 10: Diagram Label Completion**
**Frequency:** ⭐⭐ Low (mostly in Academic module)

**Strategy:**
1. **Identify what the diagram shows** (machine? process? building?)
2. **Find that paragraph** in passage (usually clear description)
3. **Match labels** to parts described
4. **Use technical terms** from passage (exact words)

---

### **Category C: Multiple Choice**

#### **Type 11: Multiple Choice (Single Answer)**
**Frequency:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐ High

**Strategy:**
1. **Read question stem carefully** (What is it asking?)
2. **Predict answer** before looking at options
3. **Eliminate obviously wrong options** (usually 2 are clearly wrong)
4. **Look for paraphrasing** between passage and correct option
5. **Beware distractors:**
   - Words from passage appear in wrong option (trap!)
   - Partial truths (some info correct, but incomplete)

**Example Trap:**
- **Question:** "What is the main purpose of the research?"
- **Passage:** "The research examined urban planning... ultimately to improve city life quality."
- **Option A:** "To examine urban planning" (❌ This is METHOD, not PURPOSE)
- **Option B:** "To improve quality of life" (✅ This is the PURPOSE)

---

#### **Type 12: Multiple Choice (Multiple Answers)**
**Format:** "Choose TWO/THREE letters A-F"

**Strategy:**
1. **Read all options first**
2. **Eliminate clearly wrong ones**
3. **Find evidence** in passage for each remaining option
4. **Don't choose more or fewer** than specified!

---

#### **Type 13: Choosing a Title**
**Format:** "Which is the best title for the passage?"

**Strategy:**
- **Do this LAST** after reading entire passage
- **Choose most general** option that covers ALL paragraphs
- **Avoid too specific** (only covers one section)
- **Avoid too vague** (could apply to any passage)

---

### **Category D: Short Answer Questions**

#### **Type 14: Short Answer Questions**
**Frequency:** ⭐⭐ Low-Medium

**Strategy:**
1. **Check word limit** strictly (ONE WORD? TWO WORDS?)
2. **Questions follow passage order** (usually)
3. **Answer must be** exact words from passage
4. **No articles** unless necessary ("the university" vs "university")

**Example:**
- **Question:** "What did the experiment measure? (ONE WORD)"
- **Passage:** "...the experiment measured temperature fluctuations..."
- **Answer:** "temperature" ✅ (NOT "temperature fluctuations" - that's 2 words)

## 🔥 Advanced Strategies

### **1. The Skimming Technique (3 minutes per passage)**

**Purpose:** Get big picture before attempting questions

**How to skim:**
1. **Read title** (5 seconds) - What is the topic?
2. **Read first paragraph** (30 seconds) - Introduction
3. **Read first sentence of each middle paragraph** (1 min) - Main ideas
4. **Read last paragraph** (30 seconds) - Conclusion
5. **Total:** ~3 minutes - Now you know structure!

---

### **2. The Scanning Technique**

**Purpose:** Locate specific information quickly (names, dates, numbers)

**How to scan:**
- **Let your eyes jump** across the page (don't read every word)
- **Look for capital letters** (names, places)
- **Look for numbers** (dates, statistics)
- **Look for keywords** from question

**Practice:** Try finding all years/dates in a passage in under 30 seconds!

---

### **3. The Paraphrasing Recognition**

**80% of IELTS Reading is recognizing paraphrasing!**

| Word/Phrase in Question | Word/Phrase in Passage |
|:---|:---|
| Children | Youngsters, Kids, Youth |
| Increase | Rise, Grow, Surge, Climb |
| Decrease | Decline, Fall, Drop, Reduce |
| Difficult | Challenging, Demanding, Tough |
| Important | Significant, Crucial, Vital |
| Old | Ancient, Elderly, Aged |
| Help | Assist, Aid, Support, Facilitate |
| Show | Demonstrate, Illustrate, Reveal |
| Many | Numerous, Various, Several |
| Often | Frequently, Commonly, Regularly |

**Practice:** After each passage, make your own paraphrasing table!

---

### **4. Time Management Strategy**

**DON'T spend equal time on all passages!**

**Recommended split:**
- Passage 1: 15-17 mins (Easier, fastest)
- Passage 2: 18-20 mins (Medium)
- Passage 3: 20-22 mins (Hardest, needs most time)
- Buffer: 3-5 mins (Check answers, fill guesses)

**If you're running out of time:**
- **Guess intelligently** (don't leave blanks - no negative marking!)
- **Prioritize completion-type** questions (easier to scan)
- **Skip matching headings** if stuck (most time-consuming)

## 🚫 Common Mistakes & Fixes

### **Mistake 1: Reading passage word-by-word**
❌ Problem: Too slow, you'll run out of time
✅ Solution: 
- Practice skimming (read groups of words, not individual words)
- Focus on topic sentences (first sentence of paragraph)

### **Mistake 2: Using outside knowledge**
❌ Problem: Answering based on what you know, not what passage says
✅ Solution:
- **ONLY use passage information**
- Even if you know the fact is wrong, use passage answer!

### **Mistake 3: Not checking word limits**
❌ Problem: Writing "the local government" when limit is TWO WORDS
✅ Solution:
- Highlight word limits BEFORE attempting questions
- "local government" = 2 words ✅
- "the local government" = 3 words ❌

### **Mistake 4: Spending too long on one question**
❌ Problem: Stuck on question #5 for 5 minutes → No time for rest
✅ Solution:
- **2-minute rule:** If stuck for 2 minutes, move on
- Circle and come back later with fresh eyes

### **Mistake 5: Not reading instructions**
❌ Problem: Question says "Choose TWO letters" but you choose three
✅ Solution: **Read. Every. Instruction. Carefully.**

## 📅 Practice Routine

### **Daily (30-40 mins):**
1. **Vocabulary building** (10 mins):
   - Learn 10-15 academic words
   - Focus on synonyms & paraphrasing

2. **One passage practice** (20 mins):
   - Do under timed conditions
   - Deep analysis: Why did I get wrong answers?

3. **Skimming drill** (5 mins):
   - Skim a news article
   - Summarize in 1 sentence

### **Weekly (2-3 hours):**
- **Full mock test** (60 mins, 3 passages)
- **Review mistakes** (1 hour deep analysis)
- **Paraphrasing practice** (Create synonym tables)

**Strategy in test room:**
- [ ] **Read instructions** for each question type
- [ ] **Skim passage first** (3 mins) before questions
- [ ] **Do easier question types first** (completion > matching headings)
- [ ] **Manage time strictly** (15-20-20 split + 5min buffer)
- [ ] **Transfer answers as you go** (no separate transfer time!)
- [ ] **Never leave blanks** - guess if unsure!
- [ ] **Check spelling** in last 3 minutes

**Question type priority (easy to hard):**

2. ✅ Summary completion (scanning)
3. ✅ Matching features (name-scanning)
4. ✅ Multiple choice (time-consuming but manageable)
5. ⚠️ Matching headings (HARDEST - do last!)
