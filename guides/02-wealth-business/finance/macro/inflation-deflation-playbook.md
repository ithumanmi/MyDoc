# 📈 Inflation-Deflation Playbook

> "Inflation is when you pay fifteen dollars for the ten-dollar haircut you used to get for five dollars." – Sam Ewing

Tài liệu này cung cấp khung hành động theo các kịch bản lạm phát/giảm phát/stagflation để đội đầu tư và doanh nghiệp điều chỉnh chiến lược.

---

## 1. Scenario Matrix

| Scenario | Asset Performance | Strategy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| High inflation | Commodities, TIPS, Real Estate ↑ | Tilt portfolio to real assets, inflation-linked cashflows |
| Deflation | Bonds ↑, Equities ↓ | Increase duration, nâng tỷ lệ tiền mặt, quality factor |
| Stagflation | Most assets ↓ | Vàng, defensive sectors, pricing power companies |

### 1.1 Signal Checklist
- CPI YoY, PCE Core YoY
- Breakeven inflation (5y5y)
- Commodity index (CRB), shipping rates
- Wage growth vs productivity

> Khi CPI > 4% và PMI < 50 → nghiêng về stagflation scenario.

---

## 2. Playbook by Scenario

### 2.1 High Inflation (>4% YoY, demand strong)
- **Asset Tilt:** Energy/Materials equities, REITs với hợp đồng thuê điều chỉnh CPI, commodities ETF.
- **Fixed Income:** TIPS ladder, floating-rate notes, shorten duration.
- **Corporate Actions:** Đẩy nhanh price pass-through, ký hợp đồng pricing indexation.
- **Retail Banking:** review savings rate, design inflation-linked deposits.

### 2.2 Deflation (CPI < 1%, growth thu hẹp)
- **Asset Tilt:** Long-duration sovereign bonds (UST 20y+), defensive growth (tech platform cash rich).
- **Cash management:** tăng tỷ lệ tiền mặt, chuẩn bị dry powder mua tài sản distressed.
- **Inventory strategy:** giảm hàng tồn kho, renegotiate lease.
- **Credit:** tránh high yield, ưu tiên IG quality, consider liability-driven investing.

### 2.3 Stagflation (Low/negative growth + high inflation)
- **Asset Tilt:** Gold, defensive sectors (Utilities, Staples, Healthcare), commodity trend following.
- **Currency:** ưu tiên USD, CHF.
- **Ops:** tối ưu chi phí năng lượng, hedge fuel/food price.
- **Consumer products:** đóng gói value, shrinkflation hợp lý.

## 3. Business Operator Checklist

| Function | Inflation Response | Deflation Response |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pricing | Implement automatic indexation, dynamic pricing engine | Promotions để giữ volume, nhưng bảo toàn margin |
| Procurement | Hedging commodity inputs, multi-sourcing | Đàm phán giảm giá, tận dụng deflation cost |
| HR | COLA (cost-of-living adjustment), bonus theo CPI | Freeze hiring, upskill nội bộ |
| Treasury | Lock fixed-rate debt trước khi lãi tăng | Refinance dài hạn khi yield thấp |

## 4. Tooling & Dashboard

- **Data feeds:** FRED CPI, BLS wage, IMF commodity.
- **Visualization:** Inflation heatmap (headline, core, trimmed mean), break-even curves.
- **Alerts:** Slack/Telegram khi CPI surprise > consensus ±0.3%, breakeven vượt 3%.

---

## 5. Case Notes

1. **Vietnam 2008 & 2022:** CPI > 20% (2008) và 4% (2022) → SBV siết tín dụng, doanh nghiệp chuyển sang pricing theo USD.
2. **Japan 1990s:** Deflation kéo dài, JGB yield xuống 0%, equity mất decades. Bài học: leverage thấp, focus cashflow.
3. **1970s US Stagflation:** Oil shock + wage spiral → equities sideways, commodities và gold outperformed.
