# 💱 Currency Dynamics & FX Playbook

> "Currencies are the equity of countries." – Ray Dalio

Thông tin này giúp theo dõi các driver chính của FX markets (carry, balance of payments, dollar liquidity) và cách hành động.

## 1. Dollar Regime Map

| Regime | DXY Direction | Typical Drivers | Asset Impact |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| USD Bull | ↑ | Fed hawkish, global growth yếu, risk-off, USD funding stress | EM stress, commodities ↓, US assets outperform |
| USD Neutral | ↔ | Policy sync, balanced growth | Range trading |
| USD Bear | ↓ | Fed dovish, twin deficits funded, global recovery | EM equities ↑, commodities ↑ |

### 1.1 Indicators
- DXY, Broad USD index
- Cross-currency basis (JPY/USD, EUR/USD)
- FX reserve changes (SAFE China, MAS)
- US current account & fiscal deficit

## 2. Carry vs Growth Framework

| Quadrant | Growth | Carry | Play |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| High Growth + High Carry | EM FX darling (BRL, MXN) | Long carry trades |
| High Growth + Low Carry | Equity appeal (KRW, SGD) | Use options to express |
| Low Growth + High Carry | Value trap (TRY) | Avoid unless hedged |
| Low Growth + Low Carry | Funding currencies (JPY, CHF) | Funding shorts |

## 4. FX Risk Management Toolkit

1. **Hedging Policy:** xác định % exposure hedged theo horizon.
2. **Instruments:** forwards, options, NDF (cho VND), cross-currency swaps.
3. **Trigger Rules:**
    - DXY change > 5% trong 1 tháng → review hedges.
    - Local currency volatility (1m implied) > 15% → dùng options thay vì forwards.
4. **Funding Strategy:** diversify sources (local currency debt vs USD debt), theo dõi covenant FX.

## 5. Playbook Examples

### 5.1 USD Crunch (Dollar shortage)
- Symptoms: DXY > 110, FRA-OIS widens, basis swap negative sâu.
- Actions: tăng USD cash buffer, draw credit lines, switch imports to local currency nếu được, dùng cross-currency repo.

### 5.2 USD Weakening (Post-cut cycle)
- Beneficiaries: EM high beta FX, commodities, gold.
- Strategy: tăng allocation EM local debt, hedging USD liabilities bằng swaps.

### 5.3 Currency War / Competitive Devaluation
- Signals: liên tiếp các nước hạ giá nội tệ để hỗ trợ xuất khẩu.
- Actions: diversify revenue by currency, fix margin in sales contracts, consider raising USD prices.
