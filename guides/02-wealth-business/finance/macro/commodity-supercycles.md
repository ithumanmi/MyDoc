# ⛏️ Commodity Supercycle Tracker

> "The cure for high prices is high prices." – Commodity adage

Phân tích các chu kỳ dài của hàng hóa (energy, metals, agriculture) và cách doanh nghiệp/nhà đầu tư chuẩn bị.

---

## 1. Supercycle Anatomy

| Phase | Drivers | Signals | Strategy |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Early Accumulation | Capex thấp, supply shock | Inventory thấp, backwardation | Long producers, mua physical |
| Mid-Boom | Demand mạnh (China urbanization), chính sách kích thích | PMI > 52, freight tăng, CAPEX announcements | Hedge bán tương lai cho producers, trend-followers |
| Late Cycle / Blow-off | Speculation, leverage | Parabolic price, retail participation | Reduce risk, build cash |
| Bust | Supply glut, demand collapse | Contango sâu, default commodity traders | Distressed asset, M&A |

---

## 2. Monitoring Dashboard

- **Capex cycle:** báo cáo capex của majors (Exxon, BHP) → supply reaction lag 3-5 năm.
- **Inventory:** LME stocks, OECD oil inventory.
- **Futures curve:** backwardation vs contango (carry cost).
- **Macro drivers:** USD, China credit impulse, industrial production.

---

## 3. Sector Playbooks

### 3.1 Energy (Oil & Gas)
- theo dõi OPEC policy, US shale rig count, SPR releases.
- breakeven price cho producers (WTI 60-70$).
- hedge demand-side (aviation, logistics) với fuel swaps.

### 3.2 Metals (Copper, Lithium, Nickel)
- critical cho electrification. Check EV adoption, grid capex.
- supply disruptions (Chile protests, Indonesia export ban).
- long-term contracts với miners vs spot exposure.

### 3.3 Agriculture
- climate risk, El Niño/La Niña cycles.
- fertilizer price (urea, potash) ảnh hưởng yield.
- food security policies (export bans) → price spikes.
- Bối cảnh quy mô 2024 (FAO): [Nông nghiệp thế giới & Đông Nam Á](./global-agriculture-sea-overview.md).

## 4. Corporate Strategy Checklist

| Function | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Procurement | Long-term offtake agreements, diversified suppliers |
| Treasury | Commodity-linked financing, trade credit insurance |
| Product | Create surcharge clauses (fuel, metal) |
| ESG | Đầu tư efficiency (energy, water) để giảm exposure |

---

## 5. Investment Ideas by Phase

- **Early:** royalty/streaming companies, infrastructure picks (ports, rail).
- **Mid:** futures trend following, thematic ETFs (URA, PICK).
- **Late:** short over-levered producers, buy protection via options.
- **Bust:** private equity distressed assets, consolidation plays.

---

## 6. Tooling

- Data: Bloomberg commodity indices, Kpler (flows), Refinitiv.
- Modeling: supply-demand balance sheet, scenario analysis.
- Automation: alerts khi backwardation > 5%, rig count change > 10% WoW.
