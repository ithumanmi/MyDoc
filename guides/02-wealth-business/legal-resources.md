## 2. Intellectual Property (IP)

### What Developers Need to Know

**4 Types of IP:**
1. **Copyright** - Protects code, designs, content
2. **Trademark** - Protects brand names, logos
3. **Patent** - Protects inventions (rare for software in most countries)
4. **Trade Secret** - Protects confidential business info

---

### Copyright for Developers

**Key Concepts:**

✅ **Automatic Protection**
- Code you write is automatically copyrighted (no registration needed)
- But registration helps in lawsuits (US)

✅ **Work-for-Hire**
- If employed: Company owns the code
- If freelance: YOU own it unless contract says otherwise
- Always clarify in contracts!

✅ **Fair Use**
- Can use small portions for education, commentary
- But NOT for commercial products without permission

**Resources:**

- **US Copyright Office:** https://copyright.gov (Basics)
- **World Intellectual Property Organization (WIPO):** https://wipo.int
- **Vietnam IP Office:** https://ipvietnam.gov.vn

---

### Trademark Basics

**When you need it:**
- Launching a product/SaaS
- Building a brand
- Want to protect your name/logo

**How to check:**
- **US:** https://uspto.gov/trademarks (TESS search)
- **Vietnam:** https://ipvietnam.gov.vn
- **Domain check:** https://namechk.com (Check name availability everywhere)

**Cost:**
- **US:** $250-350 per class (DIY) | $1,000-2,000 (with lawyer)
- **Vietnam:** ~3-5 triệu VND (DIY) | 10-20 triệu (with lawyer)

**Recommendation:** Use lawyer for trademark (One-time cost, important protection)

## 3. Contracts & Agreements

### Essential Contract Types for Developers

#### 1. **Service Agreement (Freelance Contract)**

**Must-have clauses:**
- Scope of work (What you'll deliver)
- Payment terms (How much, when)
- Timeline & milestones
- IP ownership (Who owns the code?)
- Termination clause (How to end contract)
- Liability limitations

**Resources:**
- **Bonsai:** https://hellobonsai.com (Free templates)
- **Docracy:** https://docracy.com (Open-source legal docs)
- **Our template:** [guides/career/tax-legal-vietnam.md](career/tax-legal-vietnam.md)

---

#### 2. **NDA (Non-Disclosure Agreement)**

**When to use:**
- Client shares confidential info
- You're pitching to investors
- Hiring contractors

**Types:**
- **Unilateral:** One party shares secrets
- **Mutual:** Both parties share secrets

**Templates:**
- **NDA templates:** https://nda-template.com
- **Ironclad:** https://ironcladapp.com/free-nda

---

#### 3. **Terms of Service (ToS) & Privacy Policy**

**Required for:**
- Any website/app collecting user data
- SaaS products
- E-commerce

**Generators (FREE):**
- **Termly:** https://termly.io (Good free tier)
- **TermsFeed:** https://termsfeed.com
- **iubenda:** https://iubenda.com (GDPR-compliant)

**For Vietnam:**
- Must comply with Decree 13/2023 (Personal Data Protection)
- Need both Vietnamese & English versions

---

### Contract Red Flags 🚩

**Never sign if you see:**

❌ **"All IP belongs to client, even ideas before contract"**
- Protect your pre-existing work!

❌ **"Unlimited revisions"**
- Always cap revisions (e.g., "3 rounds included")

❌ **"Payment upon completion"**
- Insist on milestone payments or 50% upfront

❌ **"Non-compete for 5 years globally"**
- Unreasonable non-competes (Max 1-2 years, specific geography)

❌ **"Indemnify client for any lawsuits"**
- Limit liability to contract value

**When in doubt → Consult lawyer** (Worth $200-500 review fee)

## 4. Privacy & Data Protection

### GDPR (EU General Data Protection Regulation)

**Applies if:**
- You have EU users
- You process EU residents' data

**Key Requirements:**
- Get explicit consent for data collection
- Allow users to download/delete their data
- Report breaches within 72 hours
- Appoint DPO (Data Protection Officer) if large scale

**Fines:** Up to €20M or 4% revenue (whichever is higher) 😱

**Resources:**
- **GDPR.eu:** https://gdpr.eu (Plain English guide)
- **GDPR Checklist:** https://gdprchecklist.io
- **Compliance tools:** https://iubenda.com

---

### Vietnam PDPA (Personal Data Protection Act)

**Decree 13/2023 (Effective July 2023)**

**Key points:**
- Similar to GDPR but less strict
- Consent required for personal data
- Must notify users of data breaches
- Cross-border transfer restrictions

**Fines:** Up to 100 triệu VND

**Resources:**
- **Official text:** https://thuvienphapluat.vn (Decree 13/2023)
- **English summary:** Search "Vietnam PDPA 2023 guide"

---

### California Privacy (CCPA/CPRA)

**Applies if:**
- You have California users
- Revenue > $25M OR 50k+ CA users

**Similar to GDPR** but US-focused.

**Resource:** https://oag.ca.gov/privacy/ccpa

## 5. Business Structures

### Choosing the Right Entity

| Entity Type | Liability | Taxes | Cost | Best For |
|-------------|-----------|-------|------|----------|
| **Sole Proprietor** | Unlimited | Personal income tax | Free | Side projects |
| **LLC (US)** | Limited | Pass-through | $100-500 | Indie hackers |
| **C-Corp (US)** | Limited | Double taxation | $500-2000 | VC-backed startups |
| **Hộ kinh doanh (VN)** | Unlimited | Simplified | Free | Solo VN |
| **TNHH (VN)** | Limited | 20% CIT + 5% | ~50 triệu setup | VN company |

**Recommendation:**
- **< $2k/m:** Sole proprietor / Freelancer
- **$2k-10k/m:** LLC (US remote) or Hộ kinh doanh (VN)
- **> $10k/m:** Consider TNHH (VN) or Delaware C-Corp (US)

---

### Setting Up US LLC (For Vietnamese Developers)

**Why:**
- Access Stripe, US payment processors
- Professional image
- Limited liability

**How:**
- Use service: **Northwest Registered Agent** ($225/yr) or **Stripe Atlas** ($500)
- No need to visit US
- Get EIN (Tax ID) online

**Taxes:**
- File US tax return (Form 1040-NR) even if $0 owed
- Vietnam still taxes your worldwide income (Tax treaty applies)

**Resource:**
- **Stripe Atlas:** https://stripe.com/atlas
- **LegalZoom:** https://legalzoom.com

## 6. Vietnam-Specific Legal

### Business Registration

**Hộ kinh doanh (Individual Business):**
- **Process:** 1-2 days at local UBND
- **Cost:** Free
- **Documents:** CMND/CCCD, registration form
- **Tax:** Simplified (0-5% revenue depending on scale)

**TNHH (Limited Liability Company):**
- **Process:** 5-10 days
- **Cost:** ~30-50 triệu VND (legal fees, charter capital)
- **Minimum capital:** No longer required by law (but banks may require ~1 tỷ)
- **Tax:** 20% CIT + 5% dividend tax

---

### Labor Law (If Hiring Employees)

**Key Requirements:**
- **Labor Contract:** Must be in writing
- **Social Insurance:** Mandatory (BHXH, BHYT, BHTN)
- **Minimum Wage:** Regional (2026: ~4.7-5.2 triệu/month)
- **Probation:** Max 60 days (technical roles)
- **Severance:** 0.5 month per year worked

**Resource:**
- **Labor Code 2019:** https://thuvienphapluat.vn
- **English version:** Search "Vietnam Labor Code 2019 English"

---

### E-commerce Regulations

**Decree 52/2013 (E-commerce):**
- Register website with Ministry of Industry & Trade
- Required for: Online stores, marketplaces
- **Not required for:** SaaS, B2B software, blogs

**How to register:**
- Online: https://online.gov.vn
- Cost: Free
- Time: ~5 days

---

### Tax Compliance

**See detailed guide:** [guides/career/tax-legal-vietnam.md](career/tax-legal-vietnam.md)

**Quick summary:**
- **Freelance income:** Personal income tax (PIT) - Progressive 5-35%

## 7. Software Licensing

### Open Source Licenses (Choosing the Right One)

**Permissive (Can use in commercial products):**

1. **MIT License** ⭐ Most Popular
   - Do whatever you want
   - Just keep copyright notice
   - Used by: React, Vue, Rails

2. **Apache 2.0**
   - Similar to MIT
   - + Patent protection
   - Used by: Kubernetes, Android

3. **BSD (2-clause/3-clause)**
   - Very permissive
   - Used by: FreeBSD, Django

**Copyleft (Must open-source derivative works):**

1. **GPL (v2/v3)**
   - Derivative work must be GPL
   - Used by: Linux, WordPress

2. **AGPL**
   - GPL + Network use = distribution
   - Used by: MongoDB (old), Mastodon

3. **LGPL**
   - GPL but for libraries
   - Can link without GPL-ing your code

**How to choose:**

```
Want max adoption? → MIT
Want patent protection? → Apache 2.0
Want derivatives to stay open? → GPL
Building SaaS? → AGPL (controversial, consider MIT + paid tier)
```

**Resources:**
- **ChooseALicense.com:** https://choosealicense.com (GitHub's tool)
- **TLDRLegal:** https://tldrlegal.com (Plain English explanations)
- **SPDX License List:** https://spdx.org/licenses

---

### Commercial Licensing

**If selling software:**

1. **End-User License Agreement (EULA)**
   - What users can/can't do
   - Liability limitations

2. **Subscription Agreement**
   - SaaS terms
   - Service levels (SLA)
   - Cancellation policy

**Templates:**

## 8. When to Hire a Lawyer

### DIY vs Lawyer Decision Tree

**You can DIY:**
✅ Simple service contracts (<$5k projects)
✅ Standard NDA
✅ Terms of Service for small app (use generator)
✅ Choosing open-source license
✅ Trademark search (initial)

**Hire a lawyer:**
⚠️ Contracts > $10k value
⚠️ Partnership agreements (Founder splits)
⚠️ Fundraising (Investors, SAFE notes)
⚠️ Trademark filing (Final step)
⚠️ Patents (Always use patent lawyer)
⚠️ Lawsuits / Disputes
⚠️ Complex employment situations
⚠️ M&A (Selling your company)

---

### Finding & Working with Lawyers

**How to find:**

**Vietnam:**
- **LawNet:** https://lawnet.vn
- **Referral:** Ask in startup communities (Facebook: "Vietnam Startup Network")
- **Law firms:** Tilleke & Gibbins, YKVN, Venture Legal

**Global (Online):**
- **UpCounsel:** https://upcounsel.com (Vetted lawyers, $100-500/hr)
- **Rocket Lawyer:** https://rocketlawyer.com (Subscription + lawyer access)
- **Atrium:** https://atrium.co (Startup-focused)

**Cost expectations:**
- **Vietnam:** 2-10 triệu VND/hour (depending on firm)
- **US:** $200-600/hour
- **Contract review:** $500-1,500 flat fee
- **Company formation:** $2,000-5,000

**Tips:**
- Get fixed-price quotes when possible
- Ask for junior lawyer on routine tasks (Lower rate)
- Many offer free 15-min consultation

## ⚠️ Important Reminders

### Golden Rules

1. **"If in doubt, ask a lawyer"**
   - $500 consultation >> $50k lawsuit

2. **"Get it in writing"**
   - Verbal agreements = Hard to enforce
   - Always have contracts

3. **"Read before you sign"**
   - Every. Single. Word.
   - If you don't understand, ask

4. **"Protect your IP"**
   - Copyright notices on code
   - Trademarks for brands
   - NDAs before sharing ideas

5. **"Stay compliant"**
   - Privacy laws (GDPR, PDPA)
   - Tax obligations
   - Business registrations
