# Chapter 6: Follow radars & weekly brief

## Core Idea
Follow = time-boxed antennas (play-culture + industry) producing tagged bullets — not a second full-time job or a tech-domain dump.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Play-Culture Radar**: Discourse/taste/community norms antenna
  - When to use: Stay aware without feed addiction
  - How: 1–2 sources/bucket; filter; ≤3 weekly bullets; mostly `play-only`
- **Industry Radar**: Charts/policy/publisher/hiring antenna
  - When to use: Earn/Make awareness
  - How: Headlines + escalate only with clear action; else none
- **Weekly Games Brief**: Single template merging both ≤45′
  - When to use: End of Follow block
  - How: ≤3 culture + ≤3 industry + tags + sources + minutes

## Key Concepts
- **Tags**: `play-only` · `earn-signal` · `make-research`
- **Escalate**: One concrete next step into Earn/Make hub — not “read 2h more”
- **Radar vs deep research**: Brief ≠ TAM/SAM market deep-dive
- **Source buckets**: Curate; cut unused

## Mental Models
- Prefer remembering one insight/week over covering the industry.
- Empty earn/make tags still = valid brief.

## Anti-patterns
- **Archive drama / outrage cycles**: Skip unless personally relevant
- **Force every bullet into Earn**: Most signals are play-only
- **Infinite trade-press scroll**: Schedule research block separately if pitching

## Worked Example
Brief week:
- Culture: genre discourse surge — not adding to Next (`play-only`)
- Industry: store discovery change — if shipping there, 30′ primary doc + wishlist CTA (`earn-signal`)
Time: 35′.

## Key Takeaways
1. Split culture vs industry radars mentally.
2. Tags + time-box make Follow useful.
3. Deep market work lives in wealth market-research / career hubs.
