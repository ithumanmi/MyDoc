# 🏦 Real World Assets

## 1. Tokenization Models

- **On-chain SPV:** SPV issues tokens backed by bonds/treasuries.
- **Bank partnerships:** integrate with banks for custody.
- **Credit marketplaces:** borrower pools (Maple, Goldfinch).

## 2. Protocol Examples

- **Ondo Finance:** US Treasuries (OUSG), tokenized money market.
- **Centrifuge:** tinlake pools with real-world borrowers.
- **Maple:** institutional lending pools.

## 3. Cash Flow

- Deposit stablecoins → purchase US treasuries → yield distributed as tokens.
- Redemption via on/off ramp partners.

## 4. Risks

- Legal/regulatory compliance.
- Counterparty risk of SPV/custodian.
- Liquidity lockups, redemption delays.

## 5. Checklist

- [ ] KYC/AML requirements understood.
- [ ] Asset custody arrangement audited.
- [ ] Redemption terms (lockup, fees).
- [ ] Jurisdiction + regulatory disclosures.
