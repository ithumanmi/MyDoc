# 📊 Crypto Portfolio Management

## 1. Risk Frameworks

- **Volatility buckets:** BTC/ETH core, L1/L2, DeFi, Long-tail bets.
- **Max drawdown tolerance:** định nghĩa % loss per asset và toàn portfolio.
- **Scenario planning:** Macro (Fed, CPI), crypto native (ETF flows, regulatory news).

## 2. Position Sizing

- Kelly fraction (fractional Kelly để tránh overbet).
- Risk per trade (1-2% rule) khi quản trị directional bets.
- Use conviction/scorecard để assign weight.

## 3. Rebalancing

- Calendar-based (monthly/quarterly) vs trigger-based (deviation > target %).
- Auto-rebalance via DeFi vaults (Index Coop, Enzyme) hoặc manual spreadsheets.

## 4. Hedging & Cash Management

- Stablecoin ladder (USDC/USDT/T-bill tokens) + on/off-ramp.
- Options/futures hedging (Deribit, CME), delta-hedged strategies.
- Real-yield opportunities (Treasury bills tokenized, Maker DSR, Ethena sUSDe).

## 5. Reporting

- Track NAV, IRR, Sharpe, Sortino.
- Use tools: CoinTracking, Rotki, LemFi dashboards, Notion templates.

## 6. Checklist

- [ ] Define IPS (Investment Policy Statement) với target allocations và risk limits.
- [ ] Thiết lập log trade + thesis để học từ kết quả.
- [ ] Stress test liquidity (exit multiple positions cùng lúc?).
