# 🎁 Airdrop Strategies

## 1. Research Framework

- **Identify target ecosystems:** LayerZero, zkSync, EigenLayer, fuel chain.
- **Check official hints:** Blog posts, governance proposals, quests.
- **Map scoring dimensions:** Volume, duration, unique interactions, referral, governance.

## 2. Sybil Resistance Patterns

- **Driving factors:** Wallet age, bridging path, CEX funding, onchain behavior similarity.
- **Best practices:** Use limited set of wallets, unique behavioral patterns, avoid scripted identical flows.
- **Reputation systems:** Galxe, Gitcoin Passport, Civic Pass.

## 3. Execution Playbook

1. Lập spreadsheet tracking mỗi protocol, yêu cầu, chi phí gas.
2. Thiết kế flow tương tác nhiều sản phẩm (swap, LP, vote, deploy contract).
3. Thiết lập reminders (monthly/weekly) để duy trì hoạt động.
4. Snapshot cuối kỳ: consolidate wallet proofs, ENS, social links.

## 4. Monitoring & Signals

- Theo dõi Discord/Twitter dev hints, onchain contract upgrade (Etherscan watchlist).
- Dune dashboards tracking unique addresses, bridging volume.
- Setup alerts khi funding rounds/partnership news drop (Messari, The Block).

## 5. Checklist

- [ ] Ưu tiên chất lượng tương tác thay vì spam hàng trăm ví.
- [ ] Đánh giá ROI vs gas/time.
- [ ] Lưu trữ bằng chứng (tx hash, screenshots) để dispute nếu bị flag.
