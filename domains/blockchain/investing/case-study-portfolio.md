# 📘 Case Study: Multi-bucket Crypto Portfolio

## 1. Persona & Constraints

- **Investor:** Vietnam-based builder, retail size $100k, time horizon 24 months.
- **Objectives:** Grow capital 2-3x với risk kiểm soát (max drawdown 35%), vẫn có thanh khoản cho side projects.
- **Constraints:** Weekly time 5-6h, KYC hạn chế (ưu tiên on-chain), thuế chưa rõ ràng.

## 2. Allocation Blueprint

| Bucket | Target % | Assets | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Core Beta | 45% | BTC (25%), ETH (20%) | Macro exposure, staking yield (staking ETH, BTC via GBTC/ETF proxy). |
| Yield & Infra | 25% | LRT/restaking (rsETH), RWA (ONDO), L2 infra (OP token) | Capture structural yield + infra growth. |
| Venture / Narrative | 20% | AI x crypto (Ritual), SocialFi (Farcaster tokens), Modular DA (Celestia) | Higher risk, sized smaller, strict theses. |
| Cash / Stable Dry Powder | 10% | USDC ladder (Treasury bills on Ondo/Mountain) | Optionality for drawdowns + tax reserve. |

## 4. Quarterly Review Template

1. **Performance Attribution:** NAV vs BTC/ETH benchmark, contribution per bucket.
2. **Narrative Check:** AI/Restaking still hot? Replace laggards.
3. **Risk Metrics:** Max DD, % bucket deviation, VaR approximations.
4. **Action Items:** Rebalance to targets, rotate to new theses, update watchlist.

## 5. Toolkit

- **Tracking:** Notion/Rowy sheet, CoinTracking, Rotki custom tags.
- **Rebalancing:** DeFi index vaults (Index Coop), manual DEX swap, CEX minimal (Binance → self-custody).
- **Alerts:** TradingView, Nansen portfolio alerts, Token Unlock calendar.

## 6. Lessons Learned

- Không chase narrative nếu không có sizing plan; ghi rõ thesis + invalidate signal.
- Giữ stable ladder để không bán đáy khi thị trường điều chỉnh.
- Document mọi trade để review > học từ sai lầm.
