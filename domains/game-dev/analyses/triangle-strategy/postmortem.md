## 6. Lessons (portable)

1. If politics is USP → **surface a ritual** (Scales) and at least soft meter feedback.  
2. If comps are strong → **lead trailer with axes**, not art-style alone.  
3. If branch cost is high → **vertical slice Scales+battle** before full graph.
