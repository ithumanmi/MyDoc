## 1. Primary question

**Must answer:** After one Scales scene + one height/weather battle, can players state the three pillars without prompting?

**Success criteria:** ≥7/10 articulate “politics vote” + “position matters”; less than X rage-quits mid dialogue `(TBD thresholds)`.

## 2. Protocol (60–90 min)

| Task | Timebox | Observe for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Blind start through FTUE battle | 25m | Forecast/height comprehension |
| Play through first Scales (or sliced equivalent) | 20m | Agency / frustration when outvoted |
| One mid difficulty board | 25m | Quietuses spend; flank use |
| Debrief | 15m | Pillar recall; “FE clone?” yes/no |
