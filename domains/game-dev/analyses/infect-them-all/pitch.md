## 0. Hook

Bạn là nguồn nhiễm — **cắn để biến người thành lính**, hoặc **ăn để lấy máu** — trong khi máu tự chảy, dưới áp lực vũ khí và giai người ngày càng dày. `(observed)`

## 1. Player & occasion

- Target: mobile arcade, short sessions, fantasy zombie/vampire power trip `(assumed)`
- Session: 2–5 phút/stage; upgrade between `(observed)`

## 2. Core loop

`Move → Contact bite → Choose Infect vs Eat → Grow/heal horde → Clear stage → Soft-currency upgrades → Harder stage`

## 3. USP

- Comps: Infectonator-like infection, classic zombie runners `(genre pattern)`
- Flip: **you are the plague**, not the survivor; minion count is the skill expression `(observed)`

## 4. Pillars

| # | Pillar | Signal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Bite ≠ Eat | Players argue which button/timing `(observed)` |
| 2 | Tipping spectacle | End of stage “I barely move” `(observed)` |
| 3 | Readable density | Can still pick next bite under huge unit counts `(observed)` / sequel marketing |

Anti-goals: cover shooter fantasy; gacha roster as core `(assumed)` for original product era

## 5–8. Scope / model / risks / signals

- Scope: campaign stages + survival/blitz/tower variants `(observed)`
- Model: upfront / light IAP era mobile `(observed)` — not modern GaaS primary
- Risks: balance cliff (easy→wall); thumb density clutter
- Success: “one more stage” after a failed overeat
