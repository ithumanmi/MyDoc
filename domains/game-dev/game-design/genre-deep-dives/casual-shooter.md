# 🔫 Casual Shooter Deep Dive

## Pillars
- Accessible control (auto-fire, generous aim assist).
- Short session (3-5 phút) với progression rõ.
- Sáng tạo hero/weapon archetype.

## Core Loop
1. Queue/trận đấu nhanh (PvE horde hoặc PvP nhỏ).
2. Combat → kiếm XP, currency.
3. Upgrade weapon/hero, cosmetic.

## Control & UX
- Aim assist zones, bullet magnetism.
- Auto-fire khi crosshair chạm mục tiêu.
- Snap-to-cover, simplified traversal.

## Meta
- Hero roster với ability tree.
- Weapon rarity + mod slot.
- Battle pass + timed challenges.

## KPI
- Match completion rate >85%.
- Session length 3-5 phút, D1 retention 35-45%.
- Monetization: cosmetic bundle, BP, gacha weapon skin.

## Monetization Ethics
- Không pay-to-win: cosmetic hoặc stat minor.
- Transparent drop rate.

## Risks
- Skill gap cao, shooter gốc -> churn.
- Netcode/latency.
- Content drought (map/hero).

## ✅ Apply it
- [ ] Tune aim assist & auto-fire cho ngón tay cái.
- [ ] Thiết kế hero archetype rõ (Tank/DPS/Support).
- [ ] Lên cadence map/weapon update.
- [ ] Telemetry: hit rate, kill/death, quit mid-match.
