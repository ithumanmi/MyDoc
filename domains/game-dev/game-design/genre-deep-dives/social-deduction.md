# 🕵️ Social Deduction Deep Dive

## Pillars
- Thông tin bất cân xứng (hidden role).
- Social interaction → suspicion, persuasion.
- Session ngắn 5-10 phút.

## Core Loop
1. Briefing role (Crewmate/Impostor).
2. Gameplay phase (task/kill/sabotage).
3. Discussion/vote.

## Communication UX
- Voice chat in-game hoặc push-to-talk.
- Quick chat wheel + emoji cho mobile/safe chat.
- Replay log cho điều tra.

## Anti-Toxicity & Safety
- Moderation tool: report, mute, ban.
- Pre-made phrase cho trẻ em.
- AFK/leaver penalty.

## Meta & Progression
- Cosmetic progression (suit, pet).
- Ranked mode (hidden MMR) cẩn trọng.
- Event theme (Halloween sabotage).

## KPI
- Session completion >80%.
- Average players/match 8-10.
- Retention phụ thuộc social loop: track party play %.

## Risks
- Griefing, collusion.
- Voice toxicity.
- Lack of content variation.

## ✅ Apply it
- [ ] Thiết kế UX discussion rõ (timer, voting feedback).
- [ ] Tích hợp moderation pipeline.
- [ ] Meta cosmetic + seasonal event.
- [ ] Telemetry: vote accuracy, leave rate.
