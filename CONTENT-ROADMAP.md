## 🛠️ QUALITY STANDARDS & TEMPLATES

### **Required Sections for Each Guide:**

```markdown
1. TL;DR (1-2 sentences)
2. Table of Contents
3. "Why This Matters" section
4. 7-10 main content sections
5. Real-world examples
6. Action Plan (This Week/Month/Quarter)
7. Further Reading
8. Related Content (cross-references)
9. Key Takeaways
```

### **Formatting Guidelines:**

- **Headings:** Use emoji + clear titles (e.g., "🎯 Why This Matters")
- **Code blocks:** For frameworks, checklists, decision trees
- **Tables:** For comparisons, metrics, data
- **Diagrams:** ASCII art for simple visualizations
- **Examples:** Real companies, real scenarios (Vietnam + US)
- **Cross-links:** Markdown links to related guides

### **Word Count Targets:**

- **Comprehensive Guide:** 5,000-7,000 words
- **Deep Dive:** 7,000-10,000 words
- **Quick Reference:** 2,000-3,000 words
- **Framework/Template:** 1,000-2,000 words
